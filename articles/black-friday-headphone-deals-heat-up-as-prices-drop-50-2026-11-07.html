Black Friday Headphone Deals Heat Up as Prices Drop 50%

2026-11-07 · 3 min read
Early Black Friday offers slash prices on top headphone brands, giving tech shoppers a chance to snag premium audio gear at half price.

Black Friday is traditionally a time for deep discounts on electronics, but this year’s early‑bird deals are turning the tide for audio enthusiasts. A curated list of headphone and earbud offers from major brands such as Apple, Sony, and Bose is cutting prices by up to 50%, according to a recent roundup of the best early Black Friday deals.

Apple’s AirPods Pro Get a 50% Price Cut

Apple’s flagship AirPods Pro, known for their active noise cancellation and spatial audio, are now available for $149.99—half the usual $299.99 price tag. The discount is part of a broader strategy to attract budget‑conscious consumers ahead of the holiday rush.

Sony’s WH‑1000XM5 Slashes 30% Off

Sony’s premium over‑the‑ear headphones, the WH‑1000XM5, are being offered at $199.99, down from $299.99. The model retains its industry‑leading noise‑cancellation technology and adaptive sound control, making it a compelling choice for commuters and travelers.

Bose QuietComfort 45 Offers 25% Savings

Bose’s QuietComfort 45 headphones are now priced at $224.99, a 25% reduction from the $299.99 retail price. The QuietComfort line continues to deliver the brand’s signature comfort and sound quality, and the discount makes it more accessible for a wider audience.

Other Notable Discounts

  • Jabra Elite 85h: $149.99 (30% off)
  • Sennheiser Momentum 4: $199.99 (20% off)
  • Beats Studio3: $179.99 (25% off)

Why the Early Deals Matter

Retailers are offering these discounts ahead of the traditional Black Friday window to capture early shoppers and reduce inventory before the holiday season peaks. For consumers, the early window provides a chance to secure high‑end audio gear without the crowds and price volatility that often accompany the main Black Friday event.

How to Take Advantage

  1. Check the official brand websites for the most accurate pricing and availability.
  2. Sign up for newsletters or alerts to receive real‑time updates on flash sales.
  3. Compare specifications and user reviews to ensure the chosen model meets your audio needs.
  4. Consider extended warranties or protection plans if you plan to use the headphones extensively.

With prices slashed by up to 50%, this early Black Friday wave offers a rare opportunity to upgrade audio gear without breaking the bank. Whether you’re a music lover, gamer, or frequent traveler, the current deals make premium headphones more attainable than ever.
