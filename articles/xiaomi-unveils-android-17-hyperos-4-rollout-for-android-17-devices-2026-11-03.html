Xiaomi Unveils Android 17 HyperOS 4 Rollout for Android 17 Devices

2026-11-03 · 3 min read
Xiaomi, Redmi and Poco phones receive Android 17 HyperOS 4 update today, bringing new features and performance improvements.

Today, Xiaomi confirmed that several of its Android 17‑based devices will receive the HyperOS 4 update. The rollout begins immediately for a range of smartphones across the Xiaomi, Redmi and Poco brands, according to a statement released by the company on its official channels.

Devices Included in the Update

The update will be available for the following models:

  • Xiaomi Mi 14 Ultra
  • Redmi Note 13 Pro
  • Poco X5 Pro
  • Redmi K60
  • Poco F5

Key Features of HyperOS 4

HyperOS 4 builds on the foundation of Android 17, adding a suite of enhancements that focus on user experience, security and performance. Highlights include:

  • Improved battery management with adaptive power saving modes.
  • Enhanced privacy controls that allow users to manage app permissions more granularly.
  • Optimized UI elements for smoother navigation and reduced latency.
  • New gaming mode that prioritizes resources for high‑performance titles.

Developer and Ecosystem Support

Xiaomi has announced that the HyperOS 4 SDK will be available to third‑party developers, enabling them to create applications that leverage the new system APIs. The company also plans to expand its partner program to include more hardware manufacturers and software developers.

Impact on the Android Ecosystem

By rolling out HyperOS 4 across a broad portfolio of devices, Xiaomi is reinforcing its position as a major contributor to the Android ecosystem. The update demonstrates the company’s commitment to delivering timely software support, which is a key factor for consumers when choosing a smartphone brand.

Next Steps for Users

Users of the affected devices can check for the update by navigating to Settings > System > Software update. The update will be delivered over the air and is expected to take approximately 30 minutes to complete, depending on the device and network conditions.

Conclusion

The HyperOS 4 rollout marks another milestone in Xiaomi’s strategy to provide a cohesive software experience across its product lines. With a focus on performance, privacy and developer support, the update is poised to enhance the daily experience for millions of Android users worldwide.
