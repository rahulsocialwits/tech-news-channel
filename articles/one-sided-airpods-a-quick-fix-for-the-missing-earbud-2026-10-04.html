One‑Sided AirPods? A Quick Fix for the Missing Earbud

2026-10-04 · 3 min read
If only one of your AirPods stops working, a simple troubleshooting step can restore full audio without a costly repair.

Apple’s AirPods have become a staple of on‑the‑go listening, but users sometimes encounter a frustrating issue: one earbud stops working while the other remains fine. According to Engadget, the problem can often be traced to a simple connectivity hiccup rather than a hardware failure.

What to Check First

Engadget recommends starting with the most basic troubleshooting steps. First, ensure that the AirPods are fully charged and that the charging case is connected to power. Next, try resetting the pair by placing both earbuds in the case, closing the lid, waiting 30 seconds, then reopening the lid and pressing the setup button on the back of the case until the status light flashes amber.

Re‑pairing the Pairing

Once reset, re‑pair the AirPods to your device. Open the Bluetooth settings, select the AirPods from the list, and follow the on‑screen prompts. This often resolves the one‑sided audio problem by re‑establishing a clean connection between the earbuds and the phone.

When to Seek Professional Help

If the issue persists after resetting and re‑pairing, it may indicate a deeper hardware fault. In that case, contacting Apple Support or visiting an Apple Store is the recommended next step. Engadget notes that most users who follow the initial troubleshooting steps find a quick fix, saving time and money.
