Google Expands Gemini 4 Argon to Antigravity for AI Pro Subscribers

2026-10-16 · 3 min read
Google rolls out Gemini 4 Argon support to its Antigravity coding platform, enhancing AI-powered code generation for Pro users.

Google has announced that its latest Gemini 4 Argon model is now available on Antigravity, the company’s AI‑powered coding assistant for its AI Pro subscription tier. The rollout brings the newest generation of Gemini’s language model to developers who rely on Antigravity for rapid code generation and debugging.

What’s New with Gemini 4 Argon?

Gemini 4 Argon builds on the capabilities of earlier Gemini releases, offering improved contextual understanding and more accurate code suggestions. By integrating this model into Antigravity, Google aims to provide a smoother, more reliable coding experience for professional users.

Impact for AI Pro Subscribers

Subscribers to AI Pro will now benefit from faster response times and higher quality code completions. The update is part of Google’s broader strategy to keep its AI tools competitive in the fast‑moving developer ecosystem.

How to Access the Update

To start using Gemini 4 Argon on Antigravity, AI Pro users simply need to log in to their account and select the new model from the model picker. The feature is available immediately for all existing subscribers.
