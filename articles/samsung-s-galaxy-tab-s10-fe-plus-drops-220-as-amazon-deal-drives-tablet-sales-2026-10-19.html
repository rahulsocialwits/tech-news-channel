Samsung’s Galaxy Tab S10 FE Plus Drops $220 as Amazon Deal Drives Tablet Sales

2026-10-19 · 3 min read
Samsung’s Galaxy Tab S10 FE Plus is now available for $599.99 on Amazon, a $220 discount from its original price, featuring a 13.1‑inch display, S Pen support and 256GB storage.

Samsung’s Galaxy Tab S10 FE Plus has just landed on Amazon with a hefty discount that could make it a top choice for budget‑conscious tablet buyers. The 13.1‑inch Android tablet is now listed at $599.99, down from its original price of $819.99, a savings of $220.

Key Specs That Keep the Tab Competitive

The device comes with a large 13.1‑inch display that offers a 120‑Hz refresh rate, making scrolling and gaming smoother. It also supports the S Pen, which is included in the box, allowing users to take notes, draw or navigate the tablet with precision. Internally, the Tab S10 FE Plus houses a Qualcomm Snapdragon 870 processor, 6GB of RAM and 256GB of internal storage, which can be expanded via microSD.

Why the Discount Matters

Amazon’s deal arrives at a time when tablet sales are seeing a resurgence, as more people look for portable computing solutions that can handle both work and entertainment. The $220 price cut brings the Tab S10 FE Plus into a price range that competes directly with other mid‑tier tablets, such as the Microsoft Surface Go 3 and the Lenovo Tab P11 Pro.

What Buyers Should Know

While the discount is significant, buyers should note that the Tab S10 FE Plus does not include a stylus holder or a keyboard dock in the bundle. However, the tablet’s 10‑hour battery life and 4G LTE connectivity options make it a versatile choice for on‑the‑go productivity.

How to Get the Deal

To take advantage of the offer, shoppers can visit the Amazon listing for the Galaxy Tab S10 FE Plus. The discount is available for a limited time, so interested buyers are encouraged to act quickly before the price reverts to its original level.
