Nvidia Quietly Pulls RTX 5080 After RTX 5090 Removal, Prices Surge

2026-10-31 · 3 min read
Nvidia removes its RTX 5080 listing as the RTX 5090 is pulled, sending retail prices to double MSRP.

Nvidia has quietly taken its RTX 5080 graphics card off the market after the company removed the RTX 5090 from its online store. The move comes as the RTX 5090 was pulled earlier, and the RTX 5080 listing was subsequently removed from the Nvidia marketplace. Retail prices for the RTX 5080 have climbed to more than twice the manufacturer’s suggested retail price, reflecting the high demand and limited supply of the new generation of GPUs.

What Happened?

First the RTX 5090 was taken off the Nvidia store, and now the RTX 5080 is no longer listed. The removal of both cards has left many consumers scrambling for the next available option. The price surge to 2.2 times MSRP on the retail market indicates that the cards are in high demand and that the supply chain is strained.

Impact on Buyers

Gamers and content creators who were planning to upgrade to the RTX 5080 now face a difficult decision. With the card no longer available from Nvidia’s official channels, buyers must look to secondary markets where prices have risen sharply. The situation underscores the volatility in the GPU market and the challenges of meeting consumer demand for high-performance hardware.
