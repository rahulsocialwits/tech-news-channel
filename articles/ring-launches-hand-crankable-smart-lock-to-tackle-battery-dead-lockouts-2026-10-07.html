Ring Launches Hand‑Crankable Smart Lock to Tackle Battery‑Dead Lockouts

2026-10-07 · 3 min read
Amazon‑owned Ring introduces a smart lock that can be manually turned when its battery dies, addressing a common homeowner frustration.

Amazon‑owned Ring has entered the smart lock market with a device that tackles a familiar problem: being locked out when the battery dies. The new lock, announced on October 7, 2026, can be hand‑cranked to unlock the door, giving homeowners a reliable backup when the battery runs low.

Key Features

  • Manual Override – A built‑in crank allows the lock to be opened without power.
  • Battery‑Powered – Uses a standard battery pack that can be replaced easily.
  • Smart Connectivity – Connects to Ring’s existing ecosystem for remote access and notifications.

Why It Matters

Smart locks have become a staple in modern homes, but battery failure can leave users stranded. Ring’s manual crank offers a simple, low‑cost solution that eliminates the need for spare batteries or professional assistance.

Market Impact

With the smart lock market expected to grow, Ring’s hybrid approach may appeal to consumers who value both convenience and reliability. The company’s move also signals a broader trend of integrating fail‑safe mechanisms into connected devices.
