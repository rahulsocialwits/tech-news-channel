8BitDo Ultimate 2 Gamepad Hits Record Low Price on Amazon

2026-11-13 · 3 min read
The 8BitDo Ultimate 2 wireless controller for PC and Switch 2 is now available for $51.12, a 27% discount from its usual price.

Gamers and PC enthusiasts have a new reason to celebrate as the 8BitDo Ultimate 2 wireless controller drops to a record low price on Amazon. The fully customizable gamepad, which supports both PC and Nintendo Switch 2, is now available for $51.12, a 27% discount from its previous listing.

Features that keep the price competitive

The Ultimate 2 offers a range of features that appeal to both casual and serious players. It includes TMR joysticks, dual triggers, and a set of remappable buttons that can be configured through the 8BitDo software. The controller’s ergonomic design and wireless connectivity make it a versatile choice for a variety of gaming setups.

Why the price cut matters

With the gaming market saturated by high‑end peripherals, a price reduction of this magnitude can make the Ultimate 2 a more attractive option for budget‑conscious consumers. The discount also positions 8BitDo as a competitive alternative to more expensive controllers from larger brands.

Where to buy

Customers can purchase the discounted controller directly from Amazon’s website. The sale is currently listed as a limited‑time offer, so interested buyers are encouraged to act quickly to secure the best price.
