Raise Financial Services Unveils AI‑Powered TradingCharts Platform for Indian Traders

2026-11-27 · 3 min read
Raise Financial Services launches TradingCharts, an AI‑driven charting platform designed to give Indian traders real‑time data, analysis and trade execution in one interface.

Raise Financial Services, a Mumbai‑based fintech firm, has announced the launch of TradingCharts, a new charting platform built from the ground up to serve Indian traders and investors. The platform promises to combine visual data feeds, advanced indicators, strategy back‑testing and direct trade execution in a single, user‑friendly interface.

What TradingCharts Offers

According to the company’s announcement, TradingCharts brings together three core capabilities:

  • Visual Data Feeds – Real‑time price charts for equities, indices, commodities and currencies.
  • Information & Analysis – A library of technical indicators and custom strategies that can be applied to any chart.
  • Trade Execution – Direct connectivity to brokerage accounts, allowing users to place orders without leaving the platform.

AI at the Core

The platform’s name reflects its focus on artificial intelligence. While the announcement does not detail the specific algorithms, it highlights that TradingCharts uses AI to generate insights and suggest trading signals based on historical patterns. The system is designed to adapt to market conditions, offering users a dynamic tool that evolves with the data.

Target Audience and Market Position

Raise Financial Services positions TradingCharts as a solution for both novice and experienced traders in India. By integrating data, analysis and execution, the platform aims to reduce the friction that often deters retail investors from engaging in active trading. The company also emphasizes the platform’s scalability, suggesting it can support a growing user base as the Indian market continues to expand.

Competitive Landscape

India’s fintech ecosystem has seen a surge in trading platforms, from established players like Zerodha and Upstox to newer entrants offering AI‑enhanced tools. TradingCharts seeks to differentiate itself by offering a comprehensive suite that eliminates the need to switch between multiple apps for charting, analysis and order placement.

Future Plans

Raise Financial Services has indicated that TradingCharts will receive continuous updates, including new indicators, enhanced AI models and expanded brokerage integrations. The firm also plans to introduce educational resources to help users understand how to leverage AI insights effectively.

Conclusion

With the launch of TradingCharts, Raise Financial Services adds a robust, AI‑driven tool to India’s growing fintech landscape. By unifying data, analysis and execution, the platform could streamline the trading experience for millions of investors looking to navigate the country’s dynamic markets.
