SanDisk Unveils Ultra‑Fast 2TB Portable SSD at Record Low Price

2026-11-28 · 3 min read
SanDisk’s new 2TB portable SSD, delivering up to 1,000 MB/s read speeds, drops to $269.99 – its lowest price since launch.

SanDisk has announced a new 2TB portable SSD that promises up to 1,000 MB/s read speeds. The drive, part of SanDisk’s latest portable line, is now available for $269.99, a 33% discount from its launch price and the lowest it has been since it first hit the market.

Key Features

  • 2TB storage capacity in a compact, portable form factor
  • Read speeds up to 1,000 MB/s, making it suitable for high‑performance tasks such as video editing and gaming
  • USB‑C connectivity for quick, plug‑and‑play use on modern laptops and desktops

Market Impact

The price cut positions SanDisk competitively against other high‑speed portable SSDs, offering consumers a budget‑friendly option without compromising on performance. The move comes as the broader market sees a trend toward more affordable, high‑capacity storage solutions.
