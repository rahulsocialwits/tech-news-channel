Pangram Expands AI Detection to Video and Images

2026-11-05 · 3 min read
Pangram broadens its AI content detection suite to cover visual media, adding new tools for video and image analysis.

Pangram, the company behind the popular AI‑text detection tool, has announced a significant expansion of its product line. The new release adds detection capabilities for both video and image content, allowing users to flag synthetic media across a broader range of formats.

Why the Expansion Matters

With the rapid rise of deepfakes and AI‑generated imagery, the demand for reliable detection tools has grown. Pangram’s move to cover visual media addresses a gap that many organizations have identified in their content‑verification workflows.

How the New Tools Work

According to the company, the updated system uses a combination of machine‑learning models trained on large datasets of authentic and synthetic media. The platform can analyze frame‑by‑frame video or individual images, providing confidence scores that help users assess the likelihood of manipulation.

Implications for Content Creators and Platforms

Social media sites, news outlets, and content creators can integrate Pangram’s API to automatically screen uploads for AI‑generated material. This could reduce the spread of misinformation and protect brand integrity.

Future Outlook

Pangram plans to continue refining its models and expand support for additional media types. The company’s CEO emphasized the importance of staying ahead of emerging AI techniques that could produce more convincing synthetic content.
