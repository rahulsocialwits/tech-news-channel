Apple Opens First Hyderabad Store Ahead of December 10 Launch

2026-11-26 · 3 min read
Apple announces its inaugural retail location in Hyderabad, opening on December 10 at 5 PM in Kukatpally.

Apple has confirmed that its first retail store in India will open in Hyderabad on December 10. The new outlet, located in the Kukatpally area, will begin operations at 5 PM and will offer the full range of Apple products and services.

What to Expect

Customers can look forward to the same in‑store experience that Apple has built worldwide, including personalized setup assistance, workshops, and access to the Genius Bar for technical support. The Hyderabad store will also host product launches and special events as part of Apple’s expanding presence in the Indian market.

Significance for the Indian Market

Apple’s move comes as the company continues to grow its footprint in India, where demand for premium smartphones and accessories has surged. The new store is part of a broader strategy to strengthen retail channels and provide a dedicated space for Indian consumers to engage with Apple’s ecosystem.
