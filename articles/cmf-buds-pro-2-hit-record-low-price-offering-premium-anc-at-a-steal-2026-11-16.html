CMF Buds Pro 2 Hit Record Low Price, Offering Premium ANC at a Steal

2026-11-16 · 3 min read
CMF’s ANC earbuds, the Buds Pro 2, drop 29% to $14 off on Amazon, delivering LDAC and a smart dial case for budget‑savvy listeners.

For the first time in its history, CMF has slashed the price of its flagship Buds Pro 2 earbuds by almost a third. The 29% discount, announced on Amazon, brings the pair down to a new record low of $14 off the original price. The move comes as the company looks to broaden its appeal in a market that is increasingly price‑sensitive yet still hungry for high‑end features.

What’s on the table?

The Buds Pro 2 are a premium pair of true‑wireless earbuds that combine active noise cancellation (ANC) with LDAC support, a high‑resolution audio codec that delivers richer sound on compatible devices. The earbuds also feature a smart dial on the charging case, allowing users to control playback, volume, and ANC settings without touching their phone.

Why the price cut matters

CMF’s decision to reduce the price by 29% is significant for several reasons. First, it positions the Buds Pro 2 as a more accessible option in a crowded mid‑tier market dominated by brands such as Sony, Bose, and Apple. Second, the discount aligns with a broader trend of manufacturers offering high‑quality audio experiences at lower price points to capture market share from premium competitors. Finally, the move could help CMF attract new customers who have been hesitant to invest in ANC earbuds due to cost concerns.

Feature highlights

  • Active Noise Cancellation: The earbuds use dual microphones to cancel out ambient noise, making them suitable for commuting, travel, and office use.
  • LDAC Support: When paired with an LDAC‑enabled device, the Buds Pro 2 deliver up to 990 kbps audio, offering a noticeable improvement over standard Bluetooth codecs.
  • Smart Dial: The charging case includes a touch‑sensitive dial that lets users adjust volume, skip tracks, and toggle ANC without reaching for their phone.
  • Battery Life: The earbuds provide up to 6 hours of playback on a single charge, with an additional 12 hours from the case.

Market impact

Analysts predict that the price reduction could spur a shift in consumer behavior, encouraging buyers who previously opted for lower‑end models to upgrade to the Buds Pro 2. The discount also puts pressure on competitors to reevaluate their pricing strategies, especially as the mid‑tier segment continues to grow.

Where to buy

Customers can purchase the CMF Buds Pro 2 on Amazon’s website, where the 29% discount is currently active. The deal is expected to last for a limited time, so buyers are advised to act quickly if they want to take advantage of the savings.

Conclusion

CMF’s record‑low pricing for the Buds Pro 2 underscores the company’s commitment to making premium audio technology more affordable. With ANC, LDAC, and a smart dial case, the earbuds offer a compelling package that could redefine value in the true‑wireless market.
