TCL’s NXTPAPER 14 Tablet Drops 22% in Amazon Lightning Deal

2026-11-12 · 3 min read
TCL’s large‑screen Android tablet with a paper‑like display is now available at a 22% discount on Amazon, slashing the price to $100.

TCL has announced a limited‑time Lightning Deal on its NXTPAPER 14, a 14‑inch Android tablet that mimics the feel of paper. The promotion cuts the retail price by 22%, bringing the device down to $100 on Amazon.

What Makes the NXTPAPER 14 Stand Out

The tablet’s key feature is its E‑ink display, which offers a paper‑like reading experience with low power consumption. It runs the latest Android OS and supports a range of productivity apps, making it suitable for both reading and light work.

Deal Details and Availability

The Lightning Deal is available for a short period and is part of Amazon’s early Black Friday promotions. Customers can purchase the tablet directly from Amazon’s website, and the discount is applied automatically at checkout.

Why the Deal Matters

With the rise of e‑ink tablets and the growing demand for low‑power, eye‑friendly devices, TCL’s price cut positions the NXTPAPER 14 as an attractive option for students, professionals, and casual readers alike. The discount also aligns with Amazon’s strategy to boost sales during the holiday shopping season.
