United Airlines Limits AI Agents From Booking Flights Amid Security Concerns

2026-11-12 · 3 min read
United Airlines has announced a ban on AI agents booking flights, citing security risks and potential regulatory violations.

United Airlines has taken a decisive step to curb the use of artificial‑intelligence agents in its booking system. The carrier announced that, effective immediately, AI‑powered assistants will no longer be able to reserve seats or process ticket purchases on its website and mobile app.

Why the restriction?

The airline cited growing concerns over security and compliance. United’s spokesperson said that AI agents could pose a risk of violating Department of Transportation and Federal Aviation Administration rules, especially if the systems are not fully vetted for fraud detection and passenger data protection.

Impact on customers

Passengers who previously used chatbots or virtual assistants to book flights will need to switch to traditional booking methods. United’s customer service team has stated that the change will not affect the availability of flights or the overall booking experience, but it will require users to manually enter flight details or use the airline’s standard booking interface.

Industry reaction

Tech analysts note that United’s move reflects a broader industry debate about the role of AI in travel. While some airlines are experimenting with AI to streamline customer interactions, others are wary of the potential for data breaches and regulatory non‑compliance.

Future outlook

United has not ruled out the possibility of re‑introducing AI booking tools in the future, provided that the technology can meet stringent security standards. For now, the airline’s decision underscores the importance of balancing innovation with regulatory compliance in the rapidly evolving travel tech landscape.
