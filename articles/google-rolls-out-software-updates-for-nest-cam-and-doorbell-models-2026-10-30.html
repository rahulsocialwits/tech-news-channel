Google Rolls Out Software Updates for Nest Cam and Doorbell Models

2026-10-30 · 3 min read
Google releases new firmware for several Nest Cam and Doorbell devices, adding features and security fixes.

Google has announced a series of software updates for a range of its Nest Cam and Doorbell products. The rollout, which began on October 30, 2026, includes new features, performance improvements, and security patches for several models that have been on the market for years.

What’s New in the Update

The updates bring a number of enhancements. Users can now enjoy improved motion detection accuracy, faster response times, and a more stable connection to the cloud. Security teams have also addressed a handful of vulnerabilities that could have allowed unauthorized access to camera feeds.

Supported Devices

Google confirmed that the firmware upgrade will be available for the following Nest Cam and Doorbell models:

  • Nest Cam (Indoor)
  • Nest Cam (Outdoor)
  • Nest Cam IQ (Indoor)
  • Nest Doorbell (Video)
  • Nest Doorbell (Battery)

Owners of these devices can check for the update through the Google Home app, where a notification will appear once the new firmware is ready to install.

Why It Matters

Smart home cameras are a popular target for cyberattacks, and keeping firmware up to date is a key defense strategy. By pushing these updates, Google is reinforcing its commitment to device security and ensuring that users can continue to rely on their Nest products for reliable home monitoring.
