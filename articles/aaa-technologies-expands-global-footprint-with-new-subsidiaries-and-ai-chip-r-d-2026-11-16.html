AAA Technologies Expands Global Footprint with New Subsidiaries and AI Chip R&D

2026-11-16 · 3 min read
India’s AAA Technologies launches subsidiaries in Singapore, Mauritius, and Dubai to grow its cybersecurity and semiconductor design businesses.

India’s first listed information‑security firm, AAA Technologies, announced a strategic expansion that will see it establish four wholly owned subsidiaries in Singapore, Mauritius and Dubai. The move is aimed at accelerating the company’s global reach and boosting its edge‑AI chip research and development.

New Subsidiaries to Drive Growth

The company’s board approved the creation of the subsidiaries to support its growing semiconductor design arm and to deepen its cybersecurity offerings across key international markets. By setting up local entities, AAA Technologies intends to tap into the robust tech ecosystems of Southeast Asia and the Middle East.

Focus on Edge AI Chip R&D

Alongside the geographic expansion, AAA Technologies highlighted plans to invest in edge‑AI chip research and development. The company’s semiconductor design team will work on next‑generation chips that can deliver high performance while maintaining low power consumption, a critical requirement for many security applications.

Implications for the Cybersecurity Landscape

With the new subsidiaries, AAA Technologies will be better positioned to offer tailored security solutions to multinational clients. The expansion also signals a broader trend of Indian cybersecurity firms seeking to establish a presence in global tech hubs.
