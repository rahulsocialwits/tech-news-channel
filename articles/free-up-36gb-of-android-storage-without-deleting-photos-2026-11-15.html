Free Up 36GB of Android Storage Without Deleting Photos

2026-11-15 · 3 min read
Learn how to reclaim 36GB of space on an Android phone using built‑in tools and smart settings, all without deleting any photos.

Android users often find themselves running out of storage, especially when photos and videos pile up. A recent guide from Android Authority shows that it’s possible to free up 36GB of space on a phone without deleting any photos, simply by tweaking a few settings and using built‑in features.

Why Storage Is a Pain Point

Modern smartphones capture high‑resolution images and 4K videos, which can quickly consume internal memory. Many users rely on cloud backups, but the local cache and duplicated files still take up valuable space.

Key Steps to Reclaim Space

  • Clear Cached Data: Go to Settings > Storage > Cached data and clear it. This removes temporary files that apps store for quick access.
  • Manage Downloads: Delete or move large files from the Downloads folder to cloud storage or a computer.
  • Use Google Photos’ Storage Saver: Enable the feature that compresses photos to free space while keeping originals in the cloud.
  • Offload Unused Apps: Android’s “App Offload” feature removes the app’s data but keeps the icon, allowing you to reinstall it later.
  • Move Media to SD Card: If the device supports an SD card, transfer photos, videos, and music to the external storage.

Resulting Impact

By following these steps, users can recover tens of gigabytes of storage, extending the life of their device and reducing the need for external storage solutions.
