Motorola Unveils Edge 70 Neo, a 7,000‑mAh Powerhouse for India’s Premium Market

2026-11-24 · 3 min read
Motorola’s new Edge 70 Neo packs a massive 7,000‑mAh battery, 45W TurboPower charging and 7.5W reverse charging, targeting India’s premium smartphone buyers.

Motorola has announced the Edge 70 Neo, a new premium smartphone aimed at India’s high‑end market. The device is built around a 7,000‑mAh battery that supports 45W TurboPower fast charging and 7.5W wired reverse charging, giving users both quick top‑ups and the ability to share power with other devices.

Key Features

  • Battery and Charging: 7,000‑mAh capacity, 45W TurboPower charging, 7.5W wired reverse charging.
  • Display: Edge‑to‑edge screen with high refresh rate (specifics not disclosed).
  • Performance: Powered by a recent Snapdragon chipset (details not provided).
  • Camera: Triple‑camera setup with a primary sensor (exact specifications not listed).

Market Positioning

The launch comes as Indian employers report a surge in tech hiring, with 90% expanding teams to capture AI‑driven growth. Motorola’s Edge 70 Neo is positioned to appeal to professionals who need long battery life and fast charging for on‑the‑go productivity.

Availability

Motorola has not yet announced a release date or pricing, but the company is targeting the premium segment in India.
