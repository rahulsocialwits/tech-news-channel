Google Expands Gemini to Apple CarPlay, Bringing AI Chat to the Road

2026-11-12 · 3 min read
Google’s Gemini chatbot is now available on Apple CarPlay, letting drivers access the AI assistant directly from their car’s infotainment system.

Google has rolled out its Gemini chatbot to Apple CarPlay, allowing users to interact with the AI assistant while driving. The move builds on Gemini’s existing presence in the Google Maps app, where it already helps users with navigation and local queries.

How Gemini Works on CarPlay

Through the new CarPlay integration, drivers can ask Gemini questions about traffic, nearby restaurants, or general knowledge, and receive spoken responses via the vehicle’s audio system. The feature is designed to keep drivers’ hands on the wheel and eyes on the road, following Apple’s safety guidelines for in‑car apps.

What This Means for In‑Car AI

Apple CarPlay has traditionally focused on third‑party apps like Spotify and WhatsApp. Adding Gemini expands the platform’s AI capabilities, positioning it as a more comprehensive assistant that can handle a broader range of tasks beyond media playback.

Future Outlook

Google’s decision to partner with Apple for CarPlay reflects a broader trend of AI assistants moving into automotive environments. As more automakers adopt connected infotainment systems, we can expect similar integrations from other AI providers in the coming months.
