Walmart’s Shelf Price Blunder Turns Into Massive Gaming PC Deal

2026-11-01 · 3 min read
A mispriced Walmart display offered two high‑end gaming rigs for a fraction of their cost, sparking a frenzy among PC builders.

Walmart’s accidental price tag on a CyberPowerPC gaming desktop turned a routine shopping trip into a headline‑making deal. The retailer listed the system, featuring a Ryzen 9 9900X3D processor and an RTX 5070 graphics card, at just $1,099—well below its $2,299 retail price.

What the Deal Included

The configuration, known as the “CyberPowerPC Gamer Xtreme VR” in the store, comes with 16 GB of DDR5 RAM, a 1 TB NVMe SSD, and a 750 W power supply. The RTX 5070, a mid‑range GPU from Nvidia’s 50‑series, delivers solid performance for 1440p gaming and supports ray tracing.

How the Mistake Was Corrected

Walmart’s customer service team confirmed the error and offered a refund to the customer who purchased the unit at the discounted price. The company also announced that the mispriced items would be removed from the shelves and that the price would be corrected to the advertised $2,299.

Impact on the PC Building Community

The incident sparked a wave of online discussions, with many PC enthusiasts sharing screenshots of the deal and debating whether to take advantage of the offer before it was fixed. The story highlights the importance of accurate pricing in retail environments, especially for high‑value tech products.
