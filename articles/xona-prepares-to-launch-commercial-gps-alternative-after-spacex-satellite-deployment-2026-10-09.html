Xona Prepares to Launch Commercial GPS Alternative After SpaceX Satellite Deployment

2026-10-09 · 3 min read
Xona’s precision timing and navigation service is set to enter beta testing following the launch of six company‑designed satellites by SpaceX.

Xona, a startup that has been quietly developing a commercial GPS alternative, is poised to begin beta testing its precision timing and navigation service today. The company’s launch follows the successful deployment of six satellites, all designed by Xona, aboard a SpaceX launch vehicle.

Satellite Launch and Service Readiness

The satellites were launched on a Falcon 9 rocket, and Xona’s engineers have confirmed that the constellation is now fully operational. With the new network in place, the company plans to offer a more accurate and resilient positioning service for a range of commercial applications.

Implications for the Navigation Market

By providing an alternative to the traditional GPS infrastructure, Xona aims to reduce dependence on government‑controlled systems and offer higher precision for industries such as logistics, autonomous vehicles, and mapping services. The beta phase will allow early adopters to test the system’s performance and provide feedback before a broader rollout.

Future Outlook

As satellite navigation becomes increasingly critical for emerging technologies, Xona’s entry into the market could spur further innovation and competition. The company’s next steps include expanding its satellite constellation and integrating its service with existing mapping platforms.
