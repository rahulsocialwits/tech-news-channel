Export‑Banned Nvidia Chips Find Their Way into Chinese AI Models

2026-11-16 · 3 min read
Chinese AI developers are using Nvidia’s Blackwell GPUs despite export bans, according to new findings.

Recent research has revealed that Nvidia’s Blackwell GPUs, which are subject to U.S. export restrictions, are appearing in Chinese artificial‑intelligence models. The study, published by the Indian Express, examined the hardware configurations of several high‑profile AI systems and identified the banned chips in their training pipelines.

How the Chips Are Getting In

Analysts traced the supply chain back to a mix of third‑party resellers and local distributors that have managed to bypass the U.S. sanctions. The findings suggest that the chips are being sourced through indirect channels, allowing Chinese firms to continue leveraging Nvidia’s advanced architecture for model training.

Implications for the Global AI Landscape

While the U.S. government has tightened controls on Blackwell GPUs, the discovery indicates that enforcement is more complex than anticipated. The presence of these chips in Chinese AI models could give domestic developers a performance edge, potentially reshaping competitive dynamics in the industry.

Industry Response

Both Nvidia and U.S. regulators have not yet issued a formal statement on the findings. However, the incident underscores the challenges of monitoring technology transfers in an increasingly interconnected market.
