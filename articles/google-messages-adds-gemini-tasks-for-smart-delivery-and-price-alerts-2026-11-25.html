Google Messages Adds Gemini Tasks for Smart Delivery and Price Alerts

2026-11-25 · 3 min read
Google Messages now integrates Gemini Tasks to provide real‑time delivery updates and price alerts directly in the chat interface.

Google Messages has rolled out a new feature that lets users receive real‑time delivery updates and price alerts through the Gemini Tasks integration. The update, announced on November 25, 2026, brings the power of Google’s Gemini language model directly into the messaging app, allowing users to get actionable information without leaving their conversation threads.

How It Works

When a user receives a shipping notification or a price‑drop alert, the message is automatically parsed by Gemini Tasks. The model then generates a concise summary and actionable next steps, such as tracking a package or adding an item to a wish list. Users can also ask follow‑up questions, and Gemini will respond with relevant details pulled from the original message or external data sources.

Benefits for Everyday Users

By embedding Gemini Tasks in Google Messages, the company aims to reduce the friction of managing logistics and shopping alerts. Instead of opening separate apps or websites, users can keep all information in one place. The feature also supports voice commands, enabling hands‑free interaction for those on the go.

Privacy and Security

Google has emphasized that all data processed by Gemini Tasks remains within the secure confines of the Messages app. The company states that no personal data is stored beyond the duration of the conversation, and users can delete messages to remove any associated task data.

Future Outlook

While the current rollout focuses on delivery and price alerts, Google has hinted at expanding Gemini Tasks to other domains, such as travel itineraries and event reminders. The integration marks a step toward a more conversational and context‑aware messaging experience.
