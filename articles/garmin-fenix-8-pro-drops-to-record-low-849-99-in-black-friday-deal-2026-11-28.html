Garmin Fenix 8 Pro Drops to Record‑Low $849.99 in Black Friday Deal

2026-11-28 · 3 min read
The 51mm Garmin Fenix 8 Pro smartwatch, featuring LTE and satellite messaging, is now available for $849.99, a $350 savings from its original price.

Garmin’s flagship Fenix 8 Pro smartwatch has slashed its price to a record‑low $849.99 as part of a Black Friday promotion. The 51mm model, which adds LTE connectivity and satellite messaging to Garmin’s rugged AMOLED watch line, was originally priced at $1,199.99. The discount brings the cost down by $350, making it one of the most significant savings on a high‑end smartwatch this year.

Key Features of the Fenix 8 Pro

  • LTE Connectivity – Enables calls, texts, and app notifications without a paired phone.
  • Satellite Messaging – Allows emergency SOS messages to be sent via satellite when no cellular network is available.
  • Rugged Design – Built to meet military standards for durability and water resistance.
  • AMOLED Display – Provides vibrant colors and high contrast for outdoor visibility.

Why the Deal Matters

For consumers looking for a premium smartwatch that can operate independently of a smartphone, the Fenix 8 Pro offers a compelling combination of features and durability. The price cut makes the device more accessible to a broader audience, potentially boosting Garmin’s market share in the high‑end wearable segment.

How to Grab the Offer

Garmin’s official website and authorized retailers are currently offering the discounted price. The promotion is expected to run through the end of the Black Friday weekend, after which the price may revert to its original level.
