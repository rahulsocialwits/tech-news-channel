Anker Unveils Limited‑Edition Wukong Power Bank at $149.99

2026-09-29 · 3 min read
Anker’s Black Myth: Wukong Prime 20K power bank drops to $149.99, offering 220W charging and app support.

For tech enthusiasts looking for a high‑capacity, fast‑charging portable power solution, Anker has just released a new limited‑edition model that promises to deliver on both performance and style. The Black Myth: Wukong Prime 20K power bank is now available for $149.99, a 25% discount from its original price.

Key Features

  • Capacity: 20,000 mAh, enough to recharge most smartphones multiple times or a tablet in a single charge.
  • Output: 220W charging capability supports fast charging for laptops and other high‑power devices.
  • App Integration: The power bank can be monitored and controlled via Anker’s companion app, providing real‑time status and usage analytics.

Design and Availability

The Wukong Prime 20K sports a sleek black finish inspired by the mythical Monkey King, with a matte surface that resists fingerprints. It is part of Anker’s limited‑edition Black Myth series, which includes other themed accessories. The device is currently available through Anker’s official website and select retail partners.

Why It Matters

With the growing demand for portable power solutions that can keep up with the latest laptops and smartphones, the Wukong Prime 20K offers a compelling combination of high capacity and rapid charging. The 25% discount makes it an attractive option for consumers who need reliable power on the go without breaking the bank.
