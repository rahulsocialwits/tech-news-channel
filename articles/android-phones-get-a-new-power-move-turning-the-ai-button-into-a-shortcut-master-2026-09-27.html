Android Phones Get a New Power Move: Turning the AI Button into a Shortcut Master

2026-09-27 · 3 min read
Android users can now repurpose the built‑in AI button into a powerful shortcut key, boosting productivity and customization on their devices.

Android phones have long carried a dedicated AI button, a small physical or virtual key that launches the device’s on‑device AI assistant. While many users have found the button underutilized, a recent guide from Android Authority shows how to transform it into a versatile shortcut that can trigger a wide range of actions, from launching apps to running custom scripts.

Why the AI Button Matters

The AI button is a hardware feature that appears on several recent Android models, including the Google Pixel line and certain Samsung Galaxy devices. It is designed to provide quick access to the phone’s on‑device AI services, such as voice commands, contextual suggestions, and predictive text. However, the button’s default function is often limited to a single voice‑assistant launch, leaving many users wondering if there is more to do with it.

Turning the Button into a Shortcut Key

The guide explains that the button can be reprogrammed through the device’s settings menu. By navigating to the “Accessibility” section and selecting the AI button options, users can assign a custom action to the button. Options include opening a specific app, toggling a system setting, or executing a predefined macro. The process is straightforward: choose the desired action, confirm the change, and the button will now perform the new function whenever pressed.

Practical Use Cases

  • Quick Access to Productivity Apps: Assign the button to launch a note‑taking app or a task manager, enabling instant capture of ideas.
  • System Controls: Use the button to toggle Wi‑Fi, Bluetooth, or airplane mode, streamlining connectivity management.
  • Custom Macros: Combine multiple actions—such as muting the phone, launching a messaging app, and sending a pre‑written text—into a single button press.

Benefits for Power Users

For users who rely on their phones for work or creative tasks, repurposing the AI button can reduce the number of taps required to perform routine actions. It also offers a tactile alternative to on‑screen shortcuts, which can be especially useful in low‑light environments or when the screen is occupied by other content.

Limitations and Considerations

While the button’s new functionality is powerful, it is not universally available on all Android devices. Manufacturers may restrict access to the button’s settings, and some models may not support custom actions. Additionally, the button’s default voice‑assistant function can be restored by resetting the settings, so users should be mindful of how they configure it.

Conclusion

The Android Authority guide demonstrates that the AI button is more than a gimmick; it can become a central hub for personalized shortcuts. By following the simple steps outlined in the article, users can tailor their devices to better fit their workflow, turning a single button into a multi‑purpose tool that enhances productivity and convenience.
