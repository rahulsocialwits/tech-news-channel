Google Rolls Out Android 17 QPR2 Beta 7.1 for Pixel Devices

2026-10-18 · 3 min read
Google has released the Android 17 QPR2 Beta 7.1 update for Pixel phones, adding new features and bug fixes without major UI changes.

Google has officially rolled out the Android 17 QPR2 Beta 7.1 update for its Pixel lineup. The update arrives a week after the last preview and focuses on stability and performance improvements rather than flashy new features.

What’s new in the beta?

The release includes a number of bug fixes that address minor crashes and connectivity issues reported by early testers. It also brings subtle enhancements to the system UI, such as smoother animations and improved battery‑management algorithms. No major user‑facing changes were announced, indicating that Google is prioritizing reliability at this stage.

How to get the update

Pixel owners can download the update directly from the Settings app under System > Advanced > System update. The rollout is gradual, so users may see the update appear a few days after the initial release.

Why the beta matters

By releasing a beta version, Google allows developers and power users to test new code paths and provide feedback before the final stable release. This iterative approach helps catch regressions early and ensures a smoother experience for the broader user base.
