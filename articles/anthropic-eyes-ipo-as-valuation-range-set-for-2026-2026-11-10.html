Anthropic Eyes IPO as Valuation Range Set for 2026

2026-11-10 · 3 min read
Anthropic is moving forward with an initial public offering, with its valuation range expected to be announced this week.

Anthropic, the AI research company founded by former OpenAI employees, is reportedly advancing its plans to go public. According to a report from the Wall Street Journal, the company is expected to set its valuation range in the coming days, signaling a significant step toward an initial public offering.

IPO Timeline and Valuation

The company’s leadership has indicated that the valuation range will be disclosed soon, a move that could attract investor interest and provide a new source of capital for further research and development. While specific numbers have not yet been released, the announcement suggests that Anthropic is preparing to enter the public markets.

Industry Context

Anthropic’s potential IPO comes at a time when several AI firms are exploring public market options. The move could position the company alongside other AI leaders and provide a platform for scaling its technology and expanding its product offerings.
