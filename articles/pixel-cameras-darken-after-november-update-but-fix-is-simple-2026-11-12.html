Pixel Cameras Darken After November Update, but Fix Is Simple

2026-11-12 · 3 min read
Google’s latest Android update has caused Pixel camera apps to go dark, yet a quick software tweak restores full functionality.

Google’s November Android update has left many Pixel users frustrated as the camera app suddenly turns black. The issue appears to affect all Pixel models that received the latest software patch, causing the viewfinder to go dark when the camera is launched.

What’s Causing the Problem?

According to Android Authority, the update introduced a change in the camera’s rendering pipeline that conflicts with the device’s graphics driver. The result is a blank screen that prevents users from taking photos or recording video.

How to Fix It Quickly

The company has released a straightforward workaround: reset the camera app’s data. Users can navigate to Settings → Apps → Camera, then tap “Storage” and select “Clear data.” After restarting the device, the camera should function normally again.

Google’s Response

Google has acknowledged the glitch and is working on a patch that will be rolled out in a future update. In the meantime, the reset method offers a temporary solution for those who need to capture moments right away.
