Turn Your Old Router Into a Powerful Network Extender

2026-09-30 · 3 min read
Repurpose a discarded router to boost Wi‑Fi coverage and add smart home features without buying new hardware.

When a router finally reaches the end of its useful life, most people toss it into the recycling bin. A new guide from Engadget shows that the device can be transformed into a versatile network extender, saving money and reducing e‑waste.

Why Repurpose?

Instead of buying a new access point, the old router can be configured to act as a Wi‑Fi repeater or a dedicated smart‑home hub. The process involves a simple firmware update and a few configuration steps, making it accessible to non‑technical users.

Step‑by‑Step Setup

  1. Reset the router to factory settings to clear any previous configurations.
  2. Install the latest firmware that supports repeater mode. Many manufacturers provide this for free on their support pages.
  3. Configure repeater settings by entering the SSID and password of the main network. The router will then broadcast the same network name, extending coverage.
  4. Optional: Set up a separate SSID for IoT devices to keep them isolated from the main network.

Beyond Wi‑Fi Extension

With the right firmware, the repurposed router can also serve as a smart‑home hub, integrating with voice assistants and automating lighting or security systems. This dual functionality turns a single device into a cost‑effective networking and automation solution.

Environmental Impact

By extending the life of existing hardware, consumers reduce electronic waste and lower the demand for new components. The guide highlights that a single repurposed router can cut the carbon footprint associated with manufacturing a new access point by up to 30%.
