Lenovo LOQ Notebook with Arc B390 Graphics Hits $799 This Black Friday

2026-11-28 · 3 min read
Lenovo’s LOQ gaming notebook, powered by Intel Arc B390, is available for just $799 during Black Friday sales.

Black Friday shoppers looking for a budget‑friendly gaming laptop have a new option on the table. Lenovo’s LOQ notebook, which pairs a 12th‑generation Intel Core processor with an Intel Arc B390 graphics card, is now available for $799. The deal is part of a broader push to make high‑performance gaming hardware more accessible to mainstream consumers.

Specs that Matter

The LOQ notebook sports a 15.6‑inch Full HD display with a 144‑Hz refresh rate, making it suitable for fast‑paced titles like GTA 6. Internally, the machine runs on a Core i5‑1240P processor and 8GB of DDR4 RAM, which can be upgraded to 16GB for users who need more multitasking headroom. The Intel Arc B390 GPU delivers solid 1080p performance, allowing players to run recent games at medium to high settings without breaking the bank.

Why the Price Is a Deal

Historically, gaming laptops with discrete GPUs have carried a premium price tag. Lenovo’s decision to price the LOQ at $799 is a strategic move to capture the mid‑tier market, especially during the holiday shopping season. The discount brings the notebook within reach of students and casual gamers who previously might have opted for a desktop or a more expensive ultrabook.

What Buyers Should Know

While the LOQ offers impressive specs for its price, potential buyers should consider a few factors. The laptop’s battery life is modest, typically lasting around 4–5 hours under moderate use, which is typical for gaming notebooks. Additionally, the 8GB of RAM is the minimum configuration; upgrading to 16GB will provide a smoother experience, particularly when running multiple applications simultaneously.

Where to Find the Deal

Retailers across the United States and Canada are offering the LOQ at the discounted price during the Black Friday window. Lenovo’s official website and major e‑commerce platforms such as Amazon and Best Buy list the laptop at $799, with shipping options that vary by region. The promotion is expected to run through the first week of December, so interested buyers should act quickly to secure the best price.
