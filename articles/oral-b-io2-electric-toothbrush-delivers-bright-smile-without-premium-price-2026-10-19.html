Oral‑B iO2 Electric Toothbrush Delivers Bright Smile Without Premium Price

2026-10-19 · 3 min read
The new Oral‑B iO2 electric toothbrush offers advanced cleaning features and smart connectivity at a budget‑friendly price.

Oral‑B has launched its latest electric toothbrush, the iO2, positioning it as a high‑performance yet affordable option for consumers looking to upgrade their oral care routine. The device comes with a range of features that rival premium models, but at a price point that is noticeably lower.

Key Features

  • Smart Connectivity – The iO2 pairs with a companion app that tracks brushing habits, offers personalized coaching, and provides real‑time feedback on technique.
  • Multiple Cleaning Modes – Users can choose from standard, whitening, gum care, and deep clean settings, each calibrated to deliver optimal plaque removal.
  • Long‑Lasting Battery – A single charge powers the brush for up to two weeks of daily use, thanks to an efficient motor and low‑power design.

Design and Usability

The toothbrush features a sleek, ergonomic handle that fits comfortably in the hand. Its brush head is engineered for gentle yet effective cleaning, with bristles that adapt to the contours of the teeth and gums. The device’s lightweight construction makes it easy to maneuver, while the clear LED display provides quick status updates.

Price and Availability

Retailers are offering the iO2 at a price that sits below many of its competitors in the same category. This positioning makes it an attractive choice for consumers who want advanced features without the premium cost.
