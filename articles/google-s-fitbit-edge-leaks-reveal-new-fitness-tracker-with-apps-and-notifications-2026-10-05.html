Google’s Fitbit Edge Leaks Reveal New Fitness Tracker With Apps and Notifications

2026-10-05 · 3 min read
Early leaks show Google’s upcoming Fitbit Edge featuring a screen, app support and notification capabilities, hinting at a major refresh of the brand’s wearable lineup.

Google’s next fitness tracker, the Fitbit Edge, has surfaced in a series of leaks that give a clear picture of what the company is planning for its wearable line. The device is expected to replace the current Fitbit Charge, which has been on the market since 2023.

Design and Display

The Edge is rumored to feature a small, high‑resolution display that will allow users to view notifications and run third‑party apps directly on the watch. According to the leaks, the screen will be available in the same color palette as the Pixel 11, giving the device a cohesive look with Google’s flagship phone.

App Ecosystem

Google has confirmed that the Fitbit Edge will support a growing ecosystem of apps. The company’s recent statement about Google Books’ Android app support indicates that Google is working to ensure compatibility across a wide range of Android applications, a trend that is likely to extend to the new wearable.

Release Timeline

While the exact launch date has not been announced, the leaks suggest that the Fitbit Edge will be available later this year. The device’s features point to a significant upgrade over the current Charge, positioning Google to compete more aggressively in the fitness tracker market.
