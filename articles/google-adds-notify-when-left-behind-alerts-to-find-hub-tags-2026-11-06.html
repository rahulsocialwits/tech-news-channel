Google Adds ‘Notify When Left Behind’ Alerts to Find Hub Tags

2026-11-06 · 3 min read
Google expands Find Hub’s tracking features with new left‑behind notifications for Android users.

Google has rolled out a new feature for its Find Hub tracking service that will send Android users a notification when a connected tag or tracker is left behind. The update, announced in a recent blog post, builds on the company’s existing location‑based alerts and is designed to help people keep track of items such as keys, wallets, or luggage.

How the Feature Works

When a Find Hub tag is attached to an item, the user can enable the “Notify when left behind” setting in the Find Hub app. If the tag’s signal drops while the user is still in the same location, the app will push a notification to the phone. The alert is triggered by the tag’s last known location and the user’s current position, allowing for real‑time reminders.

Why It Matters

Many people rely on Find Hub to locate misplaced items, but accidental loss can still happen. By adding a proactive notification, Google aims to reduce the chances of forgetting a tag‑attached item. The feature is available to all Android users who have the Find Hub app installed and a compatible tag.

Availability

The update was released to the Google Play Store on November 5, 2026, and is available in most countries. Users can enable the setting in the app’s settings menu under the “Notifications” section.
