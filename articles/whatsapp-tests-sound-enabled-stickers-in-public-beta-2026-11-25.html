WhatsApp Tests Sound‑Enabled Stickers in Public Beta

2026-11-25 · 3 min read
WhatsApp is experimenting with stickers that play audio clips, adding a new layer of interactivity to its messaging platform.

WhatsApp has rolled out a new public beta that lets users create stickers that play sound when tapped. The feature, announced by the company on November 25, 2026, expands the platform’s sticker ecosystem beyond static images and animated GIFs.

How the Feature Works

Developers can now embed short audio files—up to 30 seconds—in a sticker. When a user receives the sticker, tapping it triggers the sound, which plays in the chat without requiring the recipient to download a separate file. The audio is encoded in a format that keeps file sizes small, ensuring that stickers remain lightweight.

Implications for Messaging

Sound stickers add a new dimension to non‑verbal communication. They can convey emotions, emphasize jokes, or provide contextual cues that text alone may miss. The feature also opens up creative possibilities for brands and content creators looking to engage audiences with playful audio‑visual content.

Privacy and Moderation Considerations

WhatsApp has not yet detailed how it will handle moderation of audio stickers. The company’s privacy policy states that all media is end‑to‑end encrypted, but the introduction of sound may raise new concerns about data usage and content filtering. Users can still disable audio playback in the app settings if they prefer a silent experience.

Next Steps

WhatsApp plans to gather user feedback over the next few weeks before deciding whether to roll the feature out to all users. The company has encouraged beta testers to share their experiences on the official WhatsApp community forum.
