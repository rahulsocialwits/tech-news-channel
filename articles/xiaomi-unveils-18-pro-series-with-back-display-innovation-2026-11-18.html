Xiaomi Unveils 18 Pro Series with Back‑Display Innovation

2026-11-18 · 3 min read
Xiaomi launches the 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max featuring a rear display, Leica‑tuned cameras and Qualcomm’s latest chipset.

In a move that could reshape the premium smartphone market, Xiaomi today announced the launch of its new flagship line, the Xiaomi 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max. The company highlighted a number of firsts, most notably a rear display that offers a fresh way to interact with the device while keeping the front screen free for selfies and video calls.

Rear‑Display Design

The 18 Pro series features a 6.7‑inch OLED panel on the back, positioned just below the camera module. According to Xiaomi, the display can show notifications, music controls, and even a quick camera preview, allowing users to capture photos without opening the camera app. The front of the phone remains a 6.1‑inch OLED panel, maintaining a high‑resolution experience for everyday use.

Camera System

Both models continue Xiaomi’s partnership with Leica, offering a triple‑camera setup that includes a 108‑megapixel main sensor, a 12‑megapixel ultra‑wide lens, and a 5‑megapixel telephoto. The Leica tuning promises improved color accuracy and dynamic range, especially in low‑light conditions. The 18 Pro Max adds a 50‑megapixel periscope telephoto for up to 5x optical zoom.

Performance and Battery

Under the hood, the phones run on Qualcomm’s Snapdragon 8 Gen 3, the latest flagship chipset that delivers 30% faster CPU performance and 20% better GPU efficiency compared to its predecessor. Battery capacity is 5,000 mAh, with support for 120 W wired charging and 50 W wireless charging, allowing a full charge in just 15 minutes.

Software and Features

Xiaomi’s MIUI 14, based on Android 14, brings a host of new features. The UI includes a redesigned notification center that can be accessed from the rear display, a new privacy dashboard that tracks app permissions, and enhanced AI‑driven photo editing tools. The company also announced a new “Smart Lens” mode that uses AI to automatically adjust settings for optimal results in various lighting conditions.

Market Impact

Industry analysts see the rear‑display concept as a potential differentiator in a crowded premium segment. While the feature has been tested in concept form by other manufacturers, Xiaomi’s integration of a functional display with a high‑end camera system could set a new standard for user interaction. The 18 Pro series is priced at $1,099 for the base model and $1,299 for the Pro Max, positioning it directly against rivals such as Samsung’s Galaxy S24 Ultra and Apple’s iPhone 15 Pro.

Availability

Pre‑orders begin on November 20, with shipments expected to start in early December. Xiaomi will roll out the devices in key markets across Asia, Europe, and the Americas, with a focus on regions where the brand has seen strong growth in recent years.
