TSMC Considers Up to 20% CoWoS Price Hike Amid Nvidia Demand Surge

2026-10-27 · 3 min read
TSMC may raise CoWoS advanced packaging costs by 20% as Nvidia’s Blackwell and Rubin chips strain capacity, with equipment lead times stretching to eight months.

TSMC, the world’s largest semiconductor foundry, is reportedly weighing a price increase of up to 20% for its CoWoS (Chip‑on‑Wafer‑on‑Substrate) advanced packaging technology. The move comes as demand for high‑performance chips from Nvidia’s Blackwell and upcoming Rubin processors intensifies pressure on the company’s packaging capacity.

Why the Price Increase?

CoWoS is a key technology that allows multiple dies to be stacked and interconnected on a single substrate, enabling higher performance and lower power consumption. Nvidia’s Blackwell GPUs, slated for release later this year, and the Rubin line of CPUs are expected to consume a significant share of TSMC’s advanced packaging resources. The company’s equipment deliveries for CoWoS now reportedly take up to eight months, creating a bottleneck for customers who need rapid time‑to‑market.

Potential Cost Impact

In addition to the CoWoS price hike, TSMC is also expected to raise wafer prices by 5% to 10% in 2027. Combined, these changes could result in total cost increases of up to 25% for some customers. The company’s leadership has indicated that the price adjustments are necessary to sustain its advanced packaging capabilities and meet the growing demand from leading chip designers.

Industry Reactions

Analysts note that while the price increase could squeeze margins for chip designers, it also reflects the high value of TSMC’s advanced packaging services. The company’s ability to deliver cutting‑edge packaging solutions remains a competitive advantage in the semiconductor market.

Looking Ahead

TSMC’s decision to raise CoWoS prices underscores the broader trend of supply constraints in the semiconductor industry. As demand for high‑performance computing and AI workloads continues to grow, foundries are under pressure to balance capacity, cost, and innovation. The company’s upcoming announcements will likely provide further clarity on how it plans to manage these challenges while maintaining service levels for its customers.
