Upwind Secures $400M Series C, Reaches $3.8B Valuation Without Selling to Datadog

2026-10-29 · 3 min read
Cloud security firm Upwind raises $400 million in a Series C round, valuing the company at $3.8 billion after declining a Datadog acquisition offer.

Upwind, a cloud‑security startup that protects data in transit and at rest, announced today that it has closed a Series C financing round that raised $400 million. The new funding pushes the company’s valuation to $3.8 billion, matching the price it would have received had it sold itself to Datadog in a recent acquisition offer.

Why the deal matters

Upwind’s platform uses machine‑learning models to detect and block threats across multi‑cloud environments. The company’s growth has been driven by the increasing demand for secure cloud infrastructure as enterprises migrate workloads to public clouds.

Funding details

The round was led by a consortium of investors that includes existing backers and new participants. While the announcement did not disclose the full list of investors, the $400 million commitment signals strong confidence in Upwind’s technology and market position.

Strategic implications

By choosing to raise capital instead of selling, Upwind’s founders aim to maintain control and continue scaling their product. The move also reflects a broader trend of cloud‑security firms opting for growth through funding rather than acquisition.

Future outlook

With the new capital, Upwind plans to expand its AI‑driven threat detection capabilities and broaden its customer base across North America and Europe. The company also intends to invest in research to keep pace with evolving cyber‑threat landscapes.
