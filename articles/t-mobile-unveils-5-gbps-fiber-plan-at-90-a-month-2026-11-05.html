T‑Mobile Unveils 5 Gbps Fiber Plan at $90 a Month

2026-11-05 · 3 min read
T‑Mobile offers a 5 Gbps fiber internet plan for under $100 a month, promising a 2.5× speed boost and a 10‑year price guarantee.

T‑Mobile has announced a new 5 Gbps fiber internet plan priced at $90 per month, marking a significant step toward ultra‑fast home connectivity. The offer includes a 10‑year price guarantee, ensuring customers won’t face sudden rate hikes for a decade.

Key Features of the Plan

  • Speed: Up to 5 Gbps, roughly 2.5 times faster than the company’s previous high‑speed tier.
  • Affordability: The $90 monthly fee is under $100, making it one of the most competitive ultra‑fast options on the market.
  • Long‑Term Pricing: A 10‑year price guarantee locks in the rate, protecting subscribers from future price increases.

Implications for the Market

The launch positions T‑Mobile as a serious contender in the fiber‑optic race, traditionally dominated by providers such as Verizon and AT&T. By offering a high‑speed plan at a lower price point, the carrier aims to attract both residential and small‑business customers seeking reliable, high‑bandwidth connectivity.

What Customers Need to Know

While the plan’s speed and price are attractive, potential subscribers should verify availability in their area, as fiber rollout can vary by region. Installation costs and equipment fees may also apply, depending on local infrastructure.
