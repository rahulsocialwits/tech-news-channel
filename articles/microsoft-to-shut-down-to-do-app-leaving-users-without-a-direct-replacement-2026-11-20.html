Microsoft to Shut Down To Do App, Leaving Users Without a Direct Replacement

2026-11-20 · 3 min read
Microsoft announced the discontinuation of its To Do app, the successor to Wunderlist, with no immediate replacement in the works.

Microsoft has confirmed that its To Do app, which replaced the popular Wunderlist, will be shut down soon. The company has not announced any direct replacement for the service, leaving users to seek alternatives for task management and personal organization.

What to Expect from the Shutdown

The announcement came after Microsoft’s decision to phase out the To Do app, a move that follows the earlier discontinuation of Wunderlist. While the company has not provided a timeline, it has indicated that the app will be removed from the Microsoft Store and will no longer be available for download.

Impact on Users

Users who rely on To Do for daily task tracking, calendar integration, and collaboration will need to migrate their data to other services. Microsoft has not offered a direct migration tool, so manual export and import may be required.

Possible Alternatives

In the absence of a Microsoft replacement, users might consider third‑party task management apps such as Todoist, Asana, or Trello. These platforms offer similar features, including task lists, reminders, and integration with other productivity tools.

Why Microsoft Made the Decision

While the company has not detailed its reasoning, the move aligns with a broader strategy to streamline its productivity suite and focus on core services like Outlook and Teams.
