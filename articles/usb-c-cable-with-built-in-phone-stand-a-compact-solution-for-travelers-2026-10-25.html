USB‑C Cable with Built‑in Phone Stand: A Compact Solution for Travelers

2026-10-25 · 3 min read
A new USB‑C cable doubles as a phone stand, offering a lightweight, no‑bulk option for keeping devices upright while charging.

Travelers and commuters have long struggled with the inconvenience of carrying separate phone stands or bulky charging accessories. A new product from Statik Power, the PowerStand USB‑C cable, promises to solve this problem by combining a standard charging cable with a fold‑out kickstand, all without adding extra bulk to the cable itself.

Design and Functionality

The PowerStand cable retains the familiar shape of a typical USB‑C to USB‑C charger, but features a discreet hinge near the connector. When unfolded, the hinge extends into a small, rigid stand that can hold a smartphone or tablet at a comfortable viewing angle. The stand is engineered to be sturdy enough for most phone sizes while remaining compact enough to fit in a pocket or bag.

Use Cases for On‑the‑Go Users

For those who rely on their devices for navigation, streaming, or video calls, the ability to keep a phone upright while charging can be a significant convenience. The cable’s dual function eliminates the need for a separate charging dock or a separate stand, reducing the number of items that must be packed for a trip.

Compatibility and Charging Performance

Because the cable is a standard USB‑C to USB‑C connector, it supports the same charging speeds and data transfer rates as any other cable of the same specification. Users can expect the same performance when charging a phone, tablet, or other USB‑C‑enabled device, whether the stand is folded in or out.

Potential Market Impact

While the concept of a cable with an integrated stand is not entirely new, the PowerStand’s emphasis on minimalism and portability could appeal to a broad segment of consumers. By offering a single, lightweight solution, the product may attract frequent travelers, students, and professionals who value convenience without sacrificing functionality.

Availability and Pricing

Statik Power has announced that the PowerStand cable will be available for purchase online starting next month. Pricing details have not yet been released, but the company indicates that the product will be competitively priced relative to separate charging cables and phone stands.

Conclusion

The PowerStand USB‑C cable represents a thoughtful approach to everyday tech accessories, blending practicality with design simplicity. As consumers continue to seek ways to streamline their gear, this compact solution could become a staple for those who need a reliable, space‑saving way to keep their devices upright while charging.
