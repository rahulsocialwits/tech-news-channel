AI‑Powered Smartphones Could Push Prices Up to ₹3,000 in India

2026-11-08 · 3 min read
India’s smartphone market may see a 10% price hike after Diwali as AI features and supply constraints drive costs higher.

India’s smartphone market is on the brink of a price surge, with analysts warning that the cost of new devices could rise by as much as 10% after the Diwali holiday. The primary drivers are a global memory chip shortage, dwindling inventories, and a weaker rupee against the U.S. dollar, all of which are pushing manufacturers to increase retail prices.

AI Features and the Cost Factor

One of the key reasons for the expected price bump is the growing integration of artificial intelligence in smartphones. Manufacturers are adding AI‑powered cameras, voice assistants, and on‑device processing to enhance user experience. These features require more advanced hardware, such as higher‑end processors and larger memory modules, which are already scarce in the global supply chain.

Supply Chain Constraints

Memory chips, the backbone of modern smartphones, have been in short supply for several months. This shortage has forced companies to pay a premium for the components they do secure. Coupled with a weaker rupee, the cost of importing these chips has risen sharply, translating into higher retail prices for consumers.

Impact on Indian Consumers

Industry reports suggest that the price increase could reach up to ₹3,000 for certain models. While this may seem modest, it represents a significant percentage jump for mid‑range devices that are popular among price‑sensitive shoppers. Retailers are already preparing for a shift in demand, with some predicting that consumers may delay purchases until after the holiday season.

What This Means for the Market

Despite the price hike, the overall demand for smartphones in India remains robust. However, the market may see a shift toward more affordable, AI‑enhanced devices as manufacturers look to balance cost and feature set. Consumers are advised to compare specifications and pricing carefully, especially during the post‑Diwali sales period.
