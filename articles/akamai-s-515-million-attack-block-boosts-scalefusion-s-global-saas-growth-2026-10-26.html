Akamai’s 515‑Million‑Attack Block Boosts Scalefusion’s Global SaaS Growth

2026-10-26 · 3 min read
Akamai’s security platform stopped over 515 million attacks in 90 days, helping Scalefusion protect millions of devices and maintain 99.95% uptime.

In a recent announcement, Scalefusion, a leading mobile device management provider, revealed that its partnership with Akamai has helped block more than 515 million cyberattacks across 34 billion requests in just 90 days. The collaboration is credited with enabling Scalefusion to secure millions of connected devices while keeping its service availability at an impressive 99.95%.

How the partnership works

Scalefusion’s SaaS platform manages corporate mobile fleets, ensuring that devices stay compliant with security policies and that sensitive data remains protected. By integrating Akamai’s edge security services, the company can filter malicious traffic before it reaches its servers. Akamai’s real‑time threat intelligence feeds detect and block distributed denial‑of‑service (DDoS) attempts, credential stuffing, and other common attack vectors.

Impact on device security

With the 515 million attacks blocked, Scalefusion reports a significant reduction in successful intrusion attempts. The company’s customers, which include enterprises in finance, healthcare, and retail, have seen fewer security incidents and a lower risk of data breaches. The partnership also supports Scalefusion’s goal of maintaining near‑zero downtime, a critical requirement for businesses that rely on mobile devices for daily operations.

Industry context

Cyberattacks have surged in recent months, with the number of requests reaching 34 billion in the 90‑day period covered by the report. This spike reflects the growing sophistication of threat actors targeting cloud‑based services and mobile endpoints. Scalefusion’s experience underscores the importance of edge‑based security solutions that can absorb and mitigate attacks before they reach core infrastructure.

Future outlook

Scalefusion plans to expand its partnership with Akamai, adding new threat‑detection capabilities and deeper integration with its device‑management workflows. The company also aims to share its success metrics with the broader industry, encouraging other SaaS providers to adopt similar edge‑security strategies.

For organizations looking to protect their mobile fleets, Scalefusion’s partnership with Akamai offers a proven model of how combining SaaS management with robust edge security can deliver both high availability and strong threat protection.
