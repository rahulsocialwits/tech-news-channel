Walmart’s $60 Onn 4K Pro Google TV Streamer Could Be the Last Affordable Streaming Option

2026-10-17 · 3 min read
Walmart’s Onn 4K Pro streamer offers Google TV for just $60, a rare budget-friendly choice amid rising tech prices.

Walmart’s latest entry into the streaming market, the Onn 4K Pro Google TV streamer, is priced at a surprisingly low $60. The device brings Google TV’s familiar interface and app ecosystem to a budget that few competitors can match.

Key Features at a Low Price

The streamer supports 4K HDR content, includes a built‑in Chromecast, and runs the full Google TV operating system. Users can access popular services such as Netflix, Disney+, and YouTube, and the device offers voice control via Google Assistant. The compact design and plug‑and‑play setup make it a convenient option for households looking to upgrade their streaming experience without a hefty price tag.

Market Context

With prices for streaming hardware climbing—many models now exceeding $200—Walmart’s offer stands out as a potential bargain. The retailer’s strategy appears to focus on affordability, targeting consumers who want a straightforward streaming solution without the premium cost associated with brands like Roku or Amazon Fire TV.

Availability and Stock Concerns

Early reports indicate that the Onn 4K Pro may be difficult to find in stock, as demand quickly outpaces supply. Shoppers are advised to monitor Walmart’s website and sign up for restock alerts to secure the device before it sells out again.

Implications for the Streaming Market

Walmart’s entry could pressure other manufacturers to reconsider pricing strategies, especially as consumers become more price-sensitive. If the Onn 4K Pro maintains its performance and reliability, it may set a new benchmark for low-cost streaming devices.
