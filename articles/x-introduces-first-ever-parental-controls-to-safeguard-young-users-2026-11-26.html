X Introduces First‑Ever Parental Controls to Safeguard Young Users

2026-11-26 · 3 min read
X rolls out new parental control tools, allowing parents to set limits on content, time, and interactions for children’s accounts.

In a move that could reshape how social media is used by younger audiences, X has announced the launch of its first comprehensive parental control suite. The feature set, unveiled on Thursday, gives parents the ability to set time limits, filter content, and manage who can interact with their child’s account.

Key Features of the New Controls

According to Engadget, the controls are designed to be straightforward for parents to configure. The dashboard allows users to:

  • Set daily or weekly time caps on how long a child can spend on X.
  • Filter out potentially harmful content by blocking specific keywords or topics.
  • Restrict direct messages so that only approved contacts can send private messages.
  • Enable a “safe mode” that automatically hides posts containing mature language or imagery.

Why It Matters

Social media platforms have long faced criticism for their lack of robust safeguards for minors. X’s new tools aim to address these concerns by giving parents more control over their child’s digital environment. The feature is expected to be rolled out gradually, with an initial focus on users in the United States and Canada.

Implementation and Availability

Parents can access the new settings through the X app’s privacy menu. The controls are available for both Android and iOS devices, and X has stated that the feature will be free for all users. The company also plans to provide educational resources to help parents understand how to use the tools effectively.

Industry Response

Tech analysts see the move as a strategic response to growing regulatory pressure. With governments worldwide tightening rules around online safety for children, X’s parental controls could position the platform as a responsible choice for families.

Looking Ahead

While the rollout is still in its early stages, X has indicated that it will continue to refine the feature set based on user feedback. Future updates may include more granular controls, such as the ability to block specific users or enforce stricter content filters during certain hours of the day.
