Apple Unveils Slimmer iPad Mini with 8.5‑inch OLED Display

2026-10-27 · 3 min read
Apple’s new iPad Mini is thinner, lighter and sports a larger OLED screen, marking a significant design shift for the popular tablet.

Apple has announced a new version of its iPad Mini that promises a slimmer profile and a noticeably larger display. The updated tablet measures just 5.4 mm in thickness, making it the thinnest iPad Mini yet.

Display and Design

The device features an 8.5‑inch OLED panel, a jump from the 7.9‑inch screen on previous models. The larger screen is expected to improve media consumption and productivity, while the OLED technology delivers deeper blacks and richer colors.

Form Factor and Build

Apple has kept the overall dimensions largely unchanged, but the new design reduces bulk by shaving off 0.5 mm from the top and bottom edges. The result is a lighter tablet that feels more comfortable in one hand.

Software and Features

While Apple has not disclosed new software features, the iPad Mini will run the latest iPadOS, which includes enhanced multitasking and improved support for external keyboards.

Market Impact

With the iPad Mini’s slimmer profile and larger screen, Apple positions the device as a more compelling alternative to the iPad Air for users who want a compact yet powerful tablet.
