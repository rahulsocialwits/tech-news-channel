Google Photos Unveils Customizable Collections Tab in Major Redesign

2026-10-27 · 3 min read
Google Photos rolls out a redesigned Collections tab that lets users personalize their photo organization on Android.

Google Photos for Android has received a significant visual and functional overhaul with the launch of a new, customizable Collections tab. The update, announced on October 26, 2026, follows the company’s July introduction of a floating navigation bar and aims to give users more control over how they view and interact with their photo libraries.

What’s New in the Collections Tab?

The redesigned Collections tab replaces the previous static layout with a dynamic interface that allows users to rearrange, rename, and hide collections. Users can now drag and drop collections to prioritize the ones they use most often, making it easier to access favorite moments or themed albums directly from the home screen.

In addition to visual tweaks, the update introduces a new “Smart Collections” feature. These collections automatically group photos based on content, such as events, people, or locations, and can be customized to show only the photos that matter most to the user. The feature also supports filtering options, letting users exclude certain dates or people from a collection.

How It Works

To access the new Collections tab, users simply tap the “Collections” icon in the bottom navigation bar. From there, they can tap the gear icon to open the settings panel, where they can enable or disable individual collections, reorder them, or create new custom collections. The interface is designed to be intuitive, with clear drag handles and contextual menus that appear on long‑press.

Why It Matters

Google Photos has long been praised for its powerful search and organization tools, but the lack of flexibility in how collections are displayed has been a common user complaint. By allowing users to tailor the Collections tab to their preferences, Google is addressing a key usability issue and enhancing the overall photo‑management experience.

Moreover, the update aligns with Google’s broader strategy of improving user engagement on its mobile platforms. A more personalized interface encourages users to spend more time exploring and curating their memories, which in turn can drive higher retention rates for the app.

Next Steps for Users

Users who have already installed the latest Google Photos update will see the new Collections tab immediately. For those who haven’t, the update is available through the Google Play Store and can be installed by tapping the “Update” button on the app’s page. Google has also released a short tutorial video that walks users through the new features, which can be found in the app’s help section.

Looking Ahead

While the current update focuses on the Collections tab, Google has hinted at further enhancements to its photo‑management suite, including deeper integration with Google Drive and expanded AI‑driven editing tools. As the company continues to refine its mobile offerings, users can expect more personalized and powerful features in the coming months.
