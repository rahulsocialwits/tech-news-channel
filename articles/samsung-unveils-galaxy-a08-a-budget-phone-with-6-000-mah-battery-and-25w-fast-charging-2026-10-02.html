Samsung Unveils Galaxy A08, a Budget Phone with 6,000‑mAh Battery and 25W Fast Charging

2026-10-02 · 3 min read
Samsung’s new Galaxy A08 targets mid‑segment buyers in India, featuring a 6,000‑mAh battery, 25W charging, and a 6.5‑inch display.

Samsung has added a new entry‑level device to its Galaxy A series, the Galaxy A08, aimed at mid‑segment buyers in India. The phone is built around a 6,000‑mAh battery that supports 25W fast charging, a feature that has become a selling point for budget smartphones in the region.

Design and Display

The Galaxy A08 sports a 6.5‑inch display, a size that balances usability and portability. While the resolution and panel technology are not highlighted in the launch announcement, the screen is expected to match the standard for the A‑series line, offering a clear and bright viewing experience for everyday use.

Performance and Software

Under the hood, the device is powered by a mid‑range processor that delivers smooth performance for common tasks such as messaging, browsing, and light gaming. Samsung’s One UI, based on Android, provides a familiar interface with a focus on usability and battery optimization.

Battery Life and Charging

The 6,000‑mAh battery is a standout feature, promising extended usage between charges. Coupled with 25W fast charging, users can replenish the battery quickly, reducing downtime and keeping the phone ready for day‑to‑day activities.

Market Positioning

By targeting mid‑segment buyers, Samsung aims to capture consumers who seek reliable performance without the premium price tag of flagship models. The Galaxy A08’s battery capacity and fast‑charging capability position it as a practical choice for users who prioritize endurance and convenience.

Availability

Samsung has announced that the Galaxy A08 will be available in India, where the company has a strong presence and a growing demand for affordable smartphones. The launch is part of Samsung’s broader strategy to strengthen its foothold in the competitive mid‑tier market.
