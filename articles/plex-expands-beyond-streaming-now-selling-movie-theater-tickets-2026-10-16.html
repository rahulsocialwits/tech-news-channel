Plex Expands Beyond Streaming, Now Selling Movie Theater Tickets

2026-10-16 · 3 min read
Plex partners with studios to let users buy theater tickets directly through the app, using viewer data to personalize offers

Plex, the popular media‑streaming platform, has announced a new feature that lets users purchase movie theater tickets directly from the app. The move builds on Plex’s existing data‑driven recommendation engine, which now also informs ticket offers tailored to individual viewing habits.

How It Works

When a user watches a film on Plex, the service collects anonymized viewing data. That data is shared with partner studios and advertisers, who then use it to push relevant theater ticket offers to the user’s Plex interface. The tickets can be bought and printed or stored as a digital voucher, all within the same app.

Industry Impact

By integrating ticket sales, Plex is positioning itself as a one‑stop entertainment hub. The partnership also gives studios a new channel to monetize content beyond streaming, potentially boosting revenue from theatrical releases.

What Users Need to Know

Users will see ticket offers in the Plex app’s “Buy” section. The feature is currently available in select markets and will roll out globally over the next few months. Plex has emphasized that all data shared with partners is anonymized and complies with privacy regulations.
