Mobile Phone Retailers Brace for 10% Price Hike After Diwali Amid Sharp Sales Decline

2026-11-05 · 3 min read
India’s mobile retailers predict a 10% price increase post‑Diwali as sales slump, citing a 50% drop in volume expected to linger.

India’s mobile retail sector is preparing for a sharp price adjustment in the months following Diwali. According to the All India Mobile Retail Association (AIMRA), retailers anticipate a 10% hike in handset prices even as sales volumes are projected to fall by 50% after mid‑November.

Why the price rise?

Retailers point to a combination of supply‑chain constraints and a sustained dip in consumer demand. The post‑holiday period has seen a significant drop in footfall, with many consumers delaying purchases until the next major festival. This reduced demand, coupled with higher logistics costs, is prompting retailers to adjust prices to maintain margins.

Impact on consumers

For shoppers, the price increase means that the discounts typically offered during Diwali will be less attractive in the coming months. While the holiday season still offers substantial savings, the post‑Diwali period is expected to see fewer promotions and a shift toward higher‑priced models.

Industry response

Manufacturers are monitoring the trend closely. Some are considering targeted promotions to keep inventory moving, while others are exploring price‑matching strategies to stay competitive. Retailers, meanwhile, are focusing on enhancing in‑store experiences and digital engagement to offset the decline in sales volume.

Looking ahead

Industry analysts suggest that the 50% volume drop could persist for several months, potentially reshaping the market dynamics. Retailers are advised to diversify their product mix and strengthen their online presence to mitigate the impact of the price hike and the ongoing sales slump.
