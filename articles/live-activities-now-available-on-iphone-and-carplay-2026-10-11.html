Live Activities Now Available on iPhone and CarPlay

2026-10-11 · 3 min read
Apple expands Live Activities to iPhone and CarPlay, letting users view real‑time app data directly on the lock screen and in the CarPlay interface.

Apple has rolled out Live Activities to both iPhone and CarPlay, allowing users to keep an eye on real‑time information from supported apps without unlocking their device. The feature, first introduced with watchOS, now appears on the iPhone lock screen and in the CarPlay interface, giving drivers and commuters instant access to updates such as sports scores, ride‑share status, and delivery tracking.

How It Works

Developers can publish Live Activities through the ActivityKit framework, which sends updates to the system that then displays them in a dedicated area on the lock screen. When the user opens the phone or connects to CarPlay, the same live data is shown in a compact widget that can be interacted with directly.

Benefits for Users

By keeping critical information front and center, Live Activities reduce the need to open apps repeatedly. On CarPlay, the feature is especially useful for monitoring navigation, traffic, or vehicle diagnostics while driving.

Developer Support

Apple has provided updated documentation and sample code to help developers integrate Live Activities into their apps. The framework supports multiple update intervals and can be configured to stop when the user dismisses the activity.
