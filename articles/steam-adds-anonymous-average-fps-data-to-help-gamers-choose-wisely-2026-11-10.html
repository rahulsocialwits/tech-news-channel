Steam Adds Anonymous Average FPS Data to Help Gamers Choose Wisely

2026-11-10 · 3 min read
Valve now shows average FPS for thousands of Steam Deck games, giving buyers a clearer view of performance on their hardware.

Valve has rolled out a new feature on the Steam store that displays average frames‑per‑second (FPS) data for a growing list of games. The data is collected anonymously from users who match the hardware listed in the game’s system requirements, allowing buyers to see how a title performs on a similar setup before purchasing.

How the feature works

When a user visits a game’s store page, Steam checks the user’s hardware against the game’s minimum and recommended specs. If the user’s system falls within the recommended range, the store shows an average FPS figure derived from the performance data of other users with comparable hardware. The data is aggregated and anonymized, so individual players remain private.

Why it matters for gamers

Many gamers struggle to predict how a game will run on their specific rig, especially with the rise of high‑refresh‑rate monitors and demanding titles. By providing real‑world performance metrics, Steam aims to reduce the risk of buying a game that will under‑perform on a player’s machine.

Current coverage and future plans

At launch, the feature covers over 10,000 Steam Deck titles, with plans to expand to desktop PC games. The data is updated regularly as more users play the games, ensuring that the FPS figures remain relevant.

Community response

Early reactions from the Steam community have been positive, with many praising the transparency and the potential to make more informed purchasing decisions. Valve has indicated that the feature will continue to improve as more performance data is collected.
