Nvidia Announces CEO Transition Amid Internal Petition for Jensen Huang’s Return

2026-11-13 · 3 min read
Nvidia’s board names interim CEO Collette Kress and chair Harvey C. Jones as the company navigates a leadership change after Jensen Huang’s resignation.

Nvidia has officially announced a leadership transition following the resignation of founder and CEO Jensen Huang. The board has appointed Collette Kress, the company’s executive vice‑president and chief financial officer, as interim chief executive officer, while Harvey C. Jones will become the first‑ever chair of the board.

Board‑Led Restructuring

In a statement released on November 13, Nvidia’s board confirmed the change, noting that Kress has spent 25 years in various finance roles before joining the company in 2013. The move comes as the company seeks to maintain stability while it evaluates a permanent successor.

Employee Reaction

Within days of the announcement, an internal post on the company’s employee portal called for the board to consider reinstating Huang. The petition, titled “Bring Back THE Boss,” garnered more than 1,600 comments, the majority expressing support for the former CEO’s return.

Implications for Nvidia’s Future

With the transition underway, Nvidia’s strategic direction remains a focus for investors and analysts. The company’s recent product launches and market performance will be closely watched as it navigates this leadership change.
