Unlock Android’s Hidden Running Services Menu to Spot Battery‑Draining Apps

2026-11-15 · 3 min read
Learn how to access Android’s hidden Running Services screen and monitor background apps that drain battery and memory.

Android users often wonder why their phone’s battery dies faster than expected or why memory usage spikes during the night. A lesser‑known feature in recent Android versions lets you see exactly which services are running in the background, giving you a clear view of what’s draining resources.

How to Access the Hidden Running Services Menu

Open the Settings app, scroll to the bottom and tap on System. From there, select Advanced and then Developer options. If you haven’t enabled developer options yet, tap the Build number seven times to unlock them. Once in Developer options, find the Running services entry. Tapping it opens a list of all active services, including those running in the background.

What You’ll Find

The screen displays each service’s name, the app it belongs to, and the amount of memory it’s using. You can also see whether the service is bound to a foreground activity or running in the background. This information is invaluable for troubleshooting battery drain, identifying rogue apps, or simply understanding how your phone manages resources.

Why It Matters

Android’s operating system is designed to keep apps in the background to provide a seamless experience. However, some services can become stuck or run longer than necessary, leading to unnecessary battery consumption and memory usage. By reviewing the Running Services list, users can identify problematic apps and take action—such as disabling background activity, clearing cache, or uninstalling the app altogether.

Practical Tips

  • Check after updates: New OS or app updates can introduce new background services. Reviewing the list after an update can help spot unexpected changes.
  • Use the “Stop” button: For services that are not essential, you can tap the stop icon next to the service to halt it temporarily.
  • Combine with battery usage stats: Cross‑referencing the Running Services list with the Battery usage screen can pinpoint which services are the biggest culprits.
