Tesla Announces Cybercab Sales Amid Safety Probe

2026-10-22 · 3 min read
Tesla will begin selling its autonomous Cybercab by year’s end, even as regulators review its safety compliance.

Tesla has confirmed that it will start selling its Cybercab, a two‑seat autonomous vehicle, to customers by the end of the year. The announcement came during the company’s third‑quarter earnings call, where vice president of engineering Lars Moravy highlighted the vehicle’s unique design, which eliminates traditional controls such as a steering wheel.

Regulatory scrutiny continues

Despite the rollout plans, federal regulators are investigating whether the Cybercab meets vehicle safety standards. The investigation follows concerns about the vehicle’s lack of conventional controls and its autonomous operation in mixed traffic environments.

Market implications

The Cybercab’s launch marks a significant step for Tesla’s autonomous vehicle strategy, positioning the company to compete in the emerging shared‑mobility market. Analysts note that the vehicle’s compact size and two‑seat configuration could appeal to urban commuters and ride‑share operators looking for low‑cost, high‑efficiency options.
