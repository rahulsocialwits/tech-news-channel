Samsung Expands Sleep Apnea Detection to Galaxy Watch and Ring

2026-11-03 · 3 min read
Samsung rolls out upgraded sleep apnea detection to newer Galaxy Watch models and the Galaxy Ring, offering broader health monitoring for users.

Samsung has announced that its enhanced sleep apnea detection feature will now be available on a wider range of wearable devices. The update, which builds on the company’s existing health monitoring suite, is being rolled out to newer Galaxy Watch models and the Galaxy Ring. The move follows the company’s earlier rollout of the feature to the Galaxy Watch 6 and Galaxy Watch 5 series, and it signals Samsung’s intent to make sleep health tracking more accessible to a broader user base.

What the Update Covers

The upgraded detection system uses the same sensor technology that powers the original feature, but it incorporates refined algorithms that improve accuracy across a wider range of sleep patterns. Samsung notes that the new version can identify apnea events in users who previously fell outside the detection window of older models. The update is also designed to work seamlessly with Samsung Health, allowing users to view detailed reports and receive personalized recommendations.

Device Compatibility

According to Samsung, the feature will be available on the following devices:

  • Galaxy Watch 6 and Galaxy Watch 5 series
  • Galaxy Watch 4 and Galaxy Watch 3 series
  • Galaxy Ring

Older Galaxy Watch models that do not support the updated firmware will not receive the feature, but Samsung has stated that the company is exploring ways to extend support to legacy devices in future updates.

Why It Matters

Sleep apnea is a common condition that can lead to serious health complications if left untreated. By expanding the availability of detection across more wearables, Samsung is positioning itself as a leader in consumer health technology. The company’s focus on integrating health data with its broader ecosystem—such as Samsung Health and the Galaxy ecosystem—provides users with a more comprehensive view of their well‑being.

Next Steps for Users

Users who own compatible devices can enable the feature through the Samsung Health app. The update is being rolled out in phases, with the first wave expected to reach users in the United States and South Korea by the end of the month. Samsung has also announced that it will provide additional educational resources to help users understand the significance of sleep apnea detection and how to act on the insights generated by the device.

Industry Context

Samsung’s expansion of sleep health monitoring comes at a time when wearable manufacturers are increasingly focusing on medical‑grade health tracking. The company’s move aligns with broader industry trends that emphasize preventive health care and the integration of consumer devices with clinical data streams.
