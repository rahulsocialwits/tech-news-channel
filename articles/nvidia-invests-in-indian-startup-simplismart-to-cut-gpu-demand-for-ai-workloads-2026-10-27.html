Nvidia Invests in Indian Startup Simplismart to Cut GPU Demand for AI Workloads

2026-10-27 · 3 min read
Nvidia’s latest funding round backs Simplismart, a Bengaluru‑based company that claims to reduce the GPU count needed for running AI models, boosting efficiency for enterprises worldwide.

Nvidia announced a strategic investment in Simplismart, a Bengaluru‑based startup that specializes in optimizing AI workloads to run on fewer GPUs. The funding round, reported by TechCrunch, comes as the company’s annualized revenue run rate has surged 14‑fold in just six months, with more than half of its revenue coming from the United States.

How Simplismart’s Technology Works

Simplismart’s platform rewrites AI models to improve their computational efficiency. By restructuring the underlying code and leveraging advanced scheduling techniques, the system can execute the same inference tasks with a fraction of the GPU resources traditionally required. This approach not only lowers hardware costs but also reduces energy consumption, a key concern for large‑scale data centers.

Implications for Enterprises

For companies that rely on AI for everything from customer service to predictive analytics, the ability to run models on fewer GPUs translates into significant cost savings. The startup’s technology also promises faster deployment times, as smaller GPU clusters can be provisioned more quickly and flexibly.

Industry Context

Nvidia’s backing signals confidence in Simplismart’s solution amid a broader industry push to make AI more accessible and sustainable. As AI workloads continue to grow, tools that reduce hardware dependency are becoming increasingly valuable for enterprises looking to scale responsibly.
