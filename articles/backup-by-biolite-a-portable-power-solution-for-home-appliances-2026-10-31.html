Backup by BioLite: A Portable Power Solution for Home Appliances

2026-10-31 · 3 min read
BioLite’s Backup device offers a solar‑powered backup for refrigerators during grid outages, providing a practical, though pricey, solution for homeowners.

When the power grid goes down, keeping the fridge running can be a challenge. BioLite’s new Backup device promises to solve that problem by using a combination of solar panels and a battery pack to keep household appliances powered during outages. The product is marketed as a “cool idea” that can keep your fridge running when the grid is out, but the review notes that protecting your ice cream doesn’t come cheap.

How Backup Works

The system consists of a solar panel that charges a battery pack, which in turn supplies power to a small inverter. The inverter can be connected to a refrigerator or other low‑power appliances. The device is designed to be portable, allowing users to move it to different locations as needed. According to the review, the solar panel is capable of generating enough power to keep a standard refrigerator running for several hours during a power outage.

Performance and Practicality

In real‑world tests, the Backup device was able to keep a refrigerator running for up to eight hours on a single charge, depending on the fridge’s power draw and the amount of sunlight available. The review highlights that while the device performs well under ideal conditions, its effectiveness can be limited on cloudy days or in regions with less solar exposure. Users who live in areas with frequent power outages may find the device useful, but those in more stable power environments might not see the same benefit.

Cost and Value Proposition

The review points out that the Backup device is not inexpensive. The price tag reflects the cost of the solar panel, battery pack, and inverter, as well as the engineering required to make the system portable and reliable. While the device offers a tangible benefit for keeping a refrigerator running during outages, the cost may be prohibitive for some consumers. The review suggests that the product is best suited for households that experience frequent power disruptions or for those who want a backup solution for critical appliances.

Future Outlook

BioLite’s Backup device represents a growing trend toward portable, renewable energy solutions for home use. As solar technology becomes more affordable and battery storage improves, similar products may become more accessible to a wider audience. For now, the Backup device remains a niche solution for those willing to invest in a reliable backup power source for their refrigerator.
