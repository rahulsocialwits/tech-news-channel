Ring Unveils Indoor Drone and Roving Guard Cam for Home and Business Security

2026-11-11 · 3 min read
Amazon’s Ring introduces a new indoor drone that can investigate alarms and a wheeled robot that patrols commercial spaces after hours.

Amazon’s Ring has announced two new robotic security products that expand the company’s home‑and‑business protection lineup. The first, an indoor drone, is designed to fly into a house or apartment to visually confirm the trigger of a home alarm. The second, a wheeled security robot, can patrol a business location after hours, providing a mobile presence that can be monitored remotely.

Indoor Drone Features

The drone is equipped with a camera and sensors that allow it to navigate indoor spaces autonomously. When an alarm is triggered, the drone can be dispatched to capture footage of the incident, helping homeowners and security teams determine whether a false alarm or a real threat has occurred. The device is intended to reduce the need for manual checks and to provide instant visual evidence.

Roving Guard Cam for Businesses

The wheeled robot is tailored for commercial environments. It can patrol a business after hours, following pre‑set routes or responding to alerts. The robot’s camera and motion sensors enable it to detect unusual activity and report it to security personnel. This addition to Ring’s product line offers a cost‑effective way for small and medium businesses to enhance their after‑hours security coverage.

Implications for Security Tech

These new devices reflect a broader trend in the security industry toward autonomous, AI‑driven solutions. By integrating drones and robots into security systems, companies can provide faster incident response and more comprehensive coverage without the need for constant human oversight. The launch also positions Ring as a leader in the emerging market for robotic security solutions, potentially influencing competitors to develop similar products.
