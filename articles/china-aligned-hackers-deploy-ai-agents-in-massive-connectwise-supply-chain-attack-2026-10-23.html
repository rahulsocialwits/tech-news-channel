China‑Aligned Hackers Deploy AI Agents in Massive ConnectWise Supply‑Chain Attack

2026-10-23 · 3 min read
Microsoft reports China‑aligned Storm‑0940 used the ARTEX AI agent framework to breach over 2,250 Microsoft 365 tenants via ConnectWise Cloud Backup.

Microsoft has revealed that a China‑aligned threat actor, identified as Storm‑0940, leveraged the ARTEX AI agent framework to conduct a large‑scale supply‑chain attack against its ConnectWise Cloud Backup service. The operation targeted more than 2,250 Microsoft 365 tenants, with several attacks reaching privileged access in under three hours.

How the Attack Worked

The attackers used AI‑driven agents to automate the exploitation of vulnerabilities in the ConnectWise platform. By scripting repeated sign‑in attempts and scanning for misconfigurations, the agents were able to move laterally across tenant environments, escalating privileges and exfiltrating data.

Microsoft’s Response

Microsoft has issued a public advisory detailing the attack vectors and recommended mitigation steps. The company is working with affected tenants to patch vulnerabilities, strengthen authentication controls, and monitor for suspicious activity.

Implications for the Cloud Ecosystem

This incident underscores the growing threat of AI‑enhanced attacks in the cloud supply chain. Organizations that rely on third‑party services must now consider the security posture of their partners and the potential for automated, rapid exploitation.
