Bose QuietComfort Ultra 2nd‑Gen Headphones Drop to $369 in Latest Deal

2026-11-20 · 3 min read
Bose’s flagship noise‑canceling headphones hit a new low price of $369, offering lossless USB‑C audio and advanced comfort features for music lovers and commuters.

Bose has slashed the price of its QuietComfort Ultra 2nd‑Gen headphones to $369, a full $80 off the original retail price. The move comes as the brand pushes its flagship noise‑canceling line into a broader market segment, making premium audio more accessible to everyday listeners.

What’s New in the 2nd Generation?

The QuietComfort Ultra 2nd‑Gen builds on the original Ultra’s industry‑leading active noise cancellation (ANC) and comfort. Key updates include a redesigned earcup shape for better fit, a new “ComfortFit” ear pad that adapts to the shape of the wearer’s ears, and a battery that now lasts up to 30 hours on a single charge. The headphones also support lossless audio over USB‑C, allowing users to stream high‑resolution files directly from a computer or compatible device.

Why the Price Cut Matters

When Bose first launched the QuietComfort Ultra, the price point was a barrier for many consumers. By reducing the cost to $369, the company positions the headphones as a competitive alternative to other high‑end models such as Sony’s WH‑1000XM5 and Sennheiser’s Momentum 4. The discount is especially appealing for commuters, travelers, and audiophiles who want top‑tier ANC without the premium price tag.

How the Deal Works

The discount is available through select retailers and online platforms that partner with Bose. Customers can purchase the headphones directly from Bose’s website or through authorized resellers that offer the $369 price. The deal includes the standard two‑year warranty and free shipping within the United States.

Customer Reception and Market Impact

Early reviews from tech reviewers highlight the headphones’ superior sound quality, comfortable fit, and robust ANC performance. Many reviewers note that the lossless USB‑C support is a significant advantage for users who stream music from high‑resolution libraries. The price reduction is expected to increase sales volume and broaden Bose’s market share in the premium headphone segment.

Future Outlook

Bose’s strategy of offering high‑quality audio at a more approachable price point could signal a shift in the premium headphone market. As competitors continue to innovate, Bose’s focus on comfort, battery life, and lossless audio may set a new standard for what consumers expect from top‑tier headphones.
