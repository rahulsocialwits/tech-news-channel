iFixit Teardown Reveals Why iPhone Duo Screens Crack

2026-10-26 · 3 min read
iFixit’s detailed teardown of Apple’s foldable iPhone Duo shows how the complex hinge and display design can lead to cracked screens.

Apple’s ambitious foldable iPhone Duo has been a topic of fascination and frustration since its launch. The device’s unique design, featuring a flexible OLED panel that folds in half, has attracted attention from both tech enthusiasts and critics. A recent teardown by iFixit has shed light on the mechanical stresses that may be causing the reported cracked screens.

Inside the Foldable Design

The iPhone Duo’s display is a 7.3‑inch OLED panel that folds along a central hinge. According to iFixit, the hinge mechanism is a complex assembly of metal and polymer components that allow the screen to bend while maintaining a seamless user experience. The teardown revealed that the hinge’s internal structure includes a series of small, interlocking parts that must endure repeated flexing.

Potential Causes of Cracked Screens

iFixit identified several factors that could contribute to screen damage:

  • Hinge Stress: The repeated folding and unfolding of the display places significant mechanical stress on the hinge area. Over time, this can weaken the protective glass or polymer layers covering the screen.
  • Edge Sealing: The device’s edge sealing, which keeps the display protected during use, may not fully compensate for the flexing forces, leading to microfractures.
  • Manufacturing Variability: Minor inconsistencies in the assembly process could result in uneven stress distribution across the screen, making some units more prone to cracking.

What Users Should Know

Apple has not yet issued a widespread repair program for the iPhone Duo, but the company has acknowledged the issue in customer support forums. Users experiencing cracked screens are advised to contact Apple Support for potential repair options. Meanwhile, iFixit’s teardown provides a valuable resource for technicians and hobbyists looking to understand the inner workings of the foldable display.

Industry Implications

The findings underscore the challenges of bringing foldable technology to mainstream consumers. While the concept offers exciting possibilities for larger displays in a compact form factor, the mechanical complexity introduces new reliability concerns. Future iterations of foldable devices may need to address hinge durability and edge sealing to reduce the incidence of screen damage.
