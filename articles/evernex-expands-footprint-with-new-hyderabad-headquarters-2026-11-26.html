Evernex Expands Footprint with New Hyderabad Headquarters

2026-11-26 · 3 min read
Global IT services firm Evernex opens a new India hub to support the country’s growing data‑centre and enterprise market.

Evernex, a global IT services provider, has announced the opening of its India headquarters in Hyderabad. The move is aimed at bringing the company’s multi‑vendor maintenance and IT lifecycle management expertise closer to India’s expanding data‑centre and enterprise market.

Strategic Location

Hyderabad, known for its robust technology ecosystem, offers Evernex access to a large pool of skilled IT professionals and a growing demand for critical infrastructure services.

Focus on Critical IT Infrastructure

The new hub will support Evernex’s clients with services that include maintenance, upgrades, and lifecycle management across multiple vendors. This aligns with the company’s strategy to provide end‑to‑end solutions for complex IT environments.

Implications for the Market

By establishing a dedicated presence in India, Evernex positions itself to capture a larger share of the region’s IT services market, which is expected to grow as businesses invest in digital transformation and cloud migration.
