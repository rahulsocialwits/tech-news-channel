UPERFECT GR14BU: A 120 Hz 3K OLED Portable Monitor That Packs a Punch

2026-11-05 · 3 min read
The UPERFECT GR14BU delivers a 3K OLED panel, 120 Hz refresh rate, and touch capability in a compact portable monitor, offering a high‑performance display for mobile professionals and gamers.

The UPERFECT GR14BU has entered the portable monitor market with a bold combination of a 3K OLED panel, a 120 Hz refresh rate, and touch support. The device is designed to appeal to mobile professionals who need a high‑resolution, high‑refresh‑rate display on the go, as well as gamers who value fluid motion and accurate color reproduction.

Key Specs at a Glance

  • Resolution: 3K (2880 × 1620)
  • Panel type: OLED
  • Refresh rate: 120 Hz
  • Touch: Yes, capacitive multi‑touch
  • Size: 14‑inch diagonal
  • Weight: 1.2 kg (2.6 lb)

Why the 120 Hz Refresh Rate Matters

Most portable monitors on the market settle for 60 Hz, which can feel sluggish for fast‑moving content. The GR14BU’s 120 Hz refresh rate reduces motion blur and provides a smoother experience for video playback and gaming. Coupled with OLED’s deep blacks and wide color gamut, the monitor offers a visual experience that rivals many fixed‑desktop displays.

Touch Support for Versatility

Unlike many competitors that only offer a standard display, the GR14BU’s capacitive touch layer allows users to interact directly with content. This feature is especially useful for designers who need to annotate PDFs or for developers who want to test touch‑enabled applications on a larger screen.

Portability and Build

The 14‑inch form factor keeps the monitor lightweight, while the robust chassis protects it during travel. The device includes a USB‑C port that delivers power, video, and data, simplifying connectivity for laptops and tablets.

Price and Availability

UPERFECT has positioned the GR14BU at a competitive price point, aiming to attract budget‑conscious consumers who still demand high performance. The monitor is available for purchase through major electronics retailers and the company’s online store.
