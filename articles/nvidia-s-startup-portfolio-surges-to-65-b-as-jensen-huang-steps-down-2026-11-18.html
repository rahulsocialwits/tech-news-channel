Nvidia’s Startup Portfolio Surges to $65 B as Jensen Huang Steps Down

2026-11-18 · 3 min read
Nvidia’s private investments have nearly tripled, reaching $65 B, but the CEO’s exit raises questions about future deals.

Nvidia’s private‑investment arm has seen its portfolio value jump from roughly $22 B to $65 B in just nine months, a rise driven largely by founder and CEO Jensen Huang’s aggressive dealmaking. The company has invested in a range of AI and hardware startups, including the AI‑chip maker Cerebras and the robotics firm OpenAI Robotics, among others.

Huang’s Exit and Its Implications

Huang’s departure from the board has sparked speculation about the future of Nvidia’s venture strategy. While the company has not yet announced a replacement, industry observers note that the exit could slow the pace of new investments and shake investor confidence.

Portfolio Highlights

Key holdings include:

  • Cerebras Systems – a silicon‑based AI accelerator.
  • OpenAI Robotics – a spin‑off focused on autonomous systems.
  • QuantumX – a quantum‑computing startup.

Market Reaction

Shares of Nvidia’s venture fund have remained largely flat, but analysts warn that the lack of a clear leadership vision could affect future funding rounds. The company’s board is reportedly evaluating a new strategy to maintain momentum in the competitive AI startup ecosystem.
