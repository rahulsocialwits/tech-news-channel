Lava Unveils Spark 2 Pro 5G, Android 16 Ready for India

2026-09-29 · 3 min read
Lava launches its new Spark 2 Pro 5G smartphone, shipping with Android 16 and two years of security updates.

Lava has announced the Spark 2 Pro 5G, a new entry‑level smartphone aimed at the Indian market. The device ships with Android 16 out of the box and is slated to receive one full Android OS upgrade and two years of security updates, giving users a longer‑lasting software experience.

Key Features

  • 5G connectivity for faster mobile data
  • Android 16 with the latest security patches
  • Two‑year security update window

Market Position

The Spark 2 Pro 5G is positioned as a budget‑friendly option that still offers modern connectivity and software support. By committing to a two‑year security update cycle, Lava aims to differentiate itself in a market where many low‑cost devices lag behind in software maintenance.
