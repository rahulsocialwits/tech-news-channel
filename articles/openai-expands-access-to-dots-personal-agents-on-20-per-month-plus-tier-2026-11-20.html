OpenAI Expands Access to Dots Personal Agents on $20‑Per‑Month Plus Tier

2026-11-20 · 3 min read
OpenAI is testing its proactive AI agents, Dots, for Plus subscribers, allowing users to run tasks without supervision.

OpenAI is rolling out a new feature that lets users on its $20‑per‑month Plus plan run Dots, the company’s proactive AI agents, without any human oversight. The move expands the reach of the agents beyond the limited beta that was previously available only to a small group of early adopters.

What Dots Can Do

Dots are designed to act autonomously on behalf of a user, handling tasks such as scheduling, email management, and data retrieval. The agents can learn from a user’s habits and preferences, then execute actions without needing step‑by‑step instructions.

Why the Expansion Matters

By making Dots available to the broader Plus subscriber base, OpenAI is testing the viability of a subscription‑based model for AI agents. The company hopes to gauge how many users will adopt the feature and how it will affect overall engagement with the platform.

Next Steps for Users

Subscribers who want to try Dots can enable the feature in the settings of the OpenAI app. The agents will run in the background, and users can review or override actions at any time. OpenAI has stated that it will continue to refine the agents’ safety protocols as usage grows.
