ChatGPT’s New Chrome Extension Lets You Compare Open Tabs in Real Time

2026-10-16 · 3 min read
OpenAI’s updated ChatGPT browser extension now scans your active Chrome tabs, enabling instant side‑by‑side comparisons and streamlined research workflows.

OpenAI has rolled out a significant update to its ChatGPT browser extension for Google Chrome, giving users the ability to scan and compare the content of all open tabs. The new feature, announced on October 16, 2026, allows the AI to pull text from each tab and present a side‑by‑side comparison, making it easier for researchers, students, and professionals to synthesize information without switching windows.

How the Feature Works

When the extension is activated, it scans every open tab for readable text. The AI then aggregates the extracted content and displays it in a comparison pane. Users can highlight sections, ask follow‑up questions, or request summaries directly from the pane. The interface is designed to keep the browsing experience uninterrupted, with the comparison view appearing as an overlay that can be collapsed or expanded as needed.

Benefits for Knowledge Workers

For anyone who relies on multiple sources—journalists, analysts, or students—the ability to compare documents side by side can cut research time dramatically. Instead of manually copying and pasting snippets into a word processor, the extension lets users ask the AI to identify similarities, contradictions, or gaps across the open tabs. This can be especially useful for fact‑checking, literature reviews, or compiling briefing documents.

Privacy and Permissions

OpenAI has emphasized that the extension only accesses text that is visible in the browser and does not transmit any personal data to external servers. Users can review the permissions requested during installation, and the extension can be disabled or uninstalled at any time. The company also notes that the feature is opt‑in; it does not automatically scan tabs unless the user activates the comparison mode.

Broader Context in AI‑Powered Browsing

This update follows a trend of integrating AI assistants directly into web browsers. Similar tools have appeared from other vendors, but OpenAI’s focus on real‑time comparison sets it apart. By leveraging the same language model that powers ChatGPT, the extension can provide contextual insights, such as summarizing key points or highlighting conflicting claims across sources.

What’s Next?

OpenAI has hinted at future enhancements, including the ability to pull in data from PDFs or other document types opened in the browser, and to allow collaborative editing of the comparison pane. The company also plans to refine the user interface based on feedback from early adopters, aiming to make the tool as intuitive as possible for non‑technical users.

For now, the new comparison feature is available to all users of the Chrome extension, and it represents a step toward more efficient, AI‑augmented research workflows.
