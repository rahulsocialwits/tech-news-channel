Palo Alto Networks Boosts Forecast with $4.5 B Applied Compute Deal

2026-11-19 · 3 min read
Palo Alto Networks raised its annual outlook after signing a $4.5 billion agreement with Applied Compute to expand its cloud security portfolio.

Palo Alto Networks announced a significant upgrade to its 2026 financial outlook after securing a $4.5 billion contract with Applied Compute. The agreement is aimed at bolstering the company’s cloud‑security capabilities and expanding its presence in the data‑center market.

Deal Details

The partnership will see Applied Compute’s advanced security solutions integrated into Palo Alto’s portfolio, allowing customers to protect workloads across public, private, and hybrid clouds. The deal is structured as a combination of upfront payments and performance‑based milestones.

Impact on Forecast

With the new contract, Palo Alto Networks raised its annual revenue forecast, citing the growing demand for cloud‑centric security services. The company highlighted that the partnership will help it capture a larger share of the expanding market for secure cloud infrastructure.

Industry Context

Security firms are increasingly focusing on cloud‑native solutions as enterprises migrate workloads to the cloud. Palo Alto’s move aligns with broader industry trends toward integrated, AI‑driven security platforms that can operate across diverse cloud environments.
