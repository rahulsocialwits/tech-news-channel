Google Home Speaker Gets First Discount Since Launch

2026-11-03 · 3 min read
Google offers a price cut on its Home Speaker, marking the first discount since the product’s June release.

Google has announced a price reduction on its Home Speaker, the first discount offered since the device launched in June. The move comes as part of a broader strategy to boost sales of the company’s smart‑home lineup, which also includes the Pixel phone series. The discount applies to the standard Home Speaker model and is available for a limited time through Google’s online store.

What the discount means for consumers

For buyers, the new price brings the Home Speaker closer to the mid‑range smart speaker market, where competitors such as Amazon Echo and Sonos offer similar features at comparable prices. The reduction could make the device more appealing to households looking to expand their smart‑home ecosystem without a significant price hike.

How Google is positioning its smart‑home strategy

Google’s decision to discount the Home Speaker follows a series of promotional offers for its Pixel phones, which began last week. By aligning the pricing of its smart‑home hardware with its mobile devices, Google aims to create a cohesive ecosystem that encourages users to adopt multiple products from the same brand.
