Meta’s Muse AI Now Runs on Wear OS, Bringing Smart Assistant to Your Wrist

2026-11-11 · 3 min read
Meta launches Muse AI agent for Wear OS, enabling on-device smart assistant features that still rely on the phone for heavy processing.

Meta has expanded its Muse AI assistant beyond the phone, announcing that the agent can now run on Wear OS smartwatches. The move brings the AI experience directly to the wrist, allowing users to ask questions, set reminders, and receive contextual information without opening their phone.

How Muse AI Works on Wear OS

The core of Muse AI remains on the smartphone, where the heavy lifting—natural language understanding, data retrieval, and response generation—takes place. The smartwatch acts as a lightweight interface, displaying the assistant’s replies and handling voice input. When a user speaks to the watch, the audio is sent to the phone, processed by the AI model, and the resulting text or voice reply is streamed back to the watch for display.

Key Features for Wearables

  • Hands‑free Interaction: Users can ask for weather updates, calendar events, or quick facts while on the move.
  • Contextual Reminders: Muse can suggest reminders based on location or time, leveraging the watch’s sensors.
  • Battery‑Efficient Design: By offloading computation to the phone, the watch conserves power, extending battery life.

Implications for the Wearable Ecosystem

Meta’s decision to support Wear OS positions the company as a serious contender in the smartwatch space, traditionally dominated by Apple and Google. The integration demonstrates that AI assistants can be effectively distributed across devices, offering a seamless experience while keeping the bulk of processing on more capable hardware.

Future Outlook

While the current implementation relies on the phone for processing, Meta has hinted at future plans to bring more AI functionality directly to the watch. This could include on-device language models and offline data access, further reducing latency and improving privacy.
