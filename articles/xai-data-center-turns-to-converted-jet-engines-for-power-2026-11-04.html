xAI Data Center Turns to Converted Jet Engines for Power

2026-11-04 · 3 min read
Elon Musk’s xAI data center in Mississippi is using eight repurposed jet engine turbines to meet its energy needs, a move that raises both efficiency and environmental concerns.

Elon Musk’s artificial‑intelligence venture, xAI, has announced that its new Mississippi data center will be powered by eight converted jet engine turbines. The decision follows a broader industry trend of seeking alternative energy sources for large‑scale computing facilities.

Why Jet Engines?

Jet engines are known for their high power output and compact design, making them attractive for data‑center applications where space and energy density are critical. By repurposing existing turbines, xAI aims to reduce the upfront cost of building a dedicated power plant while still delivering the megawatt‑scale capacity required for AI workloads.

Environmental and Health Concerns

While the turbines promise a high‑capacity power supply, they also emit carbon monoxide and volatile organic compounds. Local regulators have granted xAI a 25‑percent higher pollutant limit, but environmental groups have raised alarms about the potential health impacts on nearby communities.

Industry Implications

The move signals a growing willingness among tech companies to experiment with unconventional power solutions. If successful, it could set a precedent for other data‑center operators looking to balance cost, capacity, and sustainability.
