$11 Desk‑Mounted Power Switch Lets Gamers Boot PCs Without Bending

2026-11-27 · 3 min read
A budget external power switch for under‑desk PCs offers a convenient, cable‑free way to turn rigs on and off, appealing to gamers and office users alike.

Gamers and office workers who keep their PCs tucked under desks now have a cheap, plug‑in solution that eliminates the need to bend over to flip a power button. The $11 desk‑mounted switch, available from Tom’s Hardware, plugs into a standard outlet and connects to the PC via a two‑meter cable, allowing users to turn their machines on or off from a convenient surface.

Key Features

  • Compact design – the switch fits easily on a desk or monitor stand.
  • Two‑meter cable – provides flexibility for placement without cluttering the workspace.
  • RGB compatibility – works with RGB‑enabled keyboards and mice, making it a good fit for gaming rigs.

Why It Matters

For many, the traditional power button on a tower is out of reach, especially when the case is positioned under a desk or in a cramped office. The external switch offers a simple, low‑cost fix that can improve ergonomics and reduce wear on the PC’s internal power button.

Availability

The switch is priced at $11 and can be purchased through the Tom’s Hardware website. It is marketed as a must‑buy for anyone with a gaming PC under their desk, and reviewers note that it also serves as a thoughtful gift for tech enthusiasts.
