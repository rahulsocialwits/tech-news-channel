Pixel 11 Buyers Face Black Friday Discount Glitch

2026-11-20 · 3 min read
Some Pixel 11 shoppers are losing access to the $225 Black Friday discount due to a Play Pass subscription issue.

Google’s Pixel 11, which launched with a $225 Black Friday discount, is now experiencing a glitch that is preventing some buyers from accessing the offer. The problem appears to be linked to the Play Pass subscription that was supposed to unlock the discount.

What’s Happening?

According to Android Authority, a $5 Play Pass subscription may not automatically grant the extra Pixel discount. Users who purchased the phone during the Black Friday sale have reported that the discount code is not being applied at checkout, leaving them to pay the full price.

How It Affects Buyers

Customers who relied on the advertised discount are now facing higher costs. The issue has sparked frustration among early adopters and raised questions about the reliability of promotional offers tied to subscription services.

Google’s Response

Google has not yet issued an official statement regarding the glitch. However, the company’s support forums show that the issue is being investigated, and users are advised to contact customer service for assistance.

What to Do If You’re Affected

  • Check your Play Pass subscription status to ensure it is active.
  • Verify that the discount code is correctly entered during checkout.
  • Contact Google Support for a refund or discount adjustment if the code does not apply.
