Thermaltake Unveils Budget RTX 5060 Ti Gaming Desktops at $1,299

2026-11-28 · 3 min read
Thermaltake offers new prebuilt gaming PCs featuring Nvidia RTX 5060 Ti graphics, 1TB storage, and an Intel AI software bundle for holiday shoppers.

Thermaltake has launched two new budget gaming desktops that bring Nvidia’s RTX 5060 Ti graphics card into the mainstream price range. The models, priced at $1,299, are aimed at gamers who want solid performance without breaking the bank.

Key Specs

  • Graphics: Nvidia RTX 5060 Ti 8GB
  • Storage: 1TB SSD
  • Memory: Choice of DDR4 or DDR5 configurations
  • Cooling: RGB‑lit cooling system
  • Software: Intel AI software bundle included

Holiday Bundle

Both desktops come with a holiday bundle that includes two popular PC games, adding extra value for buyers during the Black Friday season.

Why It Matters

The RTX 5060 Ti offers a good balance of price and performance, making it a compelling option for gamers who want to play the latest titles at 1080p or 1440p. Thermaltake’s inclusion of an Intel AI software bundle also provides users with tools for optimizing game performance and system stability.

With the holiday shopping window still open, Thermaltake’s $1,299 desktops could become a popular choice for budget‑conscious gamers looking to upgrade their rigs.
