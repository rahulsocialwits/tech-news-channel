Newegg Unveils $1,250 Gaming PC Combo with 32GB DDR5 and Ryzen 7 9800X3D

2026-11-13 · 3 min read
Newegg’s latest bundle offers a high‑end gaming rig featuring 32GB of Corsair Vengeance DDR5‑6000, a Ryzen 7 9800X3D CPU, MSI X870E Carbon Wifi motherboard and a free Rosewill Frostmax 360mm AIO for $1,250, saving $119 over retail.

Newegg has rolled out a new gaming PC bundle that delivers premium performance at a competitive price. The package, priced at $1,250, includes a Ryzen 7 9800X3D processor, 32GB of Corsair Vengeance DDR5‑6000 memory, an MSI X870E Carbon Wifi motherboard, and a complimentary Rosewill Frostmax 360mm AIO cooler. The deal represents a $119 savings compared to the combined retail cost of the individual components.

Component Highlights

The Ryzen 7 9800X3D is a 3‑core, 8‑thread CPU that incorporates 3D V‑Cache technology, boosting gaming performance by up to 20% in certain titles. Paired with 32GB of DDR5‑6000 RAM, the system is well‑positioned for both current and upcoming AAA games that demand high memory bandwidth.

The MSI X870E Carbon Wifi motherboard offers robust power delivery, integrated Wi‑Fi 6E, and support for PCIe 4.0, ensuring future‑proof connectivity. The included Rosewill Frostmax 360mm AIO provides efficient cooling, helping maintain low temperatures during extended gaming sessions.

Pricing and Availability

Newegg’s bundle is available for a limited time and is marketed as a “high‑end AM5 gaming combo.” The $119 discount is calculated by subtracting the bundle price from the sum of the retail prices of each component. Shoppers can purchase the kit directly from Newegg’s website, where the bundle is listed under the PC Components category.

Why It Matters

For gamers looking to build a powerful system without the hassle of sourcing each part individually, Newegg’s bundle offers a convenient and cost‑effective solution. The inclusion of a high‑end CPU, ample DDR5 memory, and a premium motherboard addresses the key performance bottlenecks that often limit gaming rigs. Additionally, the free AIO cooler reduces the upfront cost of a reliable cooling solution.

Conclusion

Newegg’s $1,250 gaming PC combo demonstrates how bundled configurations can provide significant savings while delivering top‑tier performance. With the Ryzen 7 9800X3D’s 3D V‑Cache and 32GB of DDR5‑6000 memory, the kit is poised to handle demanding games and multitasking workloads alike.
