Nvidia’s New Frame Generation Preset Promises Speed, But Comes With Trade‑Offs

2026-11-22 · 3 min read
Nvidia’s latest frame generation preset offers faster performance, yet users may notice reduced visual fidelity and increased GPU load.

Nvidia has rolled out a new frame generation preset that aims to deliver smoother gameplay at a lower performance cost. The update, announced on November 22, 2026, is part of the company’s ongoing effort to make its RTX technology more accessible to a broader range of hardware.

What the Preset Does

The preset leverages the same deep‑learning super‑resolution engine that powers Nvidia’s existing frame generation feature, but it applies a lighter model. According to the company, the result is a noticeable reduction in the computational load required to generate intermediate frames. Early benchmarks show a 15‑20% increase in frame rates on mid‑range GPUs when the preset is enabled.

Potential Drawbacks

While the speed gains are clear, reviewers have pointed out that the lighter model can introduce visual artifacts. In some scenes, the generated frames exhibit a slight blurring effect and occasional ghosting, especially during rapid camera movements or complex lighting changes. Users who prioritize visual fidelity may find the trade‑off less appealing.

How to Enable the Preset

To activate the new preset, players need to update their Nvidia drivers to the latest version and then select the “Fast” option in the Frame Generation settings within the GeForce Experience overlay. The feature is currently available for games that support RTX and frame generation, such as Cyberpunk 2077 and Call of Duty: Modern Warfare II.

Industry Reaction

Industry analysts view the update as a strategic move to broaden the appeal of Nvidia’s AI‑driven graphics enhancements. By offering a performance‑first option, the company hopes to attract gamers who are constrained by older or less powerful GPUs.
