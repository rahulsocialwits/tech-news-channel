What Is DeepSeek? Understanding the AI Model and Company

September 26, 2026 · 6 min read
DeepSeek is an AI company known for developing large language models and research focused on efficient AI development. Its models have become part of the broader conversation around open and accessible AI.

What is DeepSeek?

DeepSeek is an artificial intelligence company that develops foundation models and related research. The name can refer both to the organization and to its family of AI models, depending on the context.

Why did DeepSeek attract attention?

DeepSeek drew significant interest because its research and model releases contributed to discussions about the cost and efficiency of training and running advanced AI systems. Its work also became part of a wider debate over how capable AI models can be developed and distributed.

What can its models do?

Like other modern language models, DeepSeek models can be used for tasks such as text generation, question answering, summarization, reasoning-oriented prompts and software development. Actual capabilities vary by model, version, deployment method and available tools.

Open models and accessibility

One important part of the DeepSeek story is the availability of model information and releases that developers can study, use or integrate under their respective licenses. “Open” in AI can describe different levels of access, so users should check the specific license and release terms for each model.

What should users keep in mind?

AI models can produce incorrect or incomplete information, and performance can vary by task. Organizations evaluating any model should consider accuracy, privacy, security, licensing, infrastructure requirements and the sensitivity of the information being processed.

The bottom line

DeepSeek is significant because its models and research have added another major participant to the fast-moving AI landscape. Its work is useful to understand alongside the broader technical and business changes shaping generative AI.
