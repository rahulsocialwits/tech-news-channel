Samsung 990 Pro 4TB SSD Slashes Price, Making High‑Capacity Storage More Accessible

2026-10-22 · 3 min read
The 4TB Samsung 990 Pro M.2 SSD drops 27%, hitting 20 cents per gigabyte, easing the cost of high‑capacity PCIe Gen 4 storage for gamers and professionals.

Samsung’s flagship 4TB 990 Pro M.2 SSD has seen a significant price reduction, falling 27% to a new retail level of roughly 20 cents per gigabyte. The move brings the high‑capacity PCIe Gen 4 drive closer to mainstream affordability, especially for gamers and content creators who demand large, fast storage pools.

What the 990 Pro Offers

The 990 Pro is built on Samsung’s latest 8‑nm process and features a hybrid memory architecture that blends high‑speed NVMe 4.0 with a large DRAM buffer. In benchmark tests, the drive delivers sequential read speeds up to 7,000 MB/s and write speeds around 5,000 MB/s, making it one of the fastest consumer SSDs on the market.

Impact of the Price Cut

With the price now at about 20 cents per gigabyte, the 4TB model becomes a more viable option for users who previously opted for smaller capacities or slower drives. The reduction also aligns the 990 Pro with competing PCIe Gen 4 offerings that have been priced lower for several months.

Why the Drop Matters

High‑capacity SSDs have traditionally been a niche product, largely reserved for enterprise or enthusiast builds. The price cut could accelerate adoption in mid‑tier gaming rigs, professional workstations, and even consumer laptops that support M.2 NVMe slots. It also pressures other manufacturers to revisit their pricing strategies for large‑capacity drives.

Future Outlook

Samsung’s decision to lower the price of the 990 Pro may signal a broader shift toward making premium storage more accessible. As game developers and software vendors continue to push for larger asset sizes, the demand for affordable high‑capacity SSDs is likely to grow.
