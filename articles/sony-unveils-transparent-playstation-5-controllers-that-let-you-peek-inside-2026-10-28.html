Sony Unveils Transparent PlayStation 5 Controllers That Let You Peek Inside

2026-10-28 · 3 min read
Sony’s new see‑through PS5 controllers showcase the console’s internal hardware, offering a fresh aesthetic and a glimpse into gaming tech.

Sony has introduced a new line of PlayStation 5 controllers that are literally transparent. The company’s latest accessories, released this week, feature a clear, glass‑like shell that lets users see the internal circuitry and components of the console’s hardware. The move is part of Sony’s broader strategy to blend form and function, giving gamers a unique visual experience while still delivering the same performance as the standard controller.

Design and Build

The controllers are made from a durable, see‑through material that resembles tempered glass. The design showcases the console’s internal architecture, including the processor, memory modules, and power supply. Sony has also added a protective cover that can be swapped out for a more traditional opaque look, allowing users to choose between a high‑tech aesthetic and a classic gaming feel.

Functionality Remains Unchanged

Despite the radical exterior, the controllers maintain all the features of the standard PS5 DualSense. They support haptic feedback, adaptive triggers, and the same ergonomic shape that gamers have come to expect. Sony has confirmed that the transparent shell does not interfere with the controller’s performance or battery life.

Why Transparent?

According to Sony, the transparent design is intended to celebrate the engineering behind the PS5. By exposing the hardware, the company hopes to spark curiosity and appreciation for the technology that powers modern gaming. The move also aligns with a growing trend of “tech‑in‑plain‑sight” products that appeal to tech enthusiasts who enjoy seeing how devices work.

Availability and Pricing

The new controllers are available for purchase on Sony’s official website and select retailers. They are priced at the same level as the standard DualSense, making them an attractive option for gamers who want a unique look without paying a premium. Sony has not announced any special bundles or limited editions, but the company has hinted at future releases that could feature additional colors or materials.

Industry Reaction

Early reviews from tech outlets have praised the design for its boldness and the way it showcases the console’s internals. Some reviewers noted that the transparent shell could make the controller more susceptible to fingerprints and dust, but overall the consensus is that the aesthetic upgrade is a welcome addition to the PS5 ecosystem.

Future Implications

While the transparent controllers are a cosmetic change, they signal Sony’s willingness to experiment with hardware design. If the concept proves popular, we may see more gaming peripherals that blend transparency with functionality, potentially influencing how manufacturers approach product design in the future.
