AMD GPUs Get Full DLSS 5 Experience Thanks to Modder’s Project

2026-10-18 · 3 min read
A community developer has ported Nvidia’s DLSS 5 suite to AMD Radeon cards, enabling RDNA2-4 GPUs to run all DLSS features.

In a surprising turn of events, a single modder has managed to bring the full suite of Nvidia’s DLSS 5 features to three generations of AMD Radeon graphics cards. The project, which ports all DLSS capabilities to RDNA2 through RDNA4 GPUs, means that AMD users can now enjoy the same level of image upscaling and frame generation that has been a hallmark of Nvidia’s hardware.

How the Mod Works

The developer’s initiative involves porting the DLSS 5 codebase—originally designed for Nvidia’s proprietary architecture—into a format compatible with AMD’s GPU architecture. By doing so, the mod enables RDNA2-4 cards to run the full range of DLSS features, including the latest AI-driven upscaling and frame generation techniques.

Impact on Gaming Performance

Gamers who rely on AMD GPUs can now experience smoother frame rates and higher visual fidelity without needing to upgrade to Nvidia hardware. The mod effectively levels the playing field, allowing players on AMD cards to benefit from the same performance boosts that were previously exclusive to Nvidia’s lineup.

Community Reception

While the mod has been praised for its technical achievement, it also raises questions about licensing and the boundaries of community-driven software modifications. Nvidia has not publicly commented on the project, and it remains unclear whether the mod will be officially supported or integrated into future GPU drivers.

Future Outlook

If the mod gains widespread adoption, it could prompt AMD to consider integrating DLSS-like features directly into its driver stack. Until then, the community-driven solution stands as a testament to the power of open-source collaboration in expanding the capabilities of existing hardware.
