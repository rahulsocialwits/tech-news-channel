Nothing Ear Earbuds Drop to $69, Offering Premium Features for a Bargain

2026-10-16 · 3 min read
Nothing Ear wireless earbuds now retail at $69, a 37% discount, with adaptive ANC, dual-device pairing and IP54 protection.

Nothing’s flagship wireless earbuds, the Nothing Ear, have been slashed to a new low price of $69, a 37% discount from the original retail price. The move comes as the brand looks to broaden its appeal amid a crowded mid‑tier earbuds market.

What the discount includes

The $69 price point still delivers the earbuds’ signature features: adaptive active noise cancellation, dual‑device pairing that lets users switch between a phone and a laptop with a tap, and an IP54 rating that protects against dust and light splashes. The earbuds also retain the sleek, translucent design that has become Nothing’s trademark.

Why the price cut matters

By offering premium functionality at a lower cost, Nothing positions itself against competitors like Apple’s AirPods and Samsung’s Galaxy Buds, which typically command higher price tags for similar feature sets. The discount could attract budget‑conscious consumers who want advanced noise cancellation without breaking the bank.

Market context

The earbuds market has seen a surge in price reductions as manufacturers vie for market share. Nothing’s decision to cut the price by $40 aligns with a broader trend of making high‑end audio technology more accessible.
