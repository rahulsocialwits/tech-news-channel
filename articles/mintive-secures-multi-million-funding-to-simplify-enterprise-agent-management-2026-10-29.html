Mintive Secures Multi‑Million Funding to Simplify Enterprise Agent Management

2026-10-29 · 3 min read
Mintive, a startup founded by former Google Brain engineers, raises capital from Cowboy Ventures, Coatue and AI luminaries Jeff Dean and Andrej Karpathy to help businesses control AI agents.

Two former Google Brain engineers have turned their decade‑long experience in artificial intelligence into a new venture that promises to make it easier for enterprises to manage the growing number of autonomous agents deployed across their IT environments. Mintive, the company behind the platform, announced a funding round that brought in a total of $30 million, led by Cowboy Ventures and Coatue, with participation from high‑profile angels Jeff Dean and Andrej Karpathy.

Why Mintive Matters

As organizations increasingly rely on AI agents to automate tasks, from data extraction to customer support, the complexity of monitoring and controlling these agents has become a significant operational challenge. Mintive’s platform claims to provide a unified interface that allows IT teams to set policies, track agent behavior, and enforce compliance without needing deep AI expertise.

Funding Details

The round was announced on Thursday, October 29, 2026, and represents a significant vote of confidence from investors who see a clear market need for agent governance tools. Cowboy Ventures, known for backing early‑stage tech companies, and Coatue, a large hedge fund with a growing technology portfolio, both led the investment. The participation of Jeff Dean, former head of Google AI, and Andrej Karpathy, former director of AI at Tesla, signals strong industry endorsement.

Founder Background

Mintive was founded by two engineers who have spent years working on AI at Google Brain and Coursera. Their experience spans building large‑scale machine learning systems and developing educational platforms that leverage AI to personalize learning. This blend of technical depth and product‑market fit positions Mintive to address the nuanced needs of enterprise IT departments.

Product Vision

According to the company’s announcement, the platform will offer real‑time visibility into agent activities, automated policy enforcement, and integration with existing security and compliance frameworks. By abstracting the complexity of AI agent management, Mintive aims to reduce the risk of unintended behavior and accelerate the adoption of AI across business processes.

Market Context

The rise of AI agents has been accompanied by growing concerns about governance, security, and accountability. With the increasing number of autonomous systems operating in production, enterprises are looking for solutions that can provide transparency and control. Mintive’s entry into this space aligns with broader industry trends toward AI governance and responsible AI deployment.

Next Steps

Mintive plans to use the new capital to expand its engineering team, enhance its platform’s feature set, and pursue strategic partnerships with major cloud providers. The company also intends to open a beta program for early adopters to validate its claims and refine its product roadmap.

Conclusion

Mintive’s funding round marks a notable moment in the AI ecosystem, highlighting the growing importance of tools that help enterprises manage the complexity of autonomous agents. With backing from prominent investors and industry veterans, the startup is poised to become a key player in the emerging field of AI agent governance.
