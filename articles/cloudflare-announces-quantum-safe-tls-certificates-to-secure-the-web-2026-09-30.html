Cloudflare Announces Quantum‑Safe TLS Certificates to Secure the Web

2026-09-30 · 3 min read
Cloudflare will issue quantum‑safe TLS certificates as part of a major overhaul of website authentication, strengthening security against future quantum attacks.

Cloudflare has unveiled plans to launch quantum‑safe TLS certificates, a move that will significantly enhance the security of website authentication. The new certificates will be part of a broader overhaul of the company’s authentication ecosystem, aiming to protect sites from the emerging threat posed by quantum computing.

Why Quantum‑Safe Matters

Traditional TLS certificates rely on cryptographic algorithms that could be broken by sufficiently powerful quantum computers. By adopting quantum‑safe algorithms, Cloudflare intends to future‑proof its security infrastructure and give website operators a robust defense against potential quantum attacks.

Implementation and Rollout

Cloudflare’s announcement details that the quantum‑safe certificates will be integrated into its existing certificate issuance workflow. While the exact timeline for full deployment has not been disclosed, the company emphasized that the new certificates will be available to customers as part of its ongoing commitment to secure web infrastructure.

Industry Impact

The introduction of quantum‑safe TLS certificates is expected to set a new standard for web security. By proactively addressing the quantum threat, Cloudflare is positioning itself as a leader in the next generation of internet protection, encouraging other providers to adopt similar measures.
