Anthropic Shifts Focus as Workforce Grows Older and Meetings Expand

2026-10-18 · 3 min read
Anthropic reports a maturing team and increased meeting time, reflecting a shift in its research and product development priorities.

Anthropic, the AI research company founded by former OpenAI employees, has announced that its workforce has become less junior and that employees are spending more time in meetings. The change, revealed in a recent company statement, signals a shift in the organization’s focus as it moves beyond early-stage research to more mature product development and policy work.

From Early Research to Product Maturity

In the past year, Anthropic’s team composition has evolved. The company now has a higher proportion of mid‑career and senior staff, indicating a move toward more complex projects that require deeper expertise. This trend aligns with the company’s goal of building safer and more reliable AI systems for broader deployment.

Meeting Time Increases

Anthropic’s employees are also spending more time in meetings. While the exact duration is not disclosed, the company notes that the increase reflects a greater emphasis on coordination, cross‑functional collaboration, and stakeholder engagement. The shift suggests that the organization is prioritizing alignment across its growing product and policy teams.

Implications for the AI Landscape

Anthropic’s workforce changes mirror a broader industry pattern where AI firms are scaling up from research labs to full‑stack product companies. As teams mature, the need for structured communication and governance grows, often leading to longer meetings and more formal processes.
