Lenovo Googlebook Duet 13 Leak Reveals Design Ahead of January Launch

2026-11-16 · 3 min read
A leaked image of the Lenovo Googlebook Duet 13 shows a sleek detachable design that could hit shelves in January.

Leaked photos of the Lenovo Googlebook Duet 13 have surfaced, offering a first look at the detachable Chromebook that Google teased last month. The images show a slim 13‑inch slate that can be separated from a detachable keyboard, echoing the design language of Google’s earlier Duet models.

Design Details

The photos reveal a lightweight chassis with a matte finish and a 360‑degree hinge that allows the tablet to flip into a laptop mode. The detachable keyboard features a backlit layout and a built‑in trackpad, while the tablet itself boasts a 13.3‑inch display with a 120Hz refresh rate.

Potential Features

While the leak does not disclose specifications, industry analysts expect the Duet 13 to run Chrome OS with optional Android app support. Lenovo has previously marketed its Duet line as a budget‑friendly alternative to high‑end laptops, so the new model may target students and casual users.

Release Timeline

Google’s official announcement last month hinted that detachable Googlebooks would launch in early 2027. The leaked images suggest that the Duet 13 could follow that schedule, potentially hitting the market in January 2027.
