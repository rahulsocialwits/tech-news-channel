Apple Drops 13‑inch MacBook Air Price to $1,349, Its Lowest Since June

2026-11-06 · 3 min read
Apple’s 13‑inch MacBook Air with M5, 16GB RAM and 1TB SSD is now available for $1,349 on Amazon, a $250 discount from its previous price.

Apple has slashed the price of its 13‑inch MacBook Air, making the M5‑powered model available for $1,349 on Amazon. The deal marks the lowest price for the laptop since June, when it was listed at $1,599.

Specs and Savings

The discounted MacBook Air comes with the new M5 chip, 16GB of RAM, and a 1TB SSD. The $250 price cut brings the unit down to $1,349, a 15.6% reduction from the previous $1,599 price point.

Market Impact

Apple’s decision to lower the price of the MacBook Air could help the company regain momentum in the mid‑range laptop market, where competition from Windows‑based devices has intensified. The move also aligns with the broader trend of manufacturers offering deeper discounts to attract cost‑conscious consumers.
