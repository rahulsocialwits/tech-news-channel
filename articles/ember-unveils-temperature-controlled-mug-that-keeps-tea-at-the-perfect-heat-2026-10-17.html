Ember Unveils Temperature‑Controlled Mug That Keeps Tea at the Perfect Heat

2026-10-17 · 3 min read
Ember’s latest smart mug lets users lock in their drink’s temperature without opening an app, making nightly tea rituals smoother and more convenient.

Ember, the company that has been pioneering temperature‑controlled drinkware for years, announced a new version of its flagship mug that eliminates the need to open a companion app to adjust the beverage’s heat. The update, unveiled on October 17, 2026, promises a more seamless experience for users who have grown accustomed to the brand’s precise temperature‑keeping technology.

What’s New About the Mug?

The core feature that sets the new mug apart is its ability to maintain a set temperature automatically. Users can set their desired heat level once, and the mug will keep the drink at that temperature for up to 12 hours. This is a departure from earlier models, which required a brief interaction with the Ember app to lock in the temperature. The new design also incorporates a slimmer, more ergonomic body that fits comfortably in most mugs and cups, making it easier to carry around the house or office.

Why the Change Matters

According to Ember’s product team, the decision to remove the app dependency was driven by customer feedback. Many users reported that the app was a barrier to quick adjustments, especially when they were in a hurry or didn’t want to fumble with their phone. By embedding the temperature control directly into the mug’s hardware, Ember has streamlined the user experience and reduced friction.

Technical Details

The mug’s new firmware includes a micro‑controller that monitors the liquid temperature via a built‑in sensor. When the temperature deviates from the set point, the controller activates a small heating element that gently warms the drink. The system is powered by a rechargeable battery that can be charged through a USB‑C port, and the mug’s battery life has been extended to support a full day of use on a single charge.

Market Impact

Ember’s move comes at a time when smart kitchen appliances are gaining traction. The company’s previous models have been praised for their precision and durability, and the new mug is expected to reinforce Ember’s position as a leader in the niche market of temperature‑controlled drinkware. Retailers have already begun stocking the updated mug, and early reviews suggest that it will appeal to both coffee and tea enthusiasts who value consistency in their beverages.

Looking Ahead

While the new mug focuses on simplicity, Ember has hinted at future developments that could expand its ecosystem. The company is reportedly exploring integration with smart home assistants and exploring ways to incorporate additional health‑tracking features, such as hydration reminders. For now, however, the primary goal remains to deliver a reliable, user‑friendly product that keeps drinks at the perfect temperature without the need for constant app interaction.
