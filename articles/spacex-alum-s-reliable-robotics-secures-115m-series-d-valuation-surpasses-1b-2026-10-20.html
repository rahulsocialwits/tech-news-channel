SpaceX Alum’s Reliable Robotics Secures $115M Series D, Valuation Surpasses $1B

2026-10-20 · 3 min read
Reliable Robotics raises $115 million in equity and $25 million in debt, pushing its valuation past $1 billion as it expands its autonomous aviation platform.

Reliable Robotics, the aviation startup founded by a former SpaceX engineer, has closed a Series D round that raised $115 million in equity and an additional $25 million in debt. The funding brings the company’s valuation above the $1 billion mark, a milestone that underscores the growing interest in autonomous flight solutions.

Funding Details

The new capital will support the company’s development of its autonomous aircraft platform, which aims to reduce pilot workload and improve operational efficiency for commercial and cargo operators. The equity portion of the round was led by a consortium of venture investors, while the debt component was secured through a structured financing arrangement.

Industry Context

Reliable Robotics’ valuation growth follows a broader trend of increased investment in aviation automation. The company’s technology, which integrates advanced sensors, machine learning algorithms, and robust flight control systems, positions it to compete with established aerospace firms and emerging startups alike.

Future Outlook

With the new funding, Reliable Robotics plans to accelerate testing and certification of its autonomous aircraft. The company also intends to expand its customer base in the commercial aviation sector, targeting airlines and logistics providers that seek to lower operating costs and enhance safety.
