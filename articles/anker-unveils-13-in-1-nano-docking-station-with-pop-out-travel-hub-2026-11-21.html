Anker Unveils 13‑in‑1 Nano Docking Station with Pop‑Out Travel Hub

2026-11-21 · 3 min read
Anker’s new Nano Docking Station offers a 13‑in‑1 connectivity hub and a pop‑out travel hub, now discounted ahead of Black Friday.

Anker has released its latest Nano Docking Station, a compact 13‑in‑1 hub that bundles a range of ports into a single, portable unit. The device includes a USB‑C hub, HDMI output, SD card reader, and multiple USB‑A ports, all housed in a slim chassis that can be folded into a travel‑friendly shape.

Key Features

  • Pop‑Out Travel Hub – The docking station can be unfolded into a stand‑up hub, providing easy access to all ports while on the go.
  • Compact Design – At just a few inches thick, the Nano Dock fits into most laptop bags and can be carried as a standalone accessory.
  • Wide Compatibility – Works with Windows, macOS, and Linux laptops, as well as Android tablets that support USB‑C.

Pricing and Availability

Anker is offering the Nano Docking Station at a 33% discount, bringing the price down to $50 off the regular retail price. The deal is available on Amazon and is expected to run through the Black Friday shopping period.

Why It Matters

With the rise of remote work and mobile productivity, a versatile docking solution that can be carried in a backpack is increasingly valuable. Anker’s focus on affordability and portability positions the Nano Docking Station as a strong contender in the crowded market of multi‑port hubs.
