Deepgram Expands Voice AI Stack with Daily Acquisition

2026-10-29 · 3 min read
Deepgram has announced the purchase of Daily, the maker of Pipecat, to strengthen its voice AI infrastructure and keep the platform open source.

Deepgram, a leader in speech‑to‑text and voice AI, has announced that it has acquired Daily, the company behind the popular Pipecat voice‑interaction platform. The deal is aimed at bolstering Deepgram’s voice AI infrastructure and expanding its capabilities for real‑time audio processing.

Why the acquisition matters

Daily’s Pipecat is an open‑source framework that allows developers to build conversational AI experiences that can handle live audio streams. By integrating Pipecat into its own suite, Deepgram can offer a more comprehensive set of tools for building voice‑enabled applications, from customer service bots to real‑time transcription services.

Commitment to open source

In a statement, Deepgram emphasized its commitment to keeping Pipecat open source. The company said it will continue to support the community and provide updates to the Pipecat codebase, ensuring that developers can freely build on top of the platform.

Strategic fit for Deepgram

Deepgram has positioned itself as a key player in the growing voice AI market, offering high‑accuracy transcription and real‑time audio analytics. Adding Daily’s technology expands the company’s product portfolio and strengthens its position against competitors that are also investing heavily in voice AI.

Industry context

The acquisition comes at a time when many tech firms are looking to enhance their voice capabilities. With the rise of conversational AI and the increasing demand for real‑time audio processing in customer service, media, and accessibility, Deepgram’s move is timely.

Next steps for developers

Developers who have been using Pipecat can expect continued support and new features as part of the Deepgram ecosystem. The company plans to release updates that integrate Pipecat’s live audio handling with Deepgram’s transcription and analytics services.
