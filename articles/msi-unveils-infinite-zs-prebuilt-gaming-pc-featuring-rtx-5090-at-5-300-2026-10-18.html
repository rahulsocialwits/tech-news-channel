MSI Unveils Infinite ZS Prebuilt Gaming PC Featuring RTX 5090 at $5,300

2026-10-18 · 3 min read
MSI’s new Infinite ZS prebuilt PC bundles an RTX 5090 GPU and premium components for a discounted price of $5,300, offering gamers a high‑end build without the DIY hassle.

MSI has announced the Infinite ZS, a pre‑built gaming PC that comes equipped with the newly released RTX 5090 graphics card. The system is priced at $5,300, a significant discount from the $7,599 retail price that includes other components worth roughly $2,300 on their own.

Key Specifications

  • GPU: RTX 5090 – the latest from NVIDIA’s 50‑series lineup.
  • CPU: Intel Core i9‑13900K, delivering top‑tier processing power.
  • Memory: 32 GB DDR5, ensuring smooth multitasking and gaming performance.
  • Storage: 2 TB NVMe SSD, providing ample space for games and applications.
  • Cooling: Advanced liquid cooling system to maintain optimal temperatures during extended play.

Why the Discount Matters

MSI’s pricing strategy positions the Infinite ZS as an attractive option for gamers who want a ready‑to‑play machine without the time and effort of building a system from scratch. By bundling high‑end components, MSI reduces the total cost of ownership and eliminates compatibility concerns that often arise in DIY builds.

Market Context

The RTX 5090 is part of NVIDIA’s latest generation of GPUs, which has seen strong demand from both gamers and content creators. MSI’s pre‑built offering taps into this demand by providing a turnkey solution that can be deployed immediately in a home or esports environment.

Availability

MSI has stated that the Infinite ZS will be available for purchase through its official website and select retail partners starting in early November. The company is also offering a limited‑time promotional bundle that includes a 24‑hour gaming headset and a 4K monitor.
