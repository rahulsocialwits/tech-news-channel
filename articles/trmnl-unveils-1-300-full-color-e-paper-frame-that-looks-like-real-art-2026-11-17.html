TRMNL Unveils $1,300 Full‑Color E‑Paper Frame That Looks Like Real Art

2026-11-17 · 3 min read
TRMNL launches its most colorful e‑paper display yet, a $1,300 frame that renders full‑color images with paper‑like quality.

TRMNL, the maker of e‑paper frames and DIY kits, has announced its newest product: a full‑color e‑paper display that looks like real art. The frame, priced at $1,300, is the company’s biggest and most colorful offering to date.

What Makes the TRMNL Frame Stand Out?

The device uses a proprietary e‑paper technology that can display 16 million colors while maintaining the low‑power, paper‑like feel that has become TRMNL’s hallmark. Unlike traditional LCD or OLED panels, the e‑paper screen does not require a backlight, which keeps power consumption minimal and allows the frame to be used in a variety of lighting conditions.

Design and Build

TRMNL has kept the frame’s form factor true to its earlier models, offering a sleek, minimalist design that can be mounted on a wall or placed on a stand. The frame’s edges are finished in matte black, and the screen is protected by a thin, scratch‑resistant glass layer. Users can choose from a range of frame sizes, with the new full‑color model available in 12‑inch and 15‑inch options.

Software and Connectivity

The frame runs on a lightweight operating system that supports Wi‑Fi connectivity, allowing users to stream images directly from cloud services or upload photos via a companion app. The device also includes a USB‑C port for wired connections and a microSD slot for local storage.

Use Cases

TRMNL markets the frame as a versatile display for art, photography, and personal photos. Because the e‑paper technology can hold an image for weeks without power, it is ideal for showcasing artwork in galleries or homes where frequent battery changes would be impractical.

Pricing and Availability

With a price tag of $1,300, the TRMNL full‑color frame is positioned as a premium product for collectors and enthusiasts. The company plans to ship the first units in early December, with pre‑orders already open on its website.
