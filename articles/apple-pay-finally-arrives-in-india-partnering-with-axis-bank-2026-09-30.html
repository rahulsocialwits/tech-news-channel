Apple Pay Finally Arrives in India, Partnering with Axis Bank

2026-09-30 · 3 min read
Apple Pay launches in India today after years of delay, with Axis Bank as the first partner bank.

Apple Pay has officially entered the Indian market, launching today in partnership with Axis Bank. The move follows years of speculation and a series of regulatory hurdles that kept the mobile payment service off the country’s sidelines.

How the Service Works

Users can add their Axis Bank debit or credit cards to the Wallet app on iOS devices and tap to pay at any contactless terminal that accepts Apple Pay. The service uses tokenization to keep card details secure, and the transaction is authorized through the bank’s own authentication systems.

Why the Delay?

Earlier reports indicated that several of India’s largest banks were hesitant to support Apple Pay, citing concerns over regulatory compliance and the need for a robust merchant network. The recent announcement suggests that those concerns have been addressed, at least for Axis Bank, which has already integrated the necessary infrastructure.

Market Impact

Apple’s entry into India is expected to accelerate the adoption of digital wallets in a country where cash still dominates daily transactions. With the government pushing for a cash‑less economy, the launch could spur further competition among payment providers.
