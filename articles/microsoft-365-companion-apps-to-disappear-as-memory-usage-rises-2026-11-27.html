Microsoft 365 Companion Apps to Disappear as Memory Usage Rises

2026-11-27 · 3 min read
Microsoft is phasing out its companion apps for Office 365 after a year of high memory consumption, according to XDA Developers.

Microsoft has announced that the companion apps that run alongside its Microsoft 365 suite will be discontinued. The decision follows a year‑long period in which the apps were found to consume excessive amounts of device memory, according to a report by XDA Developers.

What Are Companion Apps?

Companion apps are lightweight utilities that provide quick access to Microsoft 365 features such as Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Outlook from the home screen or notification area. They were introduced to give users faster launch times and a more integrated experience across Windows, Android, and iOS devices.

Memory Concerns Prompted the Move

During their first year, the companion apps were reported to use a significant portion of RAM, especially on lower‑end devices. This high memory footprint led to slower overall performance and, in some cases, app crashes. The XDA Developers article highlighted that the apps were “taking up too much memory,” prompting Microsoft to reconsider their viability.

Impact on Users

Users who rely on the companion apps for quick document editing or email access will need to revert to the full Microsoft 365 applications. Microsoft has stated that the core Office apps remain fully functional and that the removal will not affect the availability of any features.

Future of Microsoft 365 on Mobile

Microsoft is focusing on optimizing the main Office apps for better performance and battery life. The company has also announced plans to enhance its cloud‑based collaboration tools, such as Teams and OneDrive, to provide a smoother experience across devices.

What This Means for Enterprise Software

For enterprises that have integrated Microsoft 365 into their workflows, the discontinuation of companion apps simplifies the software stack. IT departments can now concentrate on maintaining the primary Office suite and its security updates without managing additional lightweight utilities.
