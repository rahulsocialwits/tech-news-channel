Sony Introduces Lottery System to Tackle PS5 Pro Shortage in Japan

2026-09-30 · 3 min read
Sony’s new lottery system aims to give Japanese gamers a fair chance to buy the highly sought‑after PS5 Pro amid ongoing supply constraints.

Sony has rolled out a lottery‑based purchasing system for the PlayStation 5 Pro in Japan, a move designed to curb scalping and give regular gamers a fair shot at the console. The company announced the change on Wednesday, noting that the PS5 Pro has been in short supply since its launch, with demand far outstripping available stock.

How the Lottery Works

Gamers who wish to buy a PS5 Pro must first prove that they own a PlayStation console. Once eligibility is confirmed, they can enter the lottery for a chance to purchase the console at retail price. The system is intended to prevent bulk buying by resellers and ensure that the limited units reach genuine fans.

Industry Context

The shortage of the PS5 Pro is part of a broader trend affecting high‑end gaming hardware, as manufacturers struggle to meet demand while navigating supply chain disruptions. Sony’s lottery approach mirrors similar measures taken by other companies to address scalping and maintain consumer trust.

What This Means for Gamers

While the lottery does not guarantee a purchase, it offers a more transparent and equitable process than previous methods. Players who successfully win a spot will receive a notification and can complete the transaction within a set window. Those who do not win will be entered into subsequent draws.

Looking Ahead

Sony has not yet announced how long the lottery will run or how many units will be made available each cycle. However, the company’s initiative signals a commitment to addressing consumer frustration and ensuring that the PS5 Pro remains accessible to dedicated fans.
