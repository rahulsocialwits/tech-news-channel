Liferay and MindCraft Join Forces to Modernise Enterprise Digital Experiences

2026-10-08 · 3 min read
Liferay and MindCraft partner to combine DXP and consulting expertise, helping Indian enterprises simplify digital environments and accelerate transformation.

In a move that signals a new wave of digital transformation for Indian businesses, Liferay and MindCraft have announced a partnership aimed at modernising enterprise digital experiences. The collaboration will blend Liferay’s Digital Experience Platform (DXP) with MindCraft’s technology and consulting services to help organisations simplify complex digital environments and accelerate their transformation journeys.

What the partnership brings

Liferay, known for its open‑source portal and content management capabilities, will provide its DXP as the core platform for building and managing digital experiences. MindCraft, a consulting firm with a focus on technology strategy, will bring its expertise in implementation, integration, and change management. Together, the two companies plan to offer a comprehensive solution that covers everything from design and development to deployment and ongoing optimisation.

Target market and benefits

The partnership is positioned to serve enterprises across a range of sectors, including finance, manufacturing, and public services. By combining Liferay’s flexible, modular architecture with MindCraft’s consulting know‑how, the alliance aims to reduce time‑to‑market for new digital initiatives, lower total cost of ownership, and improve user engagement.

Strategic context

India’s digital economy is growing rapidly, with a strong push from the government to digitise public services and encourage private sector digital adoption. The Liferay‑MindCraft partnership aligns with this trend, offering a ready‑made solution that can be tailored to the specific needs of Indian enterprises. The collaboration also reflects a broader industry shift toward platform‑centric approaches that enable rapid innovation while maintaining control over data and security.

Future outlook

Both companies have expressed confidence that the partnership will drive significant value for clients. Liferay’s CEO noted that the alliance will allow the company to expand its footprint in the Indian market, while MindCraft’s leadership highlighted the opportunity to leverage Liferay’s open‑source foundation to deliver cost‑effective, scalable solutions.

As enterprises continue to grapple with digital disruption, the Liferay‑MindCraft partnership offers a compelling option for organisations looking to modernise their digital experiences without starting from scratch.
