NetApp Launches Cohort 15 to Accelerate AI‑Powered Deep‑Tech Innovation

2026-10-17 · 3 min read
NetApp’s new Excellerator cohort brings together AI, security, automation and data infrastructure innovators to push the next wave of deep‑tech solutions.

NetApp has announced the launch of Cohort 15 of its Excellerator program, a deep‑tech accelerator focused on the AI era. The initiative, based in Bengaluru, India, aims to bring together innovators working on AI‑powered software engineering, security, automation and intelligent data infrastructure.

Program Focus

The cohort is designed to support startups that are developing cutting‑edge solutions in areas such as machine‑learning model deployment, secure data pipelines, and automated infrastructure management. Participants will receive mentorship, access to NetApp’s enterprise‑grade storage and data services, and opportunities to collaborate with industry partners.

Strategic Context

NetApp’s move comes as the company seeks to strengthen its position in the AI infrastructure market. By fostering a community of deep‑tech builders, the firm hopes to accelerate the adoption of AI workloads across enterprises while ensuring data security and operational efficiency.

What’s Next

Startups selected for Cohort 15 will begin their engagement in early November, with a series of workshops, demo days and potential funding rounds scheduled over the next six months. The program’s success will be measured by the commercial viability of the participating solutions and their impact on AI‑driven business outcomes.
