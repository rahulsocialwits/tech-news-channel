Todoist Data Breach: 6% of Customers’ Details Stolen

2026-11-25 · 3 min read
Todoist confirms a data breach that exposed email IDs, password hashes and account creation dates for 6% of its users.

Todoist, the popular task‑management platform, has confirmed that a hacker gained access to its production systems and stole sensitive customer data. The breach, which was discovered early this week, affected roughly 6% of the company’s user base.

What Was Compromised?

The stolen data includes email addresses, password hashes and the dates when accounts were created. No financial information or personal identification numbers were reported as part of the leak.

Response and Mitigation

Todoist has notified affected users and is working with security experts to investigate the incident. The company has also taken steps to strengthen its security posture, including tightening access controls and conducting a comprehensive audit of its production environment.

Industry Context

Data breaches continue to be a major concern for SaaS providers. This incident underscores the importance of robust security practices, especially for applications that handle user credentials and personal data.
