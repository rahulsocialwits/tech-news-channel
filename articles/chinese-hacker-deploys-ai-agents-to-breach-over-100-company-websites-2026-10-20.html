Chinese Hacker Deploys AI Agents to Breach Over 100 Company Websites

2026-10-20 · 3 min read
A Chinese hacker used a fleet of AI agents to infiltrate more than 100 corporate sites, including major brands like Deere and SharkNinja.

A Chinese hacker reportedly leveraged a fleet of AI agents to break into more than 100 company websites, including farm equipment maker Deere and appliance outfit SharkNinja. The attack used an open‑source, AI‑powered problem‑solving platform that allowed the attacker to automate reconnaissance, vulnerability exploitation and credential harvesting across a wide range of targets.

How the Attack Worked

The hacker’s toolkit combined natural language processing with automated code‑generation techniques to craft custom exploits on the fly. By feeding the AI with publicly available information about each target, the system could identify weak points and generate scripts to bypass security controls.

Impact on Target Companies

Deere and SharkNinja were among the high‑profile victims. While the full extent of data accessed remains unclear, the breach highlights the growing threat posed by AI‑enhanced hacking tools that can scale attacks rapidly.

Industry Response

Security experts warn that the ease of deploying AI agents lowers the barrier to entry for sophisticated cyberattacks. Companies are urged to strengthen their defensive posture by adopting AI‑driven threat detection and implementing stricter access controls.
