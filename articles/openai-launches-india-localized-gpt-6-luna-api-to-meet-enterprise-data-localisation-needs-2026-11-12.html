OpenAI Launches India‑Localized GPT‑6 Luna API to Meet Enterprise Data‑Localisation Needs

2026-11-12 · 3 min read
OpenAI now lets Indian enterprises run GPT‑6 Luna requests inside India, addressing data‑localisation regulations and reducing latency.

OpenAI has announced that its GPT‑6 Luna model can now be accessed through the company’s API with all processing taking place inside India. The move is aimed at helping Indian enterprises comply with data‑localisation rules that require sensitive data to remain within national borders.

How the new setup works

Under the new arrangement, requests sent to the OpenAI API are routed to servers located in India. The model processes the input, generates a response, and returns it to the client—all without the data leaving the country. This is a first‑of‑its‑kind offering for the GPT‑6 Luna model, which is already popular for its advanced language capabilities.

Why it matters for Indian businesses

India’s data‑protection framework, which came into force in 2023, mandates that certain categories of data—including personal and sensitive information—be stored and processed domestically. By keeping AI workloads in India, companies can avoid potential legal penalties and reduce the risk of data breaches during cross‑border transfers.

Impact on latency and performance

Localising the AI processing also cuts down on round‑trip latency. For applications that rely on real‑time responses, such as customer support chatbots or automated content generation, the reduced delay can translate into a smoother user experience.

Future outlook

OpenAI’s decision to offer an India‑specific deployment for GPT‑6 Luna reflects a broader trend of cloud providers tailoring services to meet regional regulatory requirements. As more enterprises adopt multi‑model AI strategies, similar localisation initiatives are likely to appear in other markets.
