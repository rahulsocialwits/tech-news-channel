Apple Opens Pre‑Orders for New Home Collection Devices on November 13

2026-11-12 · 3 min read
Apple will let customers lock in orders for two LG cameras and a video doorbell as part of its Home Collection launch.

Apple is expanding its Home Collection with a new lineup of smart‑home accessories that will be available for pre‑order starting November 13. The company’s announcement, made through an official blog post, lists two LG cameras and a video doorbell as the first devices to join the ecosystem.

What’s on the Horizon?

The LG cameras are expected to feature Apple’s HomeKit integration, allowing users to view live footage and receive alerts directly on their iPhone, iPad, or Apple TV. The video doorbell, meanwhile, will support two‑way audio and facial recognition, enabling homeowners to see and speak with visitors from anywhere.

How to Secure Your Order

Customers can place pre‑orders through the Apple Store app or website. Once the order is confirmed, Apple will ship the devices on the first available date, which the company has not yet disclosed. The pre‑order window will close on November 20, giving buyers a limited window to lock in the latest smart‑home tech.

Why It Matters

Apple’s move signals a broader push into the smart‑home market, where it has traditionally lagged behind competitors like Google and Amazon. By partnering with LG, the company can leverage the manufacturer’s expertise in camera technology while maintaining its own standards for privacy and security.
