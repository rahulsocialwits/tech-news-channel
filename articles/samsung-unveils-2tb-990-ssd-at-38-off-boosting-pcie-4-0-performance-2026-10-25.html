Samsung Unveils 2TB 990 SSD at 38% Off, Boosting PCIe 4.0 Performance

2026-10-25 · 3 min read
Samsung’s new 2TB 990 SSD offers PCIe 4.0 speeds and a 38% discount on Amazon, delivering a cost‑effective upgrade for gamers and creators.

Samsung has announced a new 2TB SSD that leverages the latest PCIe 4.0 interface, offering a significant performance boost for high‑end PCs and workstations. The drive, named the Samsung 990, is currently available on Amazon with a 38% discount, translating to a $200 savings for buyers who want to upgrade their storage without breaking the bank.

Key Specs and Performance

The 990 is built on Samsung’s latest 2.5‑inch form factor and uses a PCIe 4.0 NVMe interface, which can deliver read speeds up to 7,000 MB/s and write speeds up to 5,000 MB/s. These figures represent a noticeable improvement over the company’s previous 980 model, which was limited to PCIe 3.0 speeds. The drive also features Samsung’s new V-NAND technology, which improves endurance and reduces power consumption.

Why the 38% Discount Matters

For many consumers, the price of high‑performance SSDs remains a barrier to entry. Samsung’s 38% discount brings the 990’s list price down to a more approachable range, making it a compelling option for gamers, content creators, and professionals who need fast storage for large files and demanding applications.

Availability and Shipping

The discounted price is currently available on Amazon, with the promotion running for a limited time. Shipping is free for Prime members, and the drive ships in a protective enclosure that includes a quick‑install guide. Samsung also offers a 5‑year warranty on the 990, providing peace of mind for users who rely on the drive for critical workloads.

Implications for the SSD Market

Samsung’s aggressive pricing strategy signals a shift in the SSD market, where competition is intensifying as more manufacturers introduce PCIe 4.0 drives. By offering a high‑performance product at a lower price point, Samsung is positioning itself to capture a larger share of the consumer and enterprise segments.

What This Means for Users

For gamers, the 990’s faster load times can reduce wait times for game assets and improve overall system responsiveness. Content creators will benefit from quicker file transfers and smoother editing workflows, especially when working with 4K video or large datasets. Enterprise users can also take advantage of the drive’s endurance and reliability for mission‑critical applications.

Future Outlook

As PCIe 5.0 begins to roll out, Samsung is likely to continue refining its SSD lineup. The 990’s success could pave the way for future models that push performance even further while maintaining competitive pricing. For now, the 2TB 990 offers a compelling blend of speed, capacity, and affordability.
