Samsung’s 1TB 990 SSD Slashes Prices to 19¢ per GB Amid Memory Crunch

2026-11-14 · 3 min read
Samsung’s 990 PCIe 4.0 SSD hits a new low, offering high‑speed storage for under $40 as memory prices fall.

Samsung has announced a dramatic price cut for its 1TB 990 SSD, bringing the unit to just 19 cents per gigabyte. The move comes as the global memory market eases, allowing the company to offer a high‑performance PCIe 4.0 drive at a fraction of its original cost.

What the 990 SSD Offers

The 990 is built on Samsung’s latest 3D NAND architecture and supports PCIe 4.0, delivering read speeds up to 5,000 MB/s and write speeds up to 4,200 MB/s. Its design targets gamers, content creators, and PC builders who need fast storage without the price premium of earlier models.

Why the Price Drop Matters

With memory prices falling after a period of supply constraints, Samsung can reduce the cost of its SSDs while maintaining performance. The 1TB 990 now sells for around $40, making it an attractive upgrade for users looking to replace older SATA drives or add a secondary high‑speed drive to a system.

How to Get the Deal

Retailers are offering the 1TB 990 at a discount of $40 off the regular price. Shoppers can find the drive on major e‑commerce platforms and through select electronics retailers. The discount is available for a limited time, so early adopters stand to benefit the most.
