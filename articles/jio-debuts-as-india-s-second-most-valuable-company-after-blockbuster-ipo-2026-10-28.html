Jio Debuts as India’s Second‑Most Valuable Company After Blockbuster IPO

2026-10-28 · 3 min read
Jio’s market debut sees shares surge 21%, positioning it as India’s second‑most valuable listed company behind Reliance Industries.

On Tuesday, Jio’s shares opened at a record high, climbing 21% in their first day of trading. The surge propelled the company to become India’s second‑most valuable listed firm, trailing only its parent company, Reliance Industries.

IPO Highlights

The telecom giant’s public offering was one of the largest in India’s history, raising significant capital to expand its 5G network and digital services. Investors responded enthusiastically, driving the stock price well above the initial offering range.

Market Impact

Jio’s debut has already reshaped the Indian market landscape. Analysts note that the company’s valuation now places it among the top tier of global telecom players, reflecting the growing importance of digital infrastructure in emerging economies.

Future Outlook

With a robust capital base, Jio plans to accelerate its 5G rollout and invest in new digital platforms. The company’s leadership believes that the expanded network will support a wide range of services, from streaming to e‑commerce, further solidifying its position in the competitive telecom sector.
