Zotac Denies Warranty for RTX 3060 Owners in India After One Year

2026-09-29 · 3 min read
Zotac claims warranty starts from import date, sparking backlash among Indian RTX 3060 users.

In a move that has upset many gamers, Zotac has denied warranty support to an Indian owner of an RTX 3060 graphics card that was purchased in 2025. The company argues that the three‑year warranty it offers in India begins from the date of import, not the date of sale, and that the card was imported in 2023.

Warranty policy dispute

The owner, who bought the card in 2025, had expected the standard three‑year coverage to apply. Zotac’s response, however, cites the import date as the start of the warranty period, effectively cutting the coverage to just one year from the purchase date.

Industry reaction

Tech reviewers and consumer groups have criticized the company’s stance, noting that the policy is not clearly communicated at the point of sale. The incident highlights the importance of transparent warranty terms for hardware sold overseas.
