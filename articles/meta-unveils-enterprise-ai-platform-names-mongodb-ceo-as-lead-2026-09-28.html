Meta Unveils Enterprise AI Platform, Names MongoDB CEO as Lead

2026-09-28 · 3 min read
Meta launches a new enterprise AI platform, bringing its full tech stack to businesses and hiring MongoDB's CEO to head the initiative.

Meta has announced a new enterprise AI platform that bundles its full technology stack—including Muse, Meta Business Agent, Muse API, and Muse Code—into a single offering for businesses and developers. The company said the platform will enable enterprises to build and deploy AI solutions more quickly and securely.

Leadership and Vision

In a move that signals Meta’s commitment to the enterprise market, the company has hired MongoDB’s CEO, who will lead the new AI initiative. The appointment follows Meta’s recent push to expand its AI capabilities beyond consumer products.

Platform Features

The platform is designed to provide a comprehensive set of tools that cover data ingestion, model training, and deployment. Meta’s Muse API and Muse Code are positioned as core components, offering developers a streamlined way to integrate AI into their applications.

Strategic Implications

By bringing its AI stack to enterprises, Meta aims to compete with other cloud AI providers and capture a share of the growing demand for AI-powered business solutions. The move also reflects the broader industry trend of large tech firms expanding into enterprise services.
