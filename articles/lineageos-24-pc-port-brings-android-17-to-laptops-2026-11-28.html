LineageOS 24 PC Port Brings Android 17 to Laptops

2026-11-28 · 3 min read
The new LineageOS 24 PC port lets users run Android 17 on their laptops, offering a full Android experience on desktop hardware.

Android enthusiasts have a new reason to celebrate. The LineageOS community has released a PC port of its 24th major release, bringing the latest Android 17 experience to laptops and other desktop hardware. The project, announced on November 27, 2026, aims to give users a full Android operating system on a conventional PC, complete with the familiar app ecosystem and system settings.

What the Port Offers

The LineageOS 24 PC port is built on the same codebase that powers the popular Android 17 release for mobile devices. It supports a wide range of hardware, from Intel and AMD processors to ARM-based laptops, and includes drivers for common peripherals such as touchscreens, cameras, and external displays. Users can install the OS via a standard ISO image, boot from a USB stick, and follow a straightforward setup wizard.

Why It Matters

For developers and power users, the port provides a sandbox for testing Android apps on desktop hardware without the need for an emulator. It also opens up possibilities for custom UI tweaks, performance optimizations, and integration with desktop workflows. The project’s open‑source nature means that contributors can add support for new devices, improve stability, and extend functionality.

Community Reception

Early adopters have praised the port’s stability and the fidelity of the Android experience on laptops. One reviewer noted that the interface feels “native” and that the transition between desktop and mobile modes is seamless. The LineageOS team has also highlighted that the port is a “cure for Googlebook envy,” allowing users to run Android on a device that traditionally runs a different operating system.

Getting Started

To try the LineageOS 24 PC port, download the ISO from the official LineageOS website, create a bootable USB drive, and boot your laptop in UEFI mode. The installation process is similar to installing any other Linux distribution, with options to partition the drive, set up a user account, and configure system settings. Once installed, the OS will boot into a familiar Android home screen, complete with the Google Play Store and other pre‑installed apps.

Future Outlook

The LineageOS team plans to continue expanding device support and adding features such as improved battery management and enhanced security settings. As the Android ecosystem evolves, the PC port is poised to become a valuable tool for developers, testers, and users who want the flexibility of Android on a laptop.
