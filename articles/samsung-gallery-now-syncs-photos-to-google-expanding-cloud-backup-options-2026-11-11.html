Samsung Gallery Now Syncs Photos to Google, Expanding Cloud Backup Options

2026-11-11 · 3 min read
Samsung users can now back up photos and videos from select devices to their Google accounts, adding flexibility to the company’s cloud strategy.

Samsung has announced that its Gallery app will now sync photos and videos to Google accounts on select devices. The move gives users an alternative to Samsung’s own cloud services and aligns with the company’s broader strategy to support multiple cloud ecosystems.

How the Sync Works

When users enable the new feature, Gallery will automatically upload media to the Google Photos library associated with the user’s Google account. The sync is opt‑in, meaning users must explicitly grant permission for Gallery to access their Google account. Once enabled, the app will continue to back up new media as it is captured, and users can choose to keep a local copy on the device or delete it after upload.

Supported Devices and Compatibility

The update is available on a range of recent Samsung smartphones and tablets that run Android 13 or later. Devices that support the feature include the Galaxy S24 series, the Galaxy Z Fold 5, and the Galaxy Tab S9. Samsung has not yet announced a rollout schedule for older models.

Why Samsung is Adding Google Backup

Samsung’s decision follows a trend of manufacturers offering multi‑cloud backup options. By partnering with Google, Samsung can provide users with a familiar and widely used platform for photo storage, potentially reducing the need for users to switch to Samsung’s OneDrive‑based cloud. The feature also helps Samsung address concerns about data sovereignty and user choice in the increasingly competitive cloud market.

What Users Need to Know

Users who enable Google sync will see their media appear in Google Photos, where it can be organized, shared, and edited using Google’s suite of tools. The sync does not affect the user’s OneDrive or Samsung Cloud settings, and users can disable the feature at any time from the Gallery settings menu. Samsung has stated that the sync will not impact device performance or battery life significantly, as uploads occur in the background when the device is connected to Wi‑Fi.

Future Outlook

Samsung has indicated that it plans to expand the sync feature to include other Google services, such as Google Drive, in future updates. The company also announced that it will continue to improve its own cloud offerings, adding new storage tiers and enhanced security features. For now, the Google sync option provides a flexible, user‑controlled way to keep media safe across multiple platforms.
