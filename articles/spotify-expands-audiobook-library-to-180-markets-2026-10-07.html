Spotify Expands Audiobook Library to 180+ Markets

2026-10-07 · 3 min read
Spotify will offer 350,000 audiobooks in over 120 languages across more than 180 markets, broadening its audio content worldwide.

Spotify has announced a major expansion of its audiobook catalog, adding 350,000 titles in more than 120 languages. The move will bring the streaming service’s audio library to over 180 markets, making it one of the largest audiobook platforms in the world.

Global Reach and Language Diversity

The new rollout includes titles in languages ranging from English and Spanish to less commonly represented tongues such as Swahili and Tamil. By covering a wide linguistic spectrum, Spotify aims to cater to listeners in both developed and emerging markets.

Impact on the Audiobook Ecosystem

With the addition of so many new titles, Spotify is positioning itself as a serious competitor to dedicated audiobook services. The expansion also reflects the growing demand for on-demand audio content, especially in regions where traditional book sales are declining.

Future Plans

Spotify’s leadership has indicated that the company will continue to grow its audiobook offerings, potentially adding more niche genres and regional content in the coming months.
