GrapheneOS Launches Proof‑of‑Concept RCS Messaging App to Replace Google Messages

2026-11-23 · 3 min read
GrapheneOS has unveiled a new RCS messaging app that could eliminate the need for Google Messages on supported carriers.

GrapheneOS, the privacy‑focused Android fork, has announced a new proof‑of‑concept app that implements Rich Communication Services (RCS) without relying on Google’s messaging stack. The move comes as the open‑source community seeks alternatives to the default Google Messages app, which many users find invasive.

What the App Does

The app, still in its early stages, supports the core RCS features that users expect: group chats, read receipts, typing indicators, and high‑resolution media sharing. It is designed to work with carriers that have enabled RCS on their networks, allowing users to send and receive messages directly through the carrier’s infrastructure rather than through Google’s servers.

Why It Matters

Google Messages has long been the default messaging app on Android devices, but it requires a Google account and sends data to Google’s cloud services. For privacy advocates, this model raises concerns about data collection and surveillance. GrapheneOS’s app offers a path to a more private messaging experience while still providing the modern features that users have come to expect from RCS.

Current Status and Future Plans

At present, the app is a proof‑of‑concept. The developers have not yet released a full‑featured version for public download, and it is unclear which carriers will support it. However, the team has expressed an intention to create a standalone implementation that can run on any device with carrier support, potentially making it a viable alternative to Google Messages in the future.

Implications for the Android Ecosystem

If the app gains traction, it could encourage other privacy‑focused projects to develop their own messaging solutions. It also highlights the growing demand for open, carrier‑agnostic messaging platforms that do not depend on large tech companies’ ecosystems.

Next Steps for Users

Users interested in experimenting with the app should keep an eye on GrapheneOS’s official channels for updates. As the project matures, it may become available through the OS’s app repository or as a downloadable APK for advanced users willing to sideload the app.
