FinStackk Unveils SalesTaxx to Automate U.S. Sales Tax Compliance

2026-10-17 · 3 min read
FinStackk’s new platform tracks state sales tax obligations and pinpoints exact collection dates for U.S. businesses.

FinStackk, a fintech startup based in India, has launched SalesTaxx, a free service that helps U.S. businesses manage their sales tax responsibilities across all 50 states. The platform automatically tracks each state’s tax rules and alerts companies to the precise dates they must begin collecting tax, reducing the risk of costly compliance errors.

How SalesTaxx Works

SalesTaxx pulls data from state tax authorities and updates its database in real time. When a business registers, the tool calculates the applicable tax rates for every state where the company sells goods or services. It then generates a calendar of collection dates, ensuring that merchants start collecting tax exactly when required by law.

Benefits for Small and Medium‑Sized Enterprises

Many small and medium‑sized enterprises (SMEs) struggle with the complexity of U.S. sales tax, especially when expanding into new states. SalesTaxx offers a simple, cost‑effective solution that eliminates manual research and reduces the likelihood of audit penalties.

Future Plans

FinStackk plans to add features such as automated filing and integration with popular e‑commerce platforms. The company aims to become a one‑stop shop for U.S. sales tax compliance.
