Mark Wahlberg Launches Affordable Gym Concept to Challenge Longevity Startups

2026-10-17 · 3 min read
Actor Mark Wahlberg is opening a new gym chain that promises cold plunges, blood panels and protein muffins at a low price, aiming to compete with high‑end longevity brands.

Hollywood star Mark Wahlberg is stepping into the fitness industry with a new gym concept that promises a full wellness experience without the premium price tag. The actor, who previously invested in a gym chain that was later delisted from the NYSE, is now building his own brand that will feature cold plunges, blood panels and protein muffins as standard amenities.

Affordable Wellness Package

Wahlberg’s new venture aims to make advanced health services accessible to a broader audience. Each membership will include a cold plunge session, a routine blood panel to monitor health metrics, and a protein muffin to support recovery—all without requiring a trust fund.

Competitive Edge Against Longevity Startups

The concept directly targets the growing market of longevity startups that focus on anti‑aging technologies and premium wellness services. By bundling these offerings into a single, low‑cost package, Wahlberg hopes to attract consumers who want high‑quality health monitoring without the steep price.

Industry Context

The fitness and wellness sector has seen a surge in boutique gyms and tech‑driven health services. Wahlberg’s entry adds a celebrity‑backed brand that could reshape how consumers perceive value in gym memberships.
