Google Expands AI‑Powered Search Ads Worldwide as Core Ad Growth Slows

2026-10-29 · 3 min read
Google is rolling out AI‑driven search advertising across all markets, a move that comes as the company’s core ad revenue growth decelerates.

Google announced today that it will launch AI‑powered search advertising on a global scale, extending the feature to all regions where the company operates. The rollout follows Alphabet’s third‑quarter earnings, which showed a 25% year‑over‑year increase in revenue to $127.6 billion, but also highlighted a slowdown in core ad growth.

What the Expansion Means

The new AI Search ads use machine learning to generate ad copy that aligns more closely with user intent. Advertisers can now create campaigns that automatically adapt headlines and descriptions based on the search query, potentially improving click‑through rates and conversion.

Impact on Advertisers and Users

For marketers, the feature promises higher relevance and efficiency, as the AI can tailor messaging in real time. Users, meanwhile, may see more personalized ads that better match their search context. Google’s move is part of a broader strategy to offset the plateauing growth in its traditional ad segments.

Industry Context

Alphabet’s decision comes amid increasing competition from other platforms that are also investing heavily in AI‑driven advertising. By expanding the technology globally, Google aims to maintain its dominance in the search advertising market while offering advertisers a more sophisticated toolset.
