Nintendo Switch 2 Controllers Hit the Market Ahead of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remaster

2026-11-06 · 3 min read
New Switch 2 controllers from 8BitDo, GameSir and others launch now, offering improved ergonomics and wireless play for the upcoming Zelda remaster.

Gamers looking to upgrade their handheld experience have a new option on the table as several manufacturers roll out Switch 2‑compatible controllers just in time for the highly‑anticipated Zelda: Ocarina of Time remaster. The lineup includes wireless gamepads from 8BitDo, GameSir and other brands, all priced around $49.

Why the timing matters

The remaster of the classic Zelda title is set to launch later this year, and many players are eager to find a controller that offers better ergonomics and battery life than the standard Joy‑Con setup. The new Switch 2 controllers are designed to fit the updated console’s larger form factor while maintaining the familiar button layout.

Key features of the new controllers

  • Wireless connectivity – Bluetooth 5.0 support for a stable, low‑latency connection.
  • Ergonomic design – Contoured grips and a balanced weight distribution for extended play sessions.
  • Battery life – Up to 20 hours of gameplay on a single charge, depending on usage.
  • Compatibility – Fully compatible with the Switch 2 and backward compatible with the original Switch.

Price and availability

All three models are available for purchase online and in select retail stores. The price point of $49 positions them as a budget-friendly alternative to the official Switch Pro controller, which retails at a higher price.

What this means for the Zelda community

With the remaster’s release approaching, the availability of these controllers gives players a chance to tailor their gameplay experience. Whether you prefer a more traditional handheld feel or a console‑style grip, the new Switch 2 gamepads provide a range of options without breaking the bank.
