Eloelo Launches AI‑Powered Script‑to‑Video Tool for Indian Creators

2026-10-03 · 3 min read
Indian startup Eloelo unveils an AI platform that converts written scripts into polished videos, offering a new workflow for content creators.

Indian startup Eloelo has introduced a new artificial‑intelligence tool that turns written scripts into finished videos. The platform, announced on Saturday, is designed to streamline the production process for creators who need to convert narrative content into visual media quickly.

How the Tool Works

Eloelo’s system takes a user’s script and automatically generates a storyboard, selects relevant visuals, and adds voice‑over narration. The AI engine then stitches these elements together, producing a ready‑to‑publish video. The service is aimed at marketers, educators, and independent creators who often face time constraints when producing video content.

Target Market and Use Cases

By automating the heavy lifting of video creation, Eloelo positions itself as a solution for small businesses and individual creators who lack the resources for a full production team. The tool can be used for promotional videos, educational tutorials, and social media content, where rapid turnaround is essential.

Technology Behind the Platform

While Eloelo has not disclosed the specific AI models powering the service, the platform likely leverages natural‑language processing to parse scripts and computer‑vision techniques to source appropriate imagery. The integration of text‑to‑speech and automated editing suggests a combination of generative AI and media‑processing pipelines.

Implications for the Indian Content Ecosystem

India’s digital content market is growing rapidly, with a surge in demand for video across e‑learning, e‑commerce, and entertainment. Eloelo’s tool could lower the barrier to entry for creators, enabling more diverse voices to produce high‑quality video content without significant upfront investment.
