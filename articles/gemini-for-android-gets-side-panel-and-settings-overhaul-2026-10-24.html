Gemini for Android Gets Side‑Panel and Settings Overhaul

2026-10-24 · 3 min read
Meta’s Gemini AI app on Android receives a refreshed side‑panel layout and redesigned settings interface as part of its latest update.

Meta’s Gemini AI companion for Android has just rolled out a significant visual and functional refresh. The update focuses on two key areas: the side‑panel that hosts quick actions and the settings menu that lets users tweak the app’s behavior.

Side‑Panel Redesign

The side‑panel, which appears when users swipe from the left edge of the screen, now features a cleaner layout with clearer iconography. Users can access shortcuts to Gemini’s core functions—such as the chat interface, the image generation tool, and the voice‑to‑text feature—more quickly. The new design also groups related actions together, making navigation more intuitive.

Settings Menu Revamp

In the settings section, Meta has reorganized options into logical categories. The new structure separates privacy controls, notification preferences, and integration settings into distinct panels. This change aims to help users find the settings they need without scrolling through a long, unstructured list.

Why It Matters

Gemini’s side‑panel and settings are the primary touchpoints for everyday users. By streamlining these interfaces, Meta is addressing feedback that the app felt cluttered and difficult to navigate. The update also aligns Gemini’s Android experience more closely with its web counterpart, providing a consistent cross‑platform feel.

Meta’s move comes after the company’s broader push to make its AI tools more accessible and user‑friendly. The refreshed interface is expected to improve engagement and reduce friction for new and existing users alike.
