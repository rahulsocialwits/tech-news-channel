Logitech MX Master 3S Bluetooth Edition Hits Record Low, Offering Quiet Productivity for $28

2026-11-25 · 3 min read
Logitech’s ergonomic MX Master 3S Bluetooth mouse drops to $28, featuring MagSpeed scroll and three‑device switching, making it a top Black Friday gadget deal.

For the first time in its history, Logitech’s popular MX Master 3S Bluetooth Edition is available at a record low price of $28. The deal, announced by Android Authority on November 25, 2026, brings the quiet, ergonomic mouse—known for its MagSpeed scroll wheel and seamless three‑device switching—to a price point that makes it an irresistible Black Friday offer for productivity enthusiasts.

What Makes the MX Master 3S Stand Out?

The MX Master 3S builds on the success of its predecessor, the MX Master 3, by adding a Bluetooth‑only mode that eliminates the need for a USB receiver. This feature is especially useful for users who prefer a cleaner desk setup or who work across multiple devices such as laptops, tablets, and smartphones. The mouse also retains the familiar ergonomic shape that has earned it a loyal following among professionals who spend long hours at the computer.

Key Features Highlighted in the Deal

  • MagSpeed scroll wheel – Provides smooth, precise scrolling with a magnetic stop that reduces wear and tear.
  • Three‑device switching – Allows users to pair the mouse with up to three devices and switch between them with a simple button press.
  • Quiet operation – Designed to minimize click noise, making it ideal for shared workspaces and quiet environments.
  • Long battery life – The mouse can last up to 70 days on a single charge, reducing the need for frequent recharging.

Why the Price Drop Matters

Logitech’s decision to slash the price to $28 is significant for several reasons. First, it makes the MX Master 3S accessible to a broader audience, including students and freelancers who may have previously found the $99 price point prohibitive. Second, the timing—coinciding with the Black Friday shopping rush—positions the mouse as a top pick for tech-savvy shoppers looking for high‑quality peripherals at a discount.

Comparing to Competitors

When compared to other ergonomic mice on the market, the MX Master 3S offers a compelling combination of features and affordability. Competitors such as the Microsoft Surface Precision Mouse and the Razer Pro Click typically retail above $100, and they do not offer the same level of quiet operation or the same battery longevity. The $28 price point therefore represents a substantial value proposition for users who prioritize comfort and efficiency.

How to Take Advantage of the Deal

Android Authority notes that the discount is available through select retailers and online platforms. Shoppers should act quickly, as Black Friday deals often sell out fast. The offer is valid for a limited time, so those interested in upgrading their workstation should consider purchasing the MX Master 3S before the promotion ends.

Conclusion

Logitech’s MX Master 3S Bluetooth Edition at $28 is a standout gadget deal that combines ergonomic design, advanced features, and affordability. Whether you’re a professional looking to enhance your productivity or a casual user seeking a quiet, reliable mouse, this offer makes the MX Master 3S an excellent choice for the holiday season.
