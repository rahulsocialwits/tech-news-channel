BriefCatch Expands Legal Writing Suite to Catch AI‑Generated Errors

2026-10-16 · 3 min read
BriefCatch adds new tools to flag fabricated citations and misleading claims in AI‑generated legal briefs.

Legal tech firm BriefCatch has unveiled an expanded suite of tools designed to spot and correct errors in AI‑generated legal documents. The update, announced on October 15, 2026, adds features that flag fabricated citations, identify misleading claims, and help lawyers maintain the integrity of their briefs.

Why the Update Matters

AI models used in legal drafting can sometimes produce citations that do not exist or misrepresent case law. These inaccuracies can undermine the credibility of a brief and expose law firms to liability. BriefCatch’s new modules analyze the text against a database of verified legal sources, highlighting any inconsistencies for review.

Key Features of the Expanded Suite

  • Citation Verification – Cross‑checks every reference against a curated legal database to confirm authenticity.
  • Claim Accuracy Checker – Detects statements that lack supporting evidence or contradict established precedent.
  • Human‑in‑the‑Loop Workflow – Allows attorneys to review flagged sections before finalizing the document.

Industry Reception

Legal professionals have welcomed the update as a necessary safeguard in an era where AI is increasingly used for drafting. By providing a systematic way to audit AI output, BriefCatch aims to reduce the risk of inadvertent misinformation in court filings.

Looking Ahead

BriefCatch plans to integrate its verification tools with popular document‑management platforms, making the process seamless for firms that rely on AI for routine drafting tasks. The company also indicated future work on expanding the database to include international case law, broadening the tool’s applicability beyond U.S. courts.
