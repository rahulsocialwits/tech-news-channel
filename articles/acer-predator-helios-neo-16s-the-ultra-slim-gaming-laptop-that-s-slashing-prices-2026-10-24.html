Acer Predator Helios Neo 16S: The Ultra‑Slim Gaming Laptop That’s Slashing Prices

2026-10-24 · 3 min read
Acer’s new Predator Helios Neo 16S offers a 240 Hz QHD+ OLED display, Intel Core Ultra 9 and RTX 5070 Ti, with Best Buy discounts up to $800.

Acer has just launched the Predator Helios Neo 16S, a gaming laptop that packs a punch while keeping the chassis ultra‑slim. The device is powered by Intel’s 24‑core Core Ultra 9 processor and Nvidia’s RTX 5070 Ti graphics, and it comes with a 240 Hz QHD+ OLED display that promises buttery‑smooth visuals for competitive play.

Design and Display

The Helios Neo 16S measures only 16.2 inches diagonally, yet it delivers a QHD+ resolution of 2560 × 1440 pixels. The OLED panel supports a 240 Hz refresh rate, a feature that is rare in laptops of this size. The thin bezel design gives the screen a near‑borderless look, while the chassis itself is constructed from a lightweight aluminum alloy that keeps the overall weight under 2.5 kg.

Performance Core

Under the hood, the laptop houses Intel’s 24‑core Core Ultra 9, a processor that blends high‑performance cores with power‑efficient ones. This architecture allows the Helios Neo 16S to handle demanding games and multitasking workloads without excessive heat or battery drain. Paired with the RTX 5070 Ti, the system can run the latest titles at high settings, thanks to the GPU’s 24 GB of GDDR6 memory and 12 GB of dedicated VRAM.

Memory and Storage Options

Customers can choose between 16 GB or 32 GB of DDR5 RAM. The 16 GB model is priced at $2,225, while the 32 GB variant is available for $2,400. Both configurations come with a 1 TB NVMe SSD, ensuring fast load times for games and applications.

Discounts and Availability

Best Buy is offering discounts of up to $800 on the Helios Neo 16S. The 16 GB RAM model can be purchased for $1,425, and the 32 GB version is available for $1,600. These price cuts make the laptop competitive against other high‑end gaming machines that typically start above $3,000.

Why It Matters

For gamers who want a portable machine that doesn’t compromise on performance, the Helios Neo 16S delivers a compelling package. The combination of a high‑refresh OLED display, powerful CPU and GPU, and aggressive pricing could shift the balance in the ultra‑compact gaming laptop market.
