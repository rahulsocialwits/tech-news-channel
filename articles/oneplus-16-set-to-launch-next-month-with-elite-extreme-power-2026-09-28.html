OnePlus 16 Set to Launch Next Month with Elite Extreme Power

2026-09-28 · 3 min read
OnePlus announces the OnePlus 16, slated for release next month, featuring a new Elite Extreme power system and a limited availability outside the US and Europe.

OnePlus has officially confirmed that the OnePlus 16 will hit the market next month. The company’s announcement comes after a series of leaks that hinted at a new flagship model with a powerful new chipset and a refreshed design. According to the company’s statement, the OnePlus 16 will be powered by the Elite Extreme power system, a new generation of processors that promises higher performance and improved battery efficiency.

Limited Availability

OnePlus has also clarified that the OnePlus 16 will not be available in Europe or the United States. The company cited supply chain constraints and regional regulatory requirements as the reasons for the limited rollout. Fans in those markets will have to wait for a future release or look to the OnePlus 16’s successor.

What to Expect

While the company has not released a full spec sheet, early reports suggest that the OnePlus 16 will feature a 6.7‑inch AMOLED display, a triple‑camera setup, and a 5,000‑mAh battery. The Elite Extreme power system is expected to deliver up to 30% more performance than its predecessor while maintaining a similar power draw.

Release Timeline

OnePlus has not yet announced a specific launch date, but the company’s statement indicates that the device will be available in the second quarter of 2027. Preorders are expected to begin shortly after the official launch announcement.
