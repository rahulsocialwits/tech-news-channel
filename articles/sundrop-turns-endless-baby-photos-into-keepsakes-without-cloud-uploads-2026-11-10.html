Sundrop Turns Endless Baby Photos Into Keepsakes Without Cloud Uploads

2026-11-10 · 3 min read
New iPhone app Sundrop uses on‑device AI to help parents create photo recaps, time‑lapses and more, keeping images local and private.

Parents who find themselves scrolling through thousands of baby photos can now let a new iPhone app do the heavy lifting. Sundrop, launched this week, automatically curates and stitches together a child’s photo archive into recaps, time‑lapses, and other keepsakes—all without sending images to the cloud.

On‑Device Intelligence

The app relies on Apple’s on‑device machine‑learning framework, which means every photo stays on the phone. Sundrop scans the photo library, identifies key moments, and generates short videos or slideshows that can be shared directly from the device.

Privacy‑First Design

Because the processing happens locally, users can be confident that no personal data is transmitted to external servers. This approach addresses growing concerns about data security and privacy in mobile photography.

Practical Use Cases

Beyond simple photo organization, Sundrop offers parents a way to preserve milestones in a format that’s easy to share with family and friends. The app’s time‑lapse feature condenses months of growth into a few minutes, while the recap mode highlights the most memorable shots.

Availability

Sundrop is available for download on the App Store and is compatible with iPhone models running iOS 17 or later. The developers have emphasized that the app is free to use, with no in‑app purchases required.
