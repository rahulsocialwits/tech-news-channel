Yandex Cloud Recovers 97% of Customer Data After Ukrainian Drone Strike

2026-11-13 · 3 min read
Yandex Cloud reports restoring the majority of customer data following a drone attack that caused significant data loss.

Yandex Cloud, the Russian cloud‑service provider, announced that it has restored access to more than 97% of customer data after a series of Ukrainian drone strikes damaged its infrastructure. The company said that nearly 3% of its data was lost during the attacks, but it has since opened secret availability zones to mitigate future disruptions.

Impact of the Drone Strikes

The strikes caused extensive damage to Yandex’s data centers, leading to a temporary loss of data for some customers. Yandex Cloud attributed the loss to a lack of user backups, which the company has now addressed by enforcing backup rules by default.

Recovery Efforts

According to the company, the restoration process involved restoring data from backup copies and leveraging its newly opened availability zones. Yandex Cloud emphasized that the majority of customer data was recovered, and it has taken steps to prevent similar incidents in the future.

Industry Response

The incident highlights the growing importance of robust backup strategies and the need for cloud providers to maintain resilient infrastructure in the face of geopolitical risks.
