Sony WH‑1000XM6 Noise‑Cancelling Headphones Drop to $348 in Black Friday Deal

2026-11-19 · 3 min read
Sony’s flagship ANC headphones are now available for $348, a $30 discount from their recent price point, as part of the Black Friday 2026 sale.

For the first time since the launch of the WH‑1000XM6, Sony has slashed the price of its flagship noise‑canceling headphones. The Black Friday 2026 promotion on the Android Authority website lists the headphones at $348, a $30 reduction from the $378 price that was recently advertised. The discount brings the headphones to a level that is competitive with other premium ANC models in the market.

What the Deal Means for Consumers

The WH‑1000XM6 has been praised for its industry‑leading active noise cancellation, comfortable fit, and robust battery life. Sony’s decision to offer a price cut during the holiday shopping season is likely aimed at boosting sales volume and maintaining market share against rivals such as Bose and Apple. The $348 price point makes the headphones more accessible to a broader segment of consumers who have been waiting for a more affordable entry into high‑end ANC technology.

Key Features of the WH‑1000XM6

  • Industry‑leading adaptive noise cancellation that adjusts to the wearer’s environment.
  • Up to 30 hours of battery life on a single charge, with quick‑charge capability.
  • Comfortable, lightweight design with memory‑foam ear cups.
  • Integrated voice assistant support and touch controls.
  • High‑resolution audio support for premium sound quality.

How the Discount Fits into Sony’s Strategy

By reducing the price during the Black Friday period, Sony can stimulate demand and clear inventory before the holiday rush. The company’s marketing team has highlighted the headphones’ value proposition, emphasizing the combination of advanced noise cancellation and premium build quality. The discount also positions Sony favorably against competitors that have maintained higher price points.

What to Expect in the Coming Months

While the current deal is limited to the Black Friday window, Sony has not announced any further price reductions for the WH‑1000XM6. However, the company’s history of periodic price adjustments suggests that the headphones may see additional discounts in the future, especially as newer models are introduced. Consumers who are interested in the WH‑1000XM6 should keep an eye on Sony’s official website and authorized retailers for any upcoming promotions.

Conclusion

The $348 price for Sony’s WH‑1000XM6 noise‑canceling headphones represents a significant opportunity for audiophiles and everyday users alike. With its proven performance and now more affordable price, the headphones are poised to capture a larger share of the premium ANC market during the holiday season.
