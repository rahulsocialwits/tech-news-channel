Apple HomeView Turns Your Kitchen Into a Smart Cooking Studio

2026-11-21 · 3 min read
Apple’s new HomeView cooking feature lets users follow recipes, track ingredients, and get real‑time guidance on its smart display.

Apple’s HomeView, the company’s latest smart display, is stepping up its game in the kitchen with a feature that turns cooking into a guided, interactive experience. The new tool, announced this week, lets users follow recipes step‑by‑step, track ingredient usage, and receive real‑time visual cues right on the HomeView screen.

How It Works

When a recipe is selected, HomeView displays a clear, high‑resolution video of the cooking process. The screen highlights each ingredient as it’s added, and the on‑screen timer syncs with the steps to keep users on track. If a user needs a substitution or a different cooking technique, the display offers instant suggestions based on the recipe’s context.

Why It Matters

Apple’s move into kitchen assistance reflects a broader trend of integrating smart home devices with everyday tasks. By providing a visual, hands‑free guide, HomeView reduces the need to flip between a phone and a cookbook, making cooking more accessible for beginners and more efficient for seasoned chefs.

What’s Next

Apple has hinted that future updates could include voice‑controlled recipe adjustments and integration with its Health app to track nutritional information. For now, HomeView’s cooking feature is available to all users with the latest software update.
