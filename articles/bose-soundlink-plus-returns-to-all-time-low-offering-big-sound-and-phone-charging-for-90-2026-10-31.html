Bose SoundLink Plus Returns to All‑Time Low, Offering Big Sound and Phone Charging for $90

2026-10-31 · 3 min read
The portable Bose SoundLink Plus speaker drops to its lowest price on Amazon, delivering powerful audio and built‑in phone charging for just $90.

Amazon shoppers looking for a budget‑friendly portable speaker have a new reason to celebrate. The Bose SoundLink Plus, a long‑standing favorite for its robust sound and convenient phone‑charging feature, has just hit its all‑time low price on the retailer’s platform. The deal brings the speaker down to $90, a $90 discount from its previous listing and a significant price cut that could make the unit a top choice for budget‑conscious consumers.

What Makes the SoundLink Plus Stand Out

The SoundLink Plus is known for its powerful audio output, delivering clear highs and deep bass in a compact, handheld design. In addition to its sound quality, the speaker includes a built‑in USB‑C port that allows users to charge their smartphones or other devices while the speaker is in use. This dual functionality has made the SoundLink Plus a popular choice for travelers, outdoor enthusiasts, and anyone who wants a reliable audio companion without the need for a separate charger.

Price Drop Details

According to Android Authority, the speaker’s price has fallen to $90 on Amazon, marking its lowest point in the product’s history. The discount is significant, especially when compared to the speaker’s original MSRP of $180. The price reduction is part of a broader trend of electronics retailers offering steep discounts on popular audio products during the holiday season, as sellers look to clear inventory and attract price‑sensitive shoppers.

Why the Deal Matters

For consumers, the $90 price point makes the SoundLink Plus an attractive option for those who want Bose’s reputation for sound quality without the premium price tag. The speaker’s built‑in charging capability also adds value, allowing users to keep their devices powered while enjoying music or podcasts on the go. The deal could also spur increased competition among portable speaker brands, encouraging other manufacturers to offer similar features at competitive prices.

Future Outlook

While the current discount is a short‑term opportunity, it highlights the importance of price sensitivity in the consumer electronics market. As new audio technologies emerge and competitors introduce innovative features, Bose will need to maintain its brand appeal while staying competitive on price. The SoundLink Plus’s recent price drop may signal a strategic move to keep the product relevant in a crowded market.
