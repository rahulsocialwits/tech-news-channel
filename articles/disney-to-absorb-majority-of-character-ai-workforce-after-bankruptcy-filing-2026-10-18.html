Disney to Absorb Majority of Character.AI Workforce After Bankruptcy Filing

2026-10-18 · 3 min read
Disney has agreed to hire most of the former Character.AI staff following the chatbot startup’s bankruptcy, with its ex‑CEO now serving as Disney’s first CTO.

After filing for bankruptcy earlier this week, chatbot startup Character.AI announced that Disney will absorb the majority of its workforce. The move follows Disney’s earlier agreement to acquire some of Character.AI’s assets for $20 million.

Leadership Transition

Karandeep Anand, Character.AI’s former CEO, has joined Disney as its first Chief Technology Officer. Anand’s appointment signals Disney’s intent to integrate the startup’s conversational AI expertise into its entertainment portfolio.

Employment Impact

Sources say that most of Character.AI’s employees will receive job offers from Disney, providing continuity for the team that built the company’s flagship chatbot platform. The transition is expected to preserve the talent that drove Character.AI’s rapid growth.

Strategic Implications

Disney’s acquisition of Character.AI’s assets and talent aligns with its broader strategy to expand its content creation capabilities through advanced AI. The partnership may accelerate the development of new interactive experiences across Disney’s streaming and theme‑park properties.
