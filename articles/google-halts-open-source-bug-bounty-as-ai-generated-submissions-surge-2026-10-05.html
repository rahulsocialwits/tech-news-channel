Google Halts Open Source Bug Bounty as AI‑Generated Submissions Surge

2026-10-05 · 3 min read
Google pauses its open source bug bounty program after a spike in AI‑generated vulnerability reports, citing quality concerns and the need to refine its review process.

Google has temporarily suspended its open source bug bounty program, citing a significant rise in submissions generated by artificial intelligence. The move follows a wave of reports that the company received in the past month, many of which were flagged as low‑quality or duplicate findings.

Why the pause?

According to a TechCrunch report, the influx of AI‑driven bug reports has overwhelmed the program’s triage system. The company stated that the sheer volume of submissions has made it difficult to distinguish genuine vulnerabilities from noise, prompting a halt while it reassesses its review workflow.

Impact on the community

Bug bounty hunters and open source contributors have expressed concern that the pause could delay the discovery and patching of critical security flaws. However, Google has assured the community that it is working on new guidelines to better filter AI‑generated reports and to maintain the integrity of the program.

Next steps

Google plans to release updated submission criteria and enhanced verification tools in the coming weeks. The company also announced that it will collaborate with external security researchers to develop best practices for AI‑assisted vulnerability discovery.
