Bose Recalls Over 670,000 S1 Pro Battery Packs Amid Fire and Overheating Concerns

2026-11-06 · 3 min read
Bose has issued a recall for its S1 Pro battery packs after reports of fire and overheating incidents, affecting more than 670,000 units in the U.S. and Canada.

Bose has announced a recall of its S1 Pro battery packs after a series of incidents involving fire and overheating. The company said that more than 670,000 units sold in the United States and Canada are affected, and it is offering a free replacement for every recalled pack.

What Triggered the Recall?

According to the company’s statement, the recall follows reports from users who experienced overheating and, in some cases, fire when using the S1 Pro battery packs with compatible devices. Bose has not identified a specific defect but has urged owners to stop using the packs immediately and to contact customer support for a replacement.

How to Get a Replacement

Owners can visit Bose’s official recall page or call the customer service hotline to arrange for a free replacement battery pack. The company has set up a dedicated support line to handle inquiries and to expedite the replacement process.

Impact on Consumers

While the recall covers a large number of units, Bose has stated that no injuries have been reported. The company is working to ensure that all affected customers receive a new battery pack without charge.
