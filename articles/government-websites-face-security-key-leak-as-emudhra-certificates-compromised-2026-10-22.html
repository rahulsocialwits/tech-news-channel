Government Websites Face Security Key Leak as eMudhra Certificates Compromised

2026-10-22 · 3 min read
A widespread leak of eMudhra web security keys threatens the integrity of numerous Indian government sites, exposing users to potential fraud and data breaches.

India’s digital infrastructure is under threat after a significant leak of eMudhra web security keys was discovered. eMudhra, a leading provider of digital certificates, supplies the cryptographic keys that enable secure HTTPS connections for a wide range of government websites. The breach means that attackers could potentially intercept or forge traffic to these sites, undermining user trust and exposing sensitive data.

What the Leak Involves

The leak includes private keys that are used to sign SSL/TLS certificates. These keys are essential for establishing encrypted connections between users’ browsers and government servers. If an attacker obtains a private key, they can create a counterfeit certificate that browsers will trust, allowing them to perform man‑in‑the‑middle attacks or impersonate official sites.

Impact on Government Services

Many critical services—such as tax filing portals, land registration systems, and public procurement platforms—rely on eMudhra certificates to guarantee secure communication. The compromise could enable attackers to redirect users to malicious sites, steal login credentials, or inject malware into legitimate traffic.

Response from Authorities

Following the discovery, the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology has issued an advisory urging all affected agencies to revoke compromised certificates immediately and replace them with new ones. The eMudhra team has also announced a comprehensive audit of its key management processes and is working with cybersecurity firms to strengthen its infrastructure.

Preventive Measures for Users

While the government works to mitigate the breach, users should remain vigilant. Signs of a compromised site include unexpected security warnings, mismatched URLs, or unfamiliar certificate details. If you encounter such alerts, avoid entering personal information and report the incident to the relevant government department.

Broader Implications for Digital Trust

This incident highlights the critical importance of robust key management and regular security audits for certificate authorities. As more services move online, ensuring the integrity of the underlying cryptographic infrastructure becomes paramount to protect both users and public institutions.
