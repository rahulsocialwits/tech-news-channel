Peak XV Expands Surge Cohort, Raising Seed Investment Ceiling to $5M

2026-09-29 · 3 min read
Peak XV boosts its Surge program, adding 18 startups and a $5M seed cap, with a focus on global markets and a strong Indian presence.

Peak XV, the venture fund behind the popular accelerator program Surge, announced today that it has increased the seed investment ceiling for its newest cohort to $5 million. The move comes as the firm expands its 18‑startup cohort, adding a mix of early‑stage companies that are targeting global markets.

Global Reach and Indian Focus

Of the 18 companies selected for the latest Surge cohort, thirteen are aiming for international expansion. More than half of the startups are based in India, reflecting the country’s growing role as a hub for tech innovation. The cohort’s mix of founders and industries is designed to foster cross‑border collaboration and accelerate product launches in key markets.

What the $5M Ceiling Means

By raising the seed investment cap, Peak XV is positioning itself to compete with larger accelerators that offer more substantial early funding. The $5 million ceiling allows the firm to provide deeper capital support, enabling startups to focus on product development, hiring, and market entry without the pressure of securing additional rounds too early.

Next Steps for the Cohort

Selected companies will join the Surge program in early 2027, where they will receive mentorship, office space, and access to a network of investors and industry partners. The cohort’s launch is scheduled for the first quarter of 2027, with a public demo day planned for the end of the year.
