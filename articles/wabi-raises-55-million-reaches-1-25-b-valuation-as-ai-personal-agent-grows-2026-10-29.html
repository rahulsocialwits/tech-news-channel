Wabi Raises $55 Million, Reaches $1.25 B Valuation as AI Personal Agent Grows

2026-10-29 · 3 min read
AI startup Wabi secured a $55 million round, boosting its valuation to $1.25 billion and expanding its personal‑agent platform.

AI startup Wabi announced on Thursday that it has closed a $55 million funding round, pushing its valuation to $1.25 billion. The company, which markets itself as a “personal agent,” said the new capital will accelerate the development of its mini‑app ecosystem and broaden its user base.

Growth of the Personal‑Agent Market

Wabi’s platform lets users create and deploy small, task‑specific applications that run inside a single interface. By focusing on modularity, the company claims it can reduce the friction that often accompanies larger AI tools. The fresh funding comes at a time when the personal‑agent niche is attracting attention from investors looking for the next wave of consumer‑facing AI.

Use of the New Capital

According to the company, the $55 million will be used to expand its engineering team, enhance the underlying AI models, and invest in marketing to drive adoption among both individual users and small businesses. Wabi also plans to integrate additional data sources to improve the accuracy of its recommendations.

Industry Context

Wabi’s valuation places it among the most valuable AI startups in the personal‑assistant space. The company’s growth mirrors broader trends in the industry, where investors are increasingly backing firms that offer specialized, user‑friendly AI solutions rather than generic large‑language models.

Looking Ahead

With the new funding, Wabi aims to launch a suite of pre‑built mini‑apps for common productivity tasks by the end of the year. The company also hinted at potential partnerships with major cloud providers to streamline deployment for enterprise customers.
