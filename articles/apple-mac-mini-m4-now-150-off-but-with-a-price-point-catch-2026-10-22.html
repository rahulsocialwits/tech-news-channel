Apple Mac Mini M4 Now $150 Off, but with a Price‑Point Catch

2026-10-22 · 3 min read
Apple’s latest Mac Mini M4 is discounted by $150, yet its price remains higher than the original launch cost.

Apple’s compact Mac Mini M4 has entered a new price tier, offering a $150 discount that brings the unit closer to the mid‑range market. The deal, announced on October 22, 2026, targets consumers looking for a powerful yet space‑saving desktop solution.

What the Discount Means

The $150 reduction brings the Mac Mini M4’s price to a level that is still above its launch price. While the discount makes the device more attractive, it also highlights Apple’s strategy of maintaining premium pricing even for older‑generation models.

Key Features of the M4

Built on Apple’s M4 silicon, the Mac Mini delivers strong performance for both everyday tasks and more demanding workloads. The device supports a range of connectivity options, including Thunderbolt 4 and Wi‑Fi 6E, and is compatible with macOS Ventura.

Why the Deal Matters

For buyers, the discount offers a chance to acquire a high‑performance machine at a lower cost, though the price still reflects Apple’s premium positioning. The move may also signal a shift in Apple’s pricing strategy as newer models are introduced.
