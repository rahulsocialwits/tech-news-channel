Nothing Ear Note Earbuds Bring Built‑In AI Note‑Taking to Your Ears

2026-11-25 · 3 min read
Nothing’s new Ear Note earbuds feature on‑device AI that records and transcribes conversations, offering a hands‑free note‑taking experience.

Nothing, the company behind the popular Ear Pop and Ear Pro earbuds, has announced a new pair of in‑ear headphones that go beyond audio performance. The Ear Note earbuds come with a built‑in AI system that records spoken words and automatically transcribes them into text, allowing users to capture meetings, lectures or personal reminders without pulling out a phone or laptop.

How It Works

Each Ear Note unit contains a small microphone array and a dedicated AI processor. When the user activates the note‑taking mode, the earbuds listen to the surrounding audio and use on‑device speech‑to‑text algorithms to generate a transcript in real time. The text can then be synced to the user’s cloud account or shared directly via messaging apps.

Design and Features

The earbuds are marketed as “the best in‑ear audio ever” by Nothing, and the company has highlighted their high‑fidelity drivers and adaptive noise cancellation. The charging case is also designed to support the AI workload, featuring a larger battery and a dedicated charging port for the earbuds’ internal memory.

What’s Next

Nothing has said that more details will be released on December 8, and the company is expected to provide a full product launch event in the coming weeks. Early reviewers have praised the earbuds’ comfort and the novelty of hands‑free note‑taking, but they note that the AI transcription accuracy will be a key factor in user adoption.
