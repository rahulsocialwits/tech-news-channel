Google Wallet Adds Digital Home Keys for NFC‑Enabled Smart Locks

2026-11-12 · 3 min read
Google Wallet now lets users unlock compatible smart locks with a tap, adding a hands‑free option to its digital wallet services.

Google Wallet has expanded its digital key capabilities to include home access. The new feature allows users to store and use digital home keys that work with NFC‑enabled smart locks. When a user taps their phone or smartwatch against a compatible lock, the lock unlocks automatically, providing a seamless, hands‑free entry experience.

How It Works

Digital home keys are stored securely in the Google Wallet app. The lock reads the NFC signal and verifies the key against a cloud‑based authentication service. Once verified, the lock opens, and the key is automatically revoked for the next use, enhancing security.

Benefits for Users

• Convenience: No more keys or codes to remember.

• Security: Keys are time‑limited and can be revoked remotely.

• Integration: Works with existing NFC smart lock ecosystems.

Future Outlook

Google’s move signals a broader push toward a unified digital key ecosystem, potentially extending beyond homes to hotels, offices, and public spaces. The company is also exploring UWB (ultra‑wideband) for even more precise proximity detection.
