From Sticky to Sleek: How a DIY E‑Ink Apple TV Remote Is Turning Heads

2026-11-15 · 3 min read
A hobbyist has transformed a reTerminal Sticky into a stylish e‑ink remote for Apple TV, showcasing the growing trend of custom gadget hacks.

When a community of makers first introduced the reTerminal Sticky, it was celebrated for its compact design and versatile touch‑screen capabilities. Now, a creative enthusiast has taken the device a step further, converting it into a sleek e‑ink remote for Apple TV. The transformation demonstrates how readily available hardware can be repurposed into a polished, niche accessory.

What Makes the ReTerminal Sticky Special?

The reTerminal Sticky is a small, battery‑powered tablet that runs a lightweight Linux distribution. Its 4.3‑inch e‑ink display offers low power consumption and excellent readability in bright light. The device also features a built‑in camera, Wi‑Fi, and a range of GPIO pins, making it a popular choice for DIY projects.

The Hack: Turning a Tablet into a Remote

The maker’s project involved installing a custom firmware that turns the Sticky’s touch interface into a remote control. By mapping the screen to Apple TV’s infrared (IR) protocol, the device can send commands such as play, pause, and volume adjustments. The e‑ink display displays a minimalist interface, allowing users to see the current channel or menu without draining the battery.

Why It Matters

Custom gadget hacks like this highlight the flexibility of open‑hardware platforms. They also provide a low‑cost alternative to official accessories, appealing to tech enthusiasts who value personalization. The project showcases how a single piece of hardware can be adapted for multiple uses, extending its lifespan and utility.

Community Impact

Projects such as this inspire others in maker communities to experiment with reTerminal Sticky and similar devices. By sharing detailed guides and firmware, creators encourage a culture of innovation and resourcefulness. The result is a growing ecosystem of custom accessories that complement mainstream consumer electronics.
