TCL’s Paper‑Like 11.5‑inch Tablet Hits Black Friday with 38% Off for Prime Members

2026-11-21 · 3 min read
TCL’s NXTPAPER 11 Plus, a 11.5‑inch paper‑like tablet with stylus and flip case, is discounted 38% on Amazon for Prime members during Black Friday sales.

For shoppers looking for a unique tablet experience this Black Friday, TCL has a deal that stands out. The company’s NXTPAPER 11 Plus, a 11.5‑inch tablet that mimics the feel of paper, is now 38% off on Amazon for Prime members. The discount brings the price down to $142, a significant saving compared to the original $232 list price.

What Makes the NXTPAPER 11 Plus Different?

The tablet’s key selling point is its paper‑like display. Unlike typical LCD or OLED screens, the NXTPAPER uses a technology that reduces glare and offers a matte finish, making it easier on the eyes for extended reading or note‑taking sessions. The device comes with a stylus and a flip case, allowing users to write directly on the screen and protect the tablet when not in use.

Black Friday Timing and Availability

Amazon’s Black Friday promotion is scheduled to run from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM on November 24th. Prime members can take advantage of the 38% discount during this window, while non‑Prime shoppers will see a smaller markdown of 20% on the same day. The offer is limited to the NXTPAPER 11 Plus model and is not available for other TCL tablets.

Why the Deal Matters for Tech Buyers

Black Friday is traditionally a time for consumers to snag high‑tech gadgets at reduced prices. The NXTPAPER 11 Plus offers a niche product that blends the tactile experience of paper with the convenience of a tablet. For students, writers, and professionals who prefer a more natural writing surface, the discount makes the device an attractive option.

How to Get the Deal

To secure the 38% discount, shoppers need to be Amazon Prime members and place their order during the specified Black Friday window. The product page on Amazon lists the discounted price and includes a countdown timer that shows how long the offer will remain active. Once the sale ends, the price reverts to the standard $232.

Conclusion

TCL’s NXTPAPER 11 Plus is a standout tablet for those who value a paper‑like experience. With a 38% discount for Prime members during Black Friday, the device offers a compelling price point for anyone looking to combine the familiarity of writing on paper with the versatility of a modern tablet.
