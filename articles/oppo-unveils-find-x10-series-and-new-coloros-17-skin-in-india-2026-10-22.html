Oppo Unveils Find X10 Series and New ColorOS 17 Skin in India

2026-10-22 · 3 min read
Oppo launches the Find X10 Pro Max, Find X10, Pad Mini and Enco X4, introducing ColorOS 17 based on Android 17 in India.

Oppo has rolled out a fresh lineup of smartphones and accessories in India, marking the debut of its new operating skin, ColorOS 17, which is built on Android 17. The company unveiled the flagship Find X10 Pro Max, the mid‑range Find X10, the compact Pad Mini tablet, and the Enco X4 earbuds.

ColorOS 17 and Android 17 Integration

ColorOS 17 brings a host of visual and functional updates, including a redesigned home screen, enhanced privacy controls, and deeper integration with Android 17’s new features. Oppo plans to extend the skin to other devices in the coming months.

Hardware Highlights

  • Find X10 Pro Max – The flagship model boasts a high‑resolution display, advanced camera system, and fast charging capabilities.
  • Find X10 – A mid‑range option that balances performance and affordability.
  • Pad Mini – A lightweight tablet aimed at casual users and students.
  • Enco X4 – Wireless earbuds featuring improved battery life and noise cancellation.

Market Impact

By introducing ColorOS 17 and expanding its device portfolio, Oppo aims to strengthen its position in the competitive Indian market. The launch aligns with the company’s strategy to offer a cohesive ecosystem across smartphones, tablets, and accessories.
