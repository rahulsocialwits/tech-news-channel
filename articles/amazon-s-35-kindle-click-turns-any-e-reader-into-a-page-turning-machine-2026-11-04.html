Amazon’s $35 Kindle Click Turns Any e‑Reader Into a Page‑Turning Machine

2026-11-04 · 3 min read
A low‑cost accessory lets Kindle users enjoy a physical page‑turning feel without buying a new device.

Amazon has released a new $35 accessory that turns any compatible e‑reader into a page‑turning experience. The Kindle Click is a small, lightweight attachment that snaps onto the back of a Kindle or other e‑reader, providing a tactile button that simulates the feel of turning a page. The device is designed to work with the latest Kindle models, including the Kindle Paperwhite and Kindle Oasis, and is also compatible with other e‑readers that support the same interface.

How It Works

The Click uses a simple mechanical lever that, when pressed, sends a signal to the e‑reader to advance the page. The lever is engineered to mimic the resistance and motion of a real book page, giving readers a more natural reading rhythm. The accessory is powered by the e‑reader’s existing battery, so there is no need for additional power sources or batteries.

Why It Matters

Many readers have expressed frustration with the lack of a physical page‑turning feel on digital devices. The Kindle Click addresses this demand by offering a low‑cost, easy‑to‑install solution that enhances the reading experience without the need to purchase a new device. The accessory is expected to appeal to avid readers who value the tactile sensation of turning pages, as well as to those who prefer a more traditional reading experience.

Availability and Pricing

The Kindle Click is available for purchase on Amazon’s website and is priced at $35. The accessory is expected to ship within a few days of ordering, and Amazon is offering a limited‑time discount for early adopters. The company has not announced any plans to release a similar product for other e‑reader brands.
