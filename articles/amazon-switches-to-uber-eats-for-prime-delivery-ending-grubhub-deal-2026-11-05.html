Amazon Switches to Uber Eats for Prime Delivery, Ending GrubHub Deal

2026-11-05 · 3 min read
Amazon has announced Uber Eats as its new official restaurant delivery partner for Prime members in the United States, replacing GrubHub.

Amazon has officially announced that it will partner with Uber Eats to provide restaurant delivery services for Prime members across the United States. The move marks the end of a multi‑year relationship with GrubHub, which had served as Amazon’s delivery partner for Prime customers. The new partnership will allow Prime members to order from a wide range of restaurants through the Amazon app and receive their meals via Uber Eats’ delivery network.

Why the Switch Matters

The change reflects Amazon’s ongoing strategy to streamline its delivery ecosystem and leverage Uber Eats’ extensive logistics network. By integrating Uber Eats into the Prime experience, Amazon can offer faster delivery times and a broader selection of restaurants, potentially increasing customer satisfaction and retention.

Impact on Prime Members

Prime members will see the Uber Eats option appear in the Amazon app’s food ordering section. Orders placed through Uber Eats will be tracked in the same way as other Prime deliveries, with real‑time updates and the same Prime benefits, such as free delivery on eligible orders. The transition is expected to be seamless for users, with no immediate changes to pricing or membership requirements.

Industry Reactions

Industry analysts note that Amazon’s partnership with Uber Eats could intensify competition in the food delivery market, where companies like DoorDash, GrubHub, and Postmates also vie for market share. The move may prompt other retailers to reevaluate their delivery partnerships and explore similar collaborations to enhance customer experience.
