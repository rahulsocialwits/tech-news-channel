Samsung and Google Unveil Co‑Developed Smart Glasses with Built‑In Camera and Open‑Ear Audio

2026-11-14 · 3 min read
Samsung and Google launch a new pair of smart glasses featuring a high‑definition camera, open‑ear speakers and directional microphones for natural interaction.

Samsung and Google have announced a new line of smart glasses that combine advanced imaging, audio and connectivity features in a lightweight, wearable package. The eyewear, which is the result of a joint effort between the two companies, is designed to let users capture photos and video, listen to audio, and interact with their surroundings without the need for a separate device.

Key Features

  • High‑definition camera – The glasses include a built‑in camera capable of capturing 4K video and high‑resolution stills, allowing users to record moments hands‑free.
  • Open‑ear speakers – Instead of blocking the ears, the glasses use open‑ear technology that delivers sound directly to the listener while still allowing ambient noise to be heard.
  • Directional microphones – Multiple microphones pick up speech from the wearer’s mouth, enabling clear voice commands and hands‑free communication.

Use Cases

With these features, the glasses are positioned for a range of applications, from field reporting and content creation to hands‑free navigation and augmented‑reality overlays. The collaboration between Samsung and Google suggests a focus on seamless integration with existing ecosystems, potentially allowing the eyewear to sync with smartphones, tablets and cloud services.

Availability and Pricing

While the announcement did not disclose a launch date, Samsung indicated that the smart glasses will be available for purchase later this year. Pricing details are expected to follow as the product moves closer to release.
