Nvidia Explores Repurposing Decommissioned German Nuclear Sites as AI Data Centers

2026-10-25 · 3 min read
Nvidia and PreussenElektra are studying whether old German nuclear power plant sites can be converted into AI data centers, potentially cutting build time and cost by reusing existing grid connections and infrastructure.

Nvidia is looking beyond traditional data‑center sites in a bid to accelerate the rollout of AI infrastructure. In a partnership with German energy company PreussenElektra, the company is evaluating decommissioned nuclear power plant sites for conversion into large‑scale AI data centers.

Why old nuclear sites?

These sites already have robust grid connections, ample land, and industrial infrastructure that can support the high power and cooling demands of AI workloads. By reusing existing facilities, Nvidia could reduce the time and capital required to build a new data center from scratch.

Potential benefits

Reusing shut‑down nuclear sites could shorten development timelines and lower costs. The approach also aligns with sustainability goals, as the sites can be repurposed rather than left idle.

Next steps

Nvidia and PreussenElektra are conducting feasibility studies to assess the technical and regulatory requirements for such a conversion. If successful, the project could set a new model for future AI data‑center deployments.
