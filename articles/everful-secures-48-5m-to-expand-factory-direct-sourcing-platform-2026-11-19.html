Everful Secures $48.5M to Expand Factory‑Direct Sourcing Platform

2026-11-19 · 3 min read
Former Alibaba executive launches Everful, raising $48.5 million to help retailers source products directly from Chinese factories.

In a move that signals a shift toward more direct supply‑chain relationships, Everful, a factory‑direct sourcing platform founded by a former Alibaba executive, has closed a $48.5 million round. The funding will allow the company to broaden its reach and streamline the process for retailers looking to source products from overseas manufacturers.

How Everful Works

Everful’s platform connects retailers with Chinese factories, cutting out middlemen and reducing lead times. By providing a digital marketplace where manufacturers can showcase their capabilities and retailers can place orders directly, the service aims to lower costs and increase transparency across the supply chain.

Funding Details

The round was led by a group of strategic investors who see potential in the growing demand for direct sourcing solutions. The capital will be used to expand the platform’s technology stack, enhance its user interface, and support marketing efforts to attract more retailers and manufacturers.

Industry Context

Supply‑chain disruptions over the past years have highlighted the need for more resilient sourcing models. Companies that can quickly pivot to new suppliers or adjust order volumes are better positioned to meet consumer demand. Everful’s model addresses this by offering a flexible, data‑driven approach to sourcing.

Future Outlook

With the new funding, Everful plans to launch additional features such as real‑time inventory tracking and automated compliance checks. These enhancements are expected to further reduce friction for retailers and improve the overall efficiency of the sourcing process.
