Aina Smart Ring Adds Tiny Display to Manage AI Agents

2026-11-06 · 3 min read
The $400 Aina smart ring offers a miniature screen for controlling AI assistants on iPhone and Mac, though Android support is missing.

The Aina smart ring, priced at $400, introduces a small on‑ring display that lets users interact with AI agents directly from their wrist. The ring is designed to work with Apple devices, offering quick access to voice assistants and other AI‑powered services. While the device supports iPhone and Mac, Android compatibility is currently absent, limiting its appeal to the broader smartwatch market.

Key Features

  • Miniature Display: A tiny screen on the ring shows status updates and allows quick taps to trigger AI actions.
  • Cross‑Platform Integration: Works seamlessly with iOS and macOS, enabling users to manage AI agents without pulling out their phone.
  • Limited Android Support: Android users cannot yet use the ring, which may affect adoption among non‑Apple ecosystems.

Market Context

Smart rings have struggled to gain traction, with most devices focusing on fitness tracking or simple notifications. Aina’s attempt to combine AI control with a wearable format could differentiate it, but the lack of Android support may hinder widespread adoption. The $400 price point also places it above many competing wearables, raising questions about value for consumers.
