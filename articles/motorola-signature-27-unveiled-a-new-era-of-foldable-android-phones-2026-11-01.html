Motorola Signature 27 Unveiled: A New Era of Foldable Android Phones

2026-11-01 · 3 min read
Motorola’s Signature 27 drops a 7.6‑inch OLED foldable with a 120 Hz refresh rate, a 120 MP main camera, and a 5000‑mAh battery, promising a premium experience at a competitive price.

Motorola has officially revealed the Signature 27, its first foldable Android phone, in a video that drops a host of new specifications and design details. The device is slated for launch next week and is positioned as a direct competitor to the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 5 and the Google Pixel Fold.

Design and Display

The Signature 27 features a 7.6‑inch OLED display that folds in half, offering a 120 Hz refresh rate for smooth scrolling and gaming. The outer screen measures 6.2 inches, while the inner screen expands to 7.6 inches when unfolded. Motorola has highlighted a new “Tandem OLED” panel that promises better color accuracy and lower power consumption compared to previous models.

Camera System

Under the hood, the phone houses a 120 MP primary sensor, a 12 MP ultra‑wide lens, and a 10 MP telephoto unit. The camera setup is designed to deliver high‑resolution photos and 4K video recording at 60 fps. Motorola claims that the new sensor will outperform the flagship cameras of rival brands in low‑light conditions.

Performance and Battery

The device is powered by a Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 processor, paired with 12 GB of RAM and 256 GB of internal storage. A 5000‑mAh battery supports 45 W wired charging and 15 W wireless charging, with a claim of 50 % charge in 30 minutes under optimal conditions.

Software and Features

Running Android 14 with Motorola’s custom skin, the Signature 27 introduces a new “Fold‑Ready” UI that automatically adjusts app layouts when the device is opened or closed. The phone also supports 5G connectivity, Wi‑Fi 6E, and Bluetooth 5.3.

Price and Availability

Motorola has set the launch price at $1,299, positioning it as a more affordable alternative to the higher‑priced foldables on the market. The phone will be available in the United States, Canada, and select European countries starting on November 15.

Industry Reaction

Tech reviewers have praised the Signature 27’s display quality and camera performance, noting that the device offers a compelling balance between premium features and price. Some critics, however, point out that the 5000‑mAh battery may struggle to keep up with the power demands of a 120 Hz foldable screen during extended use.
