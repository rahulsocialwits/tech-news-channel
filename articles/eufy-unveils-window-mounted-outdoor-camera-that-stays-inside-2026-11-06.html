Eufy Unveils Window‑Mounted Outdoor Camera That Stays Inside

2026-11-06 · 3 min read
Eufy’s new $80 E10 camera sticks to windows, pointing outward for discreet outdoor security.

Eufy has released a new outdoor security camera that takes a different approach to the traditional wall‑mounted design. The $80 E10 model is a small, lightweight unit that attaches directly to a window frame, using a suction‑based system to stay in place. Once mounted, the camera points outward, allowing it to monitor the exterior of a home while remaining hidden inside the building.

Design and Features

The E10’s compact size and low profile make it easy to install on any standard window. It comes with a 1080p HD sensor, a 120‑degree field of view, and night‑vision capability. The camera also supports motion detection and can send alerts to a paired smartphone app. Users can view live footage or recorded clips directly from the app, which offers basic editing tools and cloud storage options.

Installation and Use

Installing the E10 is straightforward: users simply clean the window surface, apply the suction cup, and slide the camera into place. The suction system is designed to hold the camera securely for months, but can be removed without leaving residue. Because the camera sits inside the house, it is less visible to potential intruders, providing a subtle security solution for apartments and homes with limited wall space.

Market Context

The launch comes as demand for affordable, easy‑to‑install security cameras continues to rise. Eufy, a subsidiary of Anker, has positioned the E10 as a budget alternative to larger, more expensive outdoor cameras. By offering a product that blends indoor aesthetics with outdoor functionality, the company aims to capture a niche segment of homeowners looking for discreet surveillance.
