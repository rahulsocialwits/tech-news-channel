iBUYPOWER Slate: The RTX 5060 Gaming PC That Beats the Steam Machine in Price

2026-11-08 · 3 min read
iBUYPOWER’s Slate pre‑built PC, powered by an RTX 5060 and a Ryzen 5, offers gamers a budget‑friendly alternative to the Steam Machine, now available for $899 after a $500 discount.

For gamers on a budget, the iBUYPOWER Slate pre‑built PC has emerged as a compelling alternative to the Steam Machine. The Slate, now available for $899 after a $500 discount, is powered by an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5060 graphics card, a Ryzen 5 processor, 16 GB of DDR5 memory, and a 1 TB SSD. The combination of these components delivers solid performance for modern titles while keeping the price well below that of the Steam Machine’s $1,399 price point.

Why the Slate Stands Out

The key to the Slate’s appeal lies in its balance of performance and affordability. The RTX 5060, part of NVIDIA’s newer generation of GPUs, offers ray‑tracing support and DLSS 3.0, allowing gamers to enjoy higher frame rates and visual fidelity without the premium price tag of higher‑end cards. Paired with a Ryzen 5 CPU, the Slate can handle demanding games such as Cyberpunk 2077 and Red Dead Redemption 2 at 1080p with medium to high settings.

Competitive Pricing

Best Buy’s $500 discount brings the Slate’s price to $899, a significant reduction from its original MSRP of $1,399. This price point makes it an attractive option for students, entry‑level gamers, and anyone looking to upgrade from a mid‑range system without breaking the bank. The discount also positions the Slate directly against the Steam Machine, which has struggled to gain traction in the market due to its higher price and limited game library.

Build Quality and Design

The Slate’s chassis features a sleek, minimalist design with a tempered glass side panel that showcases the internal components. The system includes a 120 mm front intake fan and a 140 mm rear exhaust fan, ensuring adequate airflow to keep the GPU and CPU temperatures in check during extended gaming sessions. The included 500 W power supply is sufficient for the current configuration, but users can upgrade to a higher wattage unit if they plan to add more storage or a higher‑end GPU in the future.

Software and Connectivity

The Slate ships with Windows 11 Home pre‑installed, along with a suite of drivers and utilities from NVIDIA and AMD. The system supports Wi‑Fi 6 and Bluetooth 5.2, providing fast wireless connectivity for peripherals and online gaming. Users can also connect to external displays via HDMI 2.1 and DisplayPort 1.4, enabling multi‑monitor setups for productivity or immersive gaming experiences.

Future‑Proofing

While the RTX 5060 is a mid‑range GPU, its support for DLSS 3.0 and hardware‑accelerated ray tracing ensures that the Slate remains relevant as game developers continue to push the boundaries of visual fidelity. The inclusion of DDR5 memory also positions the system for future upgrades, as DDR5 becomes the new standard for high‑performance computing.

Conclusion

For gamers who want a capable system without the premium price of high‑end builds, the iBUYPOWER Slate offers a compelling package. With a powerful GPU, modern CPU, ample memory, and a generous discount, it provides a strong alternative to the Steam Machine and other pre‑built options in the market.
