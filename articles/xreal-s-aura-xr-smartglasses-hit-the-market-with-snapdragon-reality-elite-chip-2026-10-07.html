Xreal’s Aura XR Smartglasses Hit the Market with Snapdragon Reality Elite Chip

2026-10-07 · 3 min read
Xreal launches its Aura XR smartglasses, powered by Qualcomm’s Snapdragon Reality Elite, priced at $1,279 and set to compete with Meta’s new VR glasses.

Xreal has officially released its long‑hyped Aura XR smartglasses, a new entrant in the emerging mixed‑reality market. The device is built around Qualcomm’s Snapdragon Reality Elite chip, a processor specifically designed for XR workloads. According to the company, the chip delivers the performance needed for real‑time rendering and spatial audio while maintaining a low power envelope suitable for a wearable form factor.

Design and Features

The Aura glasses feature a lightweight frame and a high‑resolution display that supports both augmented and virtual reality experiences. Xreal claims the glasses can seamlessly overlay digital content onto the real world, offering a more immersive experience than traditional head‑mounted displays. The device also includes built‑in sensors for eye tracking and hand gesture recognition, allowing users to interact with virtual objects without external controllers.

Pricing and Availability

Priced at $1,279, the Aura XR smartglasses are positioned as a premium product aimed at early adopters and developers. Xreal plans to launch the glasses in the United States and select European markets later this month, with a broader global rollout expected in the first quarter of 2027.

Market Context

Xreal’s entry comes as Meta has recently announced its own VR glasses, sparking a competitive push in the XR space. While Meta’s devices focus on virtual reality, Xreal’s Aura emphasizes a hybrid approach that blends augmented and virtual experiences. The inclusion of Qualcomm’s Reality Elite chip gives the Aura a technical edge in processing power, potentially attracting developers looking for a robust platform for XR applications.
