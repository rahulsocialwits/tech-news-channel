Amazon Unveils Lightweight Kindle Colorsoft with Edge‑to‑Edge Display

2026-11-14 · 3 min read
Amazon’s new Kindle Colorsoft offers a slimmer design and full‑edge screen, but existing owners may find little incentive to upgrade.

Amazon has announced the latest iteration of its e‑reader lineup, the Kindle Colorsoft. The new model is noticeably lighter and thinner than the first‑generation Colorsoft, and it features an edge‑to‑edge display that promises a more immersive reading experience.

Design and Display

According to the company’s press release, the Colorsoft’s new chassis reduces bulk while maintaining the same 7.8‑inch screen size. The edge‑to‑edge design eliminates the traditional bezel, giving users a larger viewable area without increasing the overall footprint.

Performance and Battery Life

While Amazon has not disclosed detailed specifications, the device is expected to run on the same low‑power processor that powers earlier Colorsoft models. Battery life is projected to remain competitive, with the company claiming up to 10 weeks of use on a single charge under typical reading conditions.

Price and Availability

The Kindle Colorsoft is priced at $249, positioning it as a premium option in the e‑reader market. Amazon notes that the new model is available for pre‑order starting today, with shipments expected in early December.

Upgrade Considerations

Existing Colorsoft owners may find the upgrade less compelling, as the core functionality remains largely unchanged. Amazon’s statement emphasizes the device’s lighter weight and improved display as the primary selling points.
