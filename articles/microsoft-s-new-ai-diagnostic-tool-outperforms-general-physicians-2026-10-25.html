Microsoft’s New AI Diagnostic Tool Outperforms General Physicians

2026-10-25 · 3 min read
Microsoft researchers unveil an AI system that diagnoses patients step‑by‑step, achieving double the accuracy of general doctors.

Microsoft researchers have announced a breakthrough in medical AI, developing a diagnostic tool that mimics the sequential reasoning of human doctors. In a study published this week, the system was shown to be more than twice as accurate as general physicians when diagnosing a range of conditions.

How the AI Works

The tool follows a step‑by‑step approach, asking a series of targeted questions and evaluating symptoms in a logical order. This method mirrors the way clinicians gather information during a physical exam, allowing the AI to narrow down possibilities before arriving at a final diagnosis.

Implications for Healthcare

With its higher accuracy, the technology could serve as a valuable second opinion for primary care providers, especially in underserved areas where specialist access is limited. By reducing diagnostic errors, it may also lower the risk of costly misdiagnoses and improve patient outcomes.

Next Steps

Microsoft plans to partner with healthcare institutions to pilot the system in real‑world settings. The company is also exploring ways to integrate the AI into existing electronic health record platforms, ensuring seamless workflow for clinicians.
