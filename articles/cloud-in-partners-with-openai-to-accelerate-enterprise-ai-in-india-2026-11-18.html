Cloud.in Partners with OpenAI to Accelerate Enterprise AI in India

2026-11-18 · 3 min read
Cloud.in, a Hostin Services subsidiary, has joined OpenAI’s Select Partner program, combining OpenAI capabilities with AWS Premier Tier expertise to boost enterprise AI adoption in India.

Cloud.in, the cloud, AI, quantum computing and cybersecurity arm of Hostin Services Private Limited, announced today that it has joined OpenAI’s Select Partner program. The partnership will allow Cloud.in to integrate OpenAI’s advanced language models into its suite of services, while leveraging its existing AWS Premier Tier expertise to deliver enterprise‑grade AI solutions across India.

What the partnership means for Indian enterprises

By combining OpenAI’s cutting‑edge generative AI capabilities with Cloud.in’s deep knowledge of AWS infrastructure, the collaboration aims to lower the barrier for Indian businesses to adopt AI. Cloud.in will provide end‑to‑end support, from model deployment and fine‑tuning to compliance and governance, ensuring that enterprises can quickly build and scale AI applications.

Key benefits highlighted by Cloud.in

Cloud.in’s CEO explained that the partnership will enable clients to:

  • Access OpenAI’s GPT models through a single, managed platform.
  • Benefit from AWS’s robust security and compliance frameworks.
  • Receive dedicated support for AI strategy, architecture, and implementation.

Industry context

India’s AI market is growing rapidly, with a surge in demand for natural language processing, automation, and data analytics solutions. The OpenAI Select Partner program is designed to help local providers deliver these capabilities at scale, and Cloud.in’s entry into the program positions it as a key player in the region’s AI ecosystem.

Next steps

Cloud.in plans to roll out a suite of AI‑powered services over the coming months, targeting sectors such as finance, healthcare, and manufacturing. The company will also host workshops and training sessions to help Indian businesses understand how to integrate OpenAI models into their operations.
