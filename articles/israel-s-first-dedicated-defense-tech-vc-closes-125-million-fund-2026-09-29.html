Israel’s First Dedicated Defense Tech VC Closes $125 Million Fund

2026-09-29 · 3 min read
Protego Ventures, the country’s inaugural defense‑tech focused venture fund, has finished its debut $125 million closing, marking a milestone for Israeli security startups.

Israel’s burgeoning defense‑tech ecosystem has just received a significant boost. Protego Ventures, the nation’s first venture fund dedicated exclusively to defense technology, announced that it has closed its debut fund at $125 million. The final round was led by a mix of institutional investors and strategic partners who see strong upside in the country’s advanced security solutions.

Why the Fund Matters

Protego Ventures positions itself as a specialist investor, focusing on companies developing next‑generation weapons systems, cyber‑defense tools, and autonomous platforms. By concentrating on this niche, the fund aims to provide deep technical expertise and industry connections that generalist funds may lack.

Investor Confidence

The closing of the fund signals growing confidence in Israel’s defense‑tech sector. Analysts note that the country has consistently produced high‑impact innovations, from drone technology to AI‑driven threat detection. The $125 million capital will allow Protego to support early‑stage companies through product development and market entry.

Future Outlook

With the fund now fully subscribed, Protego plans to begin deploying capital in the coming months. The firm’s strategy includes partnering with established defense contractors and government agencies to accelerate commercialization. This move is expected to strengthen Israel’s position as a global leader in defense technology.
