Pixel November Update Unveils Persistent AOD and Notification Bugs

2026-11-13 · 3 min read
Google’s latest Pixel November 2026 update introduces new features but also brings a wave of bugs affecting always‑on‑display, lock screen and notification handling.

Google’s Pixel devices received the November 2026 update today, bringing a host of new features and performance tweaks. However, early reports from users and tech reviewers highlight a growing list of bugs that are disrupting core functionality.

Always‑On Display and Lock Screen Issues

The update’s always‑on‑display (AOD) feature, intended to keep key information visible while the phone is idle, has been reported to flicker or fail to activate on several Pixel models. Users also noted that the lock screen fails to show notifications correctly, with some alerts disappearing entirely or appearing delayed.

Notification Handling Problems

Beyond the lock screen, the update has introduced glitches in the notification system. Some users experience duplicated alerts, while others report that notifications do not appear at all. The issue appears to affect both system and third‑party apps.

Developer Response and Workarounds

Google has acknowledged the problems and is working on a patch. In the meantime, users can try a quick restart or roll back to the previous update if the option is available. The company has also urged users to report bugs through the Android Feedback tool to help prioritize fixes.

Impact on Pixel Users

While the November update promises improved battery life and new customization options, the persistent bugs are causing frustration among the Pixel community. The situation underscores the challenges of rolling out major OS updates on a global scale.
