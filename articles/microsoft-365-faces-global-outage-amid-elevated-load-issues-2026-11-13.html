Microsoft 365 Faces Global Outage Amid Elevated Load Issues

2026-11-13 · 3 min read
Microsoft 365 services including Outlook, Teams, and Copilot are down worldwide as the company works to resolve elevated load problems.

Microsoft’s flagship productivity suite, Microsoft 365, is experiencing a widespread outage that has left users unable to access Outlook, Teams, Copilot, and other core services. The disruption began in the early hours of Thursday, November 12, and has persisted into the following day, affecting customers across the globe.

What’s Impacted?

According to reports from Engadget and Android Authority, the outage is affecting several key Microsoft 365 services. Outlook and Teams are the most visible symptoms, with users reporting timeouts and error messages when attempting to log in or send emails. Copilot, the AI‑powered assistant integrated into Office apps, is also down, further compounding the impact on productivity.

Root Cause and Response

Microsoft has identified the issue as an “elevated load” on a portion of its infrastructure. In a statement released on Friday, the company explained that the increased traffic was likely contributing to connectivity errors across its services. The company has been working to mitigate the load and restore normal operations, but progress has been slower than expected.

Global Reach

The outage is not limited to a single region. The Hindu’s live updates noted that Microsoft’s efforts to fix the problem were not going “as expected,” and the Indian Express reported that the outage was global, affecting users in multiple time zones. The scale of the disruption has prompted many organizations to seek alternative communication and collaboration tools temporarily.

What Users Can Do

While Microsoft continues to address the underlying infrastructure issues, users can try the following workarounds:

  • Use the web versions of Outlook and Teams, which may be less affected by the backend load.
  • Switch to alternative email clients or collaboration platforms for critical tasks.
  • Check Microsoft’s status page and social media channels for real‑time updates.

Microsoft has assured customers that it is prioritizing the restoration of services and will provide further updates as the situation evolves.
