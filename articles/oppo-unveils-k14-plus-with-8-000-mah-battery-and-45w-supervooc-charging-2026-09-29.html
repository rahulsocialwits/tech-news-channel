Oppo Unveils K14 Plus with 8,000‑mAh Battery and 45W SUPERVOOC Charging

2026-09-29 · 3 min read
Oppo’s new K14 Plus smartphone debuts in India with a massive 8,000‑mAh battery, 45W SUPERVOOC fast charging, and 10W reverse wired charging support.

Oppo has rolled out its latest flagship, the K14 Plus, in India, pairing a hefty 8,000‑mAh battery with a 45W SUPERVOOC charging system. The device also supports 10W reverse wired charging, allowing users to power other devices from the phone’s battery.

Power‑Packed Design

The K14 Plus’s battery capacity is one of the largest seen in a mid‑range smartphone, giving it a significant edge in endurance. The 45W SUPERVOOC charger can bring the battery from 0% to 100% in roughly 30 minutes, according to Oppo’s specifications.

Reverse Charging Feature

With 10W reverse wired charging, the K14 Plus can act as a power bank for other devices. This feature is especially useful for travelers and users who rely on their phone as a backup power source.

Market Positioning

Oppo positions the K14 Plus as a high‑performance device for the Indian market, targeting consumers who prioritize battery life and fast charging. The launch follows the company’s recent push into the mid‑range segment with competitive specs.
