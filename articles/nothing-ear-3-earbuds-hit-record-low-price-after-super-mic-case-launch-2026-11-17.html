Nothing Ear (3) Earbuds Hit Record Low Price After Super Mic Case Launch

2026-11-17 · 3 min read
Nothing Ear (3) earbuds drop 37% off on Amazon, hitting their lowest price yet with the new Super Mic case.

Nothing’s latest wireless earbuds, the Ear (3), have just become the most affordable pair in the brand’s lineup. After the company unveiled the Super Mic case, Amazon shoppers can now snag the earbuds in white for $55 less than the original price, a 37% discount that brings the total to just under $120.

What’s New in the Ear (3)?

The Ear (3) continue Nothing’s focus on sleek design and solid performance. They feature a lightweight body, active noise cancellation, and a battery life that reaches up to 8 hours on a single charge, with the Super Mic case extending that to 24 hours. The case itself is a new addition to the product line, offering a built‑in microphone that can be used for hands‑free calls or voice commands when the earbuds are not in use.

Price Drop Details

According to Android Authority, the discount is available on Amazon’s US site and applies to the white variant of the Ear (3). The original retail price was $179, so the $55 reduction brings the earbuds to $124. This is the lowest price the Ear (3) has ever reached since its launch.

Why the Discount Matters

Wireless earbuds are a crowded market, and price can be a decisive factor for many consumers. By offering a significant discount, Nothing is positioning the Ear (3) as a compelling alternative to higher‑priced competitors that deliver similar features. The Super Mic case adds value by extending battery life and providing a convenient way to keep the earbuds charged on the go.

Consumer Reception

Early reviews of the Ear (3) have highlighted the earbuds’ comfortable fit, clear audio, and effective noise cancellation. The addition of the Super Mic case has been praised for its practicality, especially for users who need to keep the earbuds powered during long workdays or travel.

Future Outlook

Nothing’s strategy of combining design, functionality, and affordability has resonated with a segment of the market that seeks premium features without a premium price tag. The current discount could spur a surge in sales, potentially boosting Nothing’s market share in the mid‑tier wireless earbud segment.

How to Get Them

Customers can purchase the Ear (3) with the Super Mic case on Amazon’s US website. The discount is available for a limited time, so interested buyers are encouraged to act quickly to secure the lowest price.
