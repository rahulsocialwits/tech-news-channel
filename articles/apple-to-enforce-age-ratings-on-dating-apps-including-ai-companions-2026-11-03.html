Apple to Enforce Age Ratings on Dating Apps, Including AI Companions

2026-11-03 · 3 min read
Apple will require developers to disclose whether their dating apps connect real people or AI companions, enabling proper age ratings.

Apple announced a new policy that will affect every dating app on its App Store. Beginning next month, developers must explicitly state whether their app facilitates dating between real people, between users and AI companions, or both. The company will use that information to assign the correct age rating, ensuring that younger users are not exposed to content that may be inappropriate for them.

Why the change matters

With the rise of AI‑powered chatbots that can simulate romantic conversations, many dating platforms now offer “AI companion” features. These features can blur the line between human and machine interactions, raising concerns about user safety and consent. By mandating clear disclosure, Apple aims to give parents and guardians better insight into the nature of the app’s interactions.

Implementation details

Developers will add a new field to their app’s metadata during the submission process. The field will ask whether the app enables:

  • Dating between real people
  • Dating between users and AI companions
  • Both

Apple will then apply the appropriate age rating based on the disclosed content. Apps that fail to provide accurate information may face removal from the App Store.

Industry reaction

Some developers have expressed concern that the new requirement could complicate the app review process. Others welcome the move as a step toward greater transparency in the rapidly evolving dating app market.
