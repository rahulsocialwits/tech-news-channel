Payoneer Extends Etsy Partnership Through 2029, Expanding Global Payouts

2026-10-05 · 3 min read
Payoneer renews its partnership with Etsy, extending payouts across 16 markets in India and beyond until 2029.

Payoneer, the global payments platform, has announced a three‑year renewal of its partnership with Etsy, the online marketplace for handmade and vintage goods. The extension, which runs through 2029, will keep Payoneer’s payout services active in 16 existing markets, including India, where the company has been expanding its presence.

What the Renewal Means for Sellers

Under the new agreement, Etsy sellers in the covered regions will continue to receive their earnings via Payoneer’s multi‑currency accounts. The partnership allows sellers to access local bank accounts, debit cards, and instant payouts, simplifying the process of moving money across borders.

Payoneer’s Growing Footprint in India

Payoneer’s partnership with Etsy is part of a broader strategy to strengthen its foothold in the Indian market. The company has already partnered with other e‑commerce platforms and has been working to streamline cross‑border payments for small and medium‑sized businesses.

Industry Context

As e‑commerce continues to grow in emerging markets, payment providers like Payoneer are positioning themselves to handle the increasing volume of international transactions. The renewal with Etsy signals confidence in the platform’s ability to support sellers’ global expansion.
