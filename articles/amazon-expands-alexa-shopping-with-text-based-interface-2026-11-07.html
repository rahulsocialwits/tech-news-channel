Amazon Expands Alexa Shopping with Text‑Based Interface

2026-11-07 · 3 min read
Amazon rolls out a new text‑based shopping feature for Alexa, letting users research, compare products and complete purchases through chat.

Amazon has announced a new text‑based shopping capability for its Alexa voice assistant, allowing users to research and compare products, track deals and price drops, and complete purchases using a chat interface. The feature, unveiled on November 7, 2026, is designed to make shopping more conversational and accessible for users who prefer typing over speaking.

How the Feature Works

Users can simply type a query into the Alexa app or a compatible device and receive a list of relevant products. The system then presents price comparisons, user reviews, and availability information. Once a product is selected, Alexa can handle the entire checkout process, from adding the item to the cart to confirming the purchase.

Benefits for Shoppers and Sellers

For shoppers, the text interface reduces the friction of voice commands and allows for more precise searches. Sellers can benefit from increased visibility as their products appear in a new, searchable format that can capture users who are browsing on mobile or desktop devices.

Implications for the Voice Commerce Market

Amazon’s move signals a broader trend toward multimodal interaction in e‑commerce. By combining voice and text, the company aims to capture a wider audience and improve the overall shopping experience. The feature is part of Amazon’s ongoing strategy to keep Alexa at the forefront of smart‑home and retail innovation.
