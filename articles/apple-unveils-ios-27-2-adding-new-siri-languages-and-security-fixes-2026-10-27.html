Apple Unveils iOS 27.2, Adding New Siri Languages and Security Fixes

2026-10-27 · 3 min read
Apple’s latest iOS 27.2 update expands Siri’s language support and patches critical security issues for iPhones and iPads.

Apple today released iOS 27.2, the newest software update for its iPhone and iPad lineup. The patch brings a suite of new features, including expanded language support for Siri, and addresses several security vulnerabilities that could affect device users worldwide.

New Siri Language Capabilities

The update introduces additional languages for Siri, allowing more users to interact with the assistant in their native tongue. While Apple has not disclosed the full list of languages, the move is part of the company’s ongoing effort to make Siri more accessible across diverse markets.

Security Enhancements

iOS 27.2 includes critical security fixes that patch known exploits in the operating system. The update also tightens the handling of sensitive data, ensuring that personal information remains protected against emerging threats. Users who have not yet installed the update are encouraged to do so promptly to maintain the highest level of device security.

Availability and Installation

iOS 27.2 is available for download through the Settings app under General > Software Update. The update is compatible with all iPhones and iPads that previously supported iOS 27.1, and it can be installed over Wi‑Fi or via a wired connection to a Mac or PC.

Apple’s latest release underscores the company’s commitment to both user experience and security, offering a more inclusive Siri experience while safeguarding the privacy of its global user base.
