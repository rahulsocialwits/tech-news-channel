ThermoWorks Unveils Compact RFX Meat Mini Probe for Small Cuts

2026-10-29 · 3 min read
ThermoWorks releases a shorter version of its RFX Meat wireless probe, delivering the same performance in a more portable form factor.

ThermoWorks has announced the RFX Meat Mini, a shorter version of its popular RFX Meat wireless probe. The new probe is designed for small cuts, offering the same performance as the full‑sized grilling probe while being more compact and easier to handle.

Key Features

  • Compact Design – The RFX Meat Mini is shorter than the original, making it ideal for thin steaks, chops, and other small cuts.
  • Wireless Connectivity – Like its predecessor, the Mini connects via Bluetooth to the ThermoWorks app, allowing real‑time temperature monitoring on a smartphone or tablet.
  • Accurate Readings – The probe maintains the high accuracy and fast response time that ThermoWorks is known for, ensuring precise cooking results.
  • Durable Build – Constructed from the same stainless‑steel material, the Mini is built to withstand repeated use in the kitchen.

Why It Matters

For home cooks and professional chefs alike, having a probe that fits smaller cuts without sacrificing accuracy can streamline the cooking process. The RFX Meat Mini’s portability also makes it a convenient tool for outdoor grilling or limited kitchen space.

Availability

The RFX Meat Mini is available for purchase through ThermoWorks’ website and select retailers. Pricing details have not yet been released, but the company expects it to be competitively priced relative to the full‑sized model.
