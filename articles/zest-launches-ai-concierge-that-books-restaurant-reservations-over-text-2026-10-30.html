Zest Launches AI Concierge That Books Restaurant Reservations Over Text

2026-10-30 · 3 min read
Zest’s new AI agent lets users text to find restaurants, vote on options and secure reservations, streamlining group dining decisions.

In a move that could reshape how groups decide where to eat, Zest has unveiled an AI‑powered concierge that operates entirely through text messages. The new agent can search for restaurants, compile a shortlist that reflects the tastes of all participants, and even handle the booking process, all without a phone call or a web interface.

How the AI Works

Users start by texting the Zest bot a simple request, such as “Find Italian places near me.” The AI then pulls data from local listings, reviews and user preferences to generate a set of options. Each option is presented to the group, who can vote via text. Once a majority is reached, the bot automatically sends the reservation request to the chosen restaurant and confirms the booking.

Benefits for Group Dining

Group dining has long been a logistical headache. Traditional approaches—phone calls, spreadsheets, or third‑party apps—often lead to miscommunication or missed reservations. Zest’s text‑based workflow eliminates the need for a dedicated app and keeps the conversation in a familiar medium. The AI’s ability to aggregate preferences means that every voice is considered, reducing the back‑and‑forth that typically accompanies group decisions.

Privacy and Security

Zest emphasizes that all data exchanged with the bot is encrypted end‑to‑end. The company states that it does not store personal preferences beyond the duration of the booking process, ensuring that users’ dining choices remain private.

Market Context

The restaurant reservation market is already crowded, with services like OpenTable and Resy dominating the space. Zest’s approach differentiates itself by focusing on a frictionless, text‑centric experience that can be accessed from any device with messaging capabilities. By removing the need for a dedicated app, the company hopes to lower the barrier to entry for users who prefer simple, conversational interfaces.

Future Plans

While the current rollout is limited to a handful of cities, Zest plans to expand its coverage to include more regions and cuisines. The company also hints at integrating payment options in the future, allowing the AI to handle the entire dining experience from selection to checkout.
