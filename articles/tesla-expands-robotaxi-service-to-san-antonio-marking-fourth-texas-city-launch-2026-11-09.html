Tesla Expands Robotaxi Service to San Antonio, Marking Fourth Texas City Launch

2026-11-09 · 3 min read
Tesla rolls out its autonomous ride‑hailing fleet to San Antonio, expanding its robotaxi network across Texas.

On November 9, 2026, Tesla announced that its robotaxi service would begin operating in San Antonio, Texas. The move brings the autonomous ride‑hailing fleet to a fourth Texas city, following earlier launches in Austin, Dallas, and Houston. The company’s expansion is part of a broader strategy to deploy its self‑driving technology in urban environments across the United States.

What the Service Offers

Customers in San Antonio will be able to request a Tesla robotaxi through the company’s mobile app. The vehicles, equipped with Tesla’s full self‑driving hardware, are designed to navigate city streets without a human driver. The service is expected to operate on a pay‑per‑ride basis, with pricing that reflects the distance traveled and the time of day.

Why Texas Matters

Texas has become a key testing ground for Tesla’s autonomous technology. The state’s relatively permissive regulatory environment and growing urban populations provide an ideal backdrop for the company to refine its systems and gather real‑world data. By adding San Antonio to its roster, Tesla can further evaluate its software in a diverse traffic setting.

Next Steps for Tesla

Following the San Antonio launch, Tesla plans to expand the robotaxi network to additional cities. The company is also working on improving its navigation algorithms and safety protocols to ensure reliable operation in a variety of weather and traffic conditions.
