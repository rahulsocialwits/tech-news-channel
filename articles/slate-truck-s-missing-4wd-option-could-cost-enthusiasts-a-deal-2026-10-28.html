Slate Truck’s Missing 4WD Option Could Cost Enthusiasts a Deal

2026-10-28 · 3 min read
The Slate Truck’s lack of a 4WD option may turn off buyers who want off‑road capability, according to Engadget.

The Slate Truck, known for its extensive customization menu, has drawn criticism for not offering a 4‑wheel‑drive configuration. Enthusiasts who want off‑road performance say the omission could be a dealbreaker.

Customization vs. Capability

Slate’s platform allows buyers to tweak everything from paint colors to suspension settings. However, the absence of a 4WD option means the truck can only be built as a front‑wheel‑drive vehicle.

Impact on the Market

For buyers who prioritize off‑road capability, the lack of 4WD limits the truck’s appeal. Some reviewers suggest that adding the feature could broaden Slate’s customer base and make the truck more competitive against rivals that already offer 4WD.

Possible Future Updates

Slate has not yet announced plans to add 4WD. The company’s focus on modularity suggests that future updates could introduce the option, but no timeline has been provided.
