Fairphone Partners with Boost Mobile to Bring Repairable Smartphones to the U.S. Market

2026-11-12 · 3 min read
Fairphone, the sustainable phone maker, has secured its first U.S. carrier partnership with Boost Mobile, offering repairable smartphones and wireless plans to American consumers.

Fairphone, the Dutch company known for its fully repairable smartphones, has announced a partnership with U.S. carrier Boost Mobile. The deal will allow Boost Mobile customers to purchase Fairphone devices and bundle them with wireless plans, marking the brand’s first entry into the American market.

Why a carrier partnership matters

Until now, Fairphone’s sales have been limited to its own website and a handful of European carriers. By teaming up with Boost Mobile, the company can tap into a large U.S. customer base that prefers carrier‑bundled plans over buying phones outright. The partnership also signals a growing appetite among U.S. carriers for sustainable and repair‑friendly devices.

What the partnership offers

Under the agreement, Boost Mobile will carry Fairphone’s flagship model, the Fairphone 5, and offer it with a range of data plans. Customers will be able to purchase the phone through Boost’s online store or in‑store kiosks, and the carrier will provide support for repairs and spare parts, leveraging Fairphone’s existing repair network.

Fairphone’s repair philosophy

Fairphone’s design philosophy centers on modularity and repairability. Each component—from the battery to the camera module—can be swapped out with minimal tools. The company also offers a repair kit and detailed instructions on its website, allowing users to fix common issues themselves. This approach reduces e‑waste and extends the device’s lifespan.

Boost Mobile’s sustainability push

Boost Mobile has been expanding its sustainability initiatives, including a commitment to reduce its carbon footprint and promote circular economy practices. Partnering with Fairphone aligns with these goals, offering customers a greener alternative to mainstream smartphones.

Market implications

The collaboration could encourage other carriers to explore similar partnerships, potentially increasing the availability of repairable devices in the U.S. market. It also reflects a broader trend of consumers demanding more sustainable tech options, a trend that has been accelerating in recent years.

Next steps for Fairphone

Fairphone plans to expand its U.S. presence beyond Boost Mobile, targeting additional carriers and retail partners. The company also aims to localize its repair services, setting up repair hubs in major U.S. cities to support the growing customer base.
