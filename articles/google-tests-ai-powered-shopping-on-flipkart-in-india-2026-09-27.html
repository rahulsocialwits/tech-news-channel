Google Tests AI‑Powered Shopping on Flipkart in India

2026-09-27 · 3 min read
Google pilots Gemini‑driven purchasing on Walmart‑owned Flipkart, offering a new AI shopping experience for select users and products.

Google is experimenting with a new way for shoppers to buy products on Walmart‑owned Flipkart using its Gemini AI and a feature called AI Mode. The pilot, announced on September 26, 2026, is limited to a handful of items and a small group of users in India. The company plans to expand the rollout later in October.

How the Pilot Works

When a user visits Flipkart, they can activate Gemini‑powered AI Mode. The AI can answer questions about products, suggest alternatives, and even place orders on behalf of the shopper. The system is designed to streamline the buying process by reducing the number of clicks needed to complete a purchase.

Scope and Future Plans

Google has not yet disclosed the exact number of products or users involved in the test. However, the company has indicated that the pilot will cover a curated selection of items that are popular among Indian consumers. The goal is to evaluate how well the AI can handle the nuances of local language, payment methods, and delivery logistics.

Implications for the E‑Commerce Landscape

If successful, the partnership could signal a shift toward AI‑driven shopping experiences in emerging markets. By leveraging Gemini’s conversational abilities, Google aims to make online shopping more intuitive and personalized. Flipkart, as a major player in India’s e‑commerce sector, could benefit from increased conversion rates and customer satisfaction.

Next Steps

Google plans to monitor user engagement and satisfaction during the limited test. Feedback will guide adjustments to the AI’s recommendation engine and purchasing workflow. A broader rollout is expected in October, contingent on the pilot’s performance metrics.
