Samsung Messages Adds Hands‑Free Texting Features

2026-11-27 · 3 min read
Samsung’s messaging app will soon let users find, send and forward texts using voice commands, expanding its hands‑free capabilities.

Samsung has announced a new update for its native Messages app that will enable users to locate, send and forward text messages using voice commands. The feature, still under development, is part of the company’s broader push to make everyday communication more accessible and hands‑free.

How the Feature Works

When activated, the app will listen for a user’s voice and interpret commands such as “find the last message from John” or “forward this text to Sarah.” The system will then display the relevant conversation and execute the requested action without requiring the user to touch the screen.

Development Status

Samsung has stated that the feature is not yet complete and that further refinements are planned. The company is testing the technology in a limited beta before a wider rollout is expected later this year.

Implications for Users

By integrating voice control into its messaging platform, Samsung aims to improve accessibility for users with mobility challenges and to streamline communication for those who prefer hands‑free interaction. The update also positions Samsung to compete with other voice‑enabled messaging services that are gaining popularity.
