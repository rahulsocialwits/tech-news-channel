Black Friday Laptop Deals Heat Up as Prices Drop Ahead of the Shopping Day

2026-11-19 · 3 min read
Early Black Friday laptop discounts are already hitting the market, offering buyers a chance to snag high‑end models at steep markdowns before the holiday rush.

As the holiday shopping season kicks into high gear, early Black Friday laptop deals are already making headlines. Wired’s recent review highlights that some of the most coveted laptops of 2026 have seen significant price reductions in the weeks leading up to the big day.

Which Models Are Getting the Biggest Cuts?

The report notes that flagship machines from major manufacturers—such as the latest MacBook Pro, Dell XPS 15, and Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon—have all seen price drops ranging from 10% to 25%. These discounts are especially appealing to professionals and gamers who rely on powerful hardware for demanding tasks.

Why the Early Discounts?

Retailers are offering early bargains to capture early‑bird shoppers and to clear inventory before the holiday surge. By providing attractive markdowns ahead of the traditional Black Friday window, stores aim to boost sales and reduce the risk of overstocking.

What Buyers Should Look For

When hunting for a deal, consumers should compare specifications across models, focusing on processor speed, graphics capability, and battery life. Wired’s review emphasizes that while price is a key factor, performance consistency and build quality remain critical for long‑term satisfaction.

How to Make the Most of the Deals

1. Set a budget and stick to it—early discounts can still be steep for high‑end devices.
2. Check return policies—many retailers offer extended return windows during the holiday season.
3. Read user reviews—real‑world feedback can reveal issues not apparent in spec sheets.

Looking Ahead

While the early wave of discounts is already in motion, retailers are expected to roll out additional promotions as Black Friday approaches. Tech enthusiasts and casual buyers alike should keep an eye on price trackers and retailer newsletters to catch the best offers before they disappear.
