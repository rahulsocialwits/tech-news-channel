Samsung’s Odyssey G9 Gaming Monitor Slashes 35% Off, Now $650

2026-09-30 · 3 min read
Samsung’s 49‑inch Odyssey G9 ultra‑wide gaming monitor drops 35% from its original price, hitting $650 for a limited time.

Samsung has announced a record price cut on its flagship 49‑inch Odyssey G9 gaming monitor, slashing the retail price by 35% to $650. The move comes as the company looks to keep its high‑end display lineup competitive in a market that has seen a wave of new releases and aggressive pricing from rivals.

What the deal means for gamers

The Odyssey G9, which boasts a 144Hz refresh rate, 1ms response time, and a 5120×1440 resolution, has long been a premium choice for gamers who want an immersive, ultra‑wide experience. The new price point brings the monitor within reach of a broader segment of the gaming community, potentially driving higher sales volumes for Samsung’s high‑performance display division.

How the discount compares to the original price

At its original retail price, the Odyssey G9 was listed at $1,000. The 35% reduction brings the cost down to $650, a $350 saving for consumers. This discount is one of the steepest price cuts seen for a gaming monitor in recent months, according to the source from Android Authority.

Availability and timing

The promotion is available for a limited time only, with the discount applied to purchases made through Amazon. Samsung has not announced a specific end date for the offer, but the retailer’s “today‑only” phrasing suggests that the deal may be short‑lived. Interested buyers are encouraged to act quickly to secure the monitor before the price reverts to its original level.

Industry context

Samsung’s decision to cut the price of the Odyssey G9 comes amid a broader trend of price adjustments across the gaming hardware sector. Other manufacturers have also reduced prices on high‑end monitors and graphics cards in response to supply chain constraints and shifting consumer demand. By offering a substantial discount on its flagship model, Samsung aims to maintain its market share in the competitive ultra‑wide monitor segment.

What’s next for the Odyssey G9

While the price reduction is a significant development, Samsung has not announced any changes to the Odyssey G9’s specifications or feature set. The monitor continues to support NVIDIA G-Sync and AMD FreeSync, ensuring smooth gameplay for a wide range of graphics cards. The company’s focus appears to be on making the product more accessible rather than altering its technical capabilities.
