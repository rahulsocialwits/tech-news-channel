Google Gemini Adds Separate Privacy Toggles for AI Training and Chat History

2026-11-17 · 3 min read
Google now lets users opt out of Gemini’s AI training while keeping their chat history, giving clearer control over personal data.

Google has rolled out a new privacy feature for its Gemini AI platform, allowing users to separate the settings for AI training and chat history. The change comes after growing concerns about how conversational data is used to improve machine learning models.

How the new toggles work

In the Gemini activity settings, two independent switches let users decide whether their conversations can be used to train the model and whether their chat history is retained. Turning off the training toggle stops Gemini from using the content to refine its algorithms, while the history toggle still lets users view past interactions.

Why it matters

Previously, opting out of training also erased chat history, forcing users to choose between privacy and continuity. The new design gives a more granular approach, aligning with broader industry moves toward data transparency.

Impact on messaging apps

Google’s Gemini integration extends beyond the web interface. As announced at I/O 2026, Gemini can now be used to send emails and text messages via Google Messages and iMessage. The feature leverages the same privacy controls, so users can decide how much of their communication data feeds back into the AI system.

Looking ahead

With these updates, Google aims to build trust while maintaining the performance gains that come from large-scale data training. The company has stated it will continue to refine its privacy options based on user feedback.
