Google Maps Adds Motorcycle‑Specific Navigation to Android Auto

2026-11-25 · 3 min read
Google Maps rolls out new Android Auto feature that tailors routes for motorcyclists, improving safety and convenience on the road.

Google Maps has expanded its Android Auto offering to include dedicated navigation routes for motorcycles. The update, announced on November 25, 2026, adds a new layer of route optimization that takes into account the unique handling and safety considerations of two‑wheel vehicles. Riders can now receive turn‑by‑turn directions that prioritize smoother turns, avoid steep inclines, and highlight bike‑friendly roads.

Why the Update Matters

Motorcycle navigation has traditionally lagged behind car‑centric mapping services. By integrating motorcycle‑specific routing into Android Auto, Google aims to reduce the risk of accidents and improve the overall riding experience. The feature also aligns with the company’s broader push to support a wider range of vehicle types on its platform.

How It Works

When a user selects the motorcycle mode, the app queries a database of road conditions, traffic patterns, and historical accident data to generate a route that balances speed with safety. The system also flags potential hazards such as construction zones or narrow bridges that could pose a danger to riders.

Availability

The update is rolling out to Android Auto users worldwide. To access the new feature, riders simply need to open Google Maps on their connected device and choose the motorcycle icon before starting navigation.
