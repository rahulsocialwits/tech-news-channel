Yokogawa Unveils OpreX Universal Autonomous Control System R1.01 for Process Automation

2026-11-26 · 3 min read
Yokogawa Electric launches a new PLC, the OpreX Universal Autonomous Control System R1.01, designed to streamline industrial process automation.

Yokogawa Electric Corporation announced the release of its latest programmable logic controller, the OpreX Universal Autonomous Control System R1.01. The new PLC is positioned as an all‑new solution for process automation applications, offering enhanced flexibility and integration capabilities for industrial environments.

Key Features of the OpreX R1.01

  • Universal Compatibility – The system supports a wide range of industrial protocols, enabling seamless communication with existing equipment.
  • Autonomous Control – Built‑in autonomous functions allow the PLC to manage routine tasks without continuous operator input, improving efficiency.
  • Scalable Architecture – The design accommodates future expansion, making it suitable for both small‑scale plants and large industrial facilities.

Industry Impact

The introduction of the OpreX R1.01 comes at a time when manufacturers are seeking smarter, more adaptable control solutions to meet evolving production demands. By providing a unified platform that reduces the need for multiple specialized devices, Yokogawa aims to lower maintenance costs and simplify system integration.

Availability

Yokogawa has stated that the OpreX Universal Autonomous Control System R1.01 will be available for purchase starting in early 2027. The company is targeting sectors such as chemical processing, oil and gas, and food and beverage manufacturing.
