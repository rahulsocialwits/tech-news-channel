Google Home Bug Leaves Nest Camera Alerts Without Video Previews

2026-11-11 · 3 min read
A recent flaw in Google Home’s integration with Nest cameras means users receive only text notifications, missing the expected video preview.

Google Home users have reported that notifications from their Nest cameras now arrive as plain text alerts, without the accompanying video preview that has become a standard feature. The issue appears to affect all Nest cameras connected through the Google Home ecosystem, leaving users unable to see a live clip when a motion event is triggered.

What’s Happening?

According to Android Authority, the bug manifests as a missing video thumbnail in the notification pane. Instead of the usual preview, the alert simply states the event type and time. Users can still view the full recording by opening the Nest app, but the convenience of a quick glance is lost.

Impact on Users

For many, the preview is a critical safety feature, allowing instant assessment of whether a motion event is a false alarm or a genuine concern. The absence of this visual cue may lead to delayed responses or missed alerts, especially for those who rely on the Home app for real‑time monitoring.

Google’s Response

Google has acknowledged the problem and is working on a fix. While a specific rollout date has not been announced, the company has urged users to keep their Home and Nest apps updated, as the latest versions contain a patch that restores the preview functionality for affected devices.

What to Do Now

Users experiencing the issue should:

  • Check for and install any available updates for the Google Home and Nest apps.
  • Restart the Google Home device to ensure firmware is current.
  • Verify that the Nest camera’s firmware is up to date via the Nest app.

Once the updates are applied, the video preview should return to normal. If the problem persists, contacting Google Support is recommended.
