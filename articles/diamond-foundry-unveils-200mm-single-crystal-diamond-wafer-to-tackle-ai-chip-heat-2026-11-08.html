Diamond Foundry Unveils 200mm Single‑Crystal Diamond Wafer to Tackle AI Chip Heat

2026-11-08 · 3 min read
Diamond Foundry’s new 200mm diamond wafer promises efficient heat removal for next‑generation AI processors, potentially easing the cooling crisis in high‑performance chips.

Diamond Foundry has announced the creation of the world’s first 200mm single‑crystal diamond wafer, a breakthrough that could reshape how heat is managed in advanced AI processors. The wafer, produced by the company’s new facility, is engineered to act as a high‑thermal‑conductivity substrate that can efficiently dissipate heat and prevent hotspots on semiconductor chips.

Why Diamond Matters for AI Chips

Artificial‑intelligence workloads generate intense heat, especially as processors become denser and faster. Traditional cooling solutions—such as copper heat sinks and liquid cooling—are reaching their limits. Diamond, with its exceptional thermal conductivity and mechanical strength, offers a material that can conduct heat away from the chip more effectively than any conventional metal.

Key Features of the 200mm Wafer

  • Size and Scale: The wafer measures 200mm in diameter, matching the industry standard for semiconductor fabrication and enabling integration with existing manufacturing lines.
  • Single‑Crystal Structure: A single‑crystal lattice eliminates grain boundaries that can impede heat flow, ensuring uniform thermal performance across the wafer.
  • Compatibility: Designed to be used as a substrate or heat spreader, the diamond wafer can be bonded directly to silicon or other semiconductor materials without compromising device integrity.

Potential Impact on AI Hardware

By providing a more efficient heat path, the diamond wafer could allow AI processors to run at higher clock speeds or with greater parallelism without overheating. This improvement could translate into faster inference times, lower power consumption, and extended device lifespans—benefits that are critical for data centers, edge devices, and high‑performance computing platforms.

Industry Reception

While the technology is still in the early stages of adoption, industry analysts see the diamond wafer as a promising solution to the cooling bottleneck that has slowed the scaling of AI chips. If the manufacturing process can be scaled and costs reduced, the material could become a standard component in next‑generation AI hardware.

Next Steps

Diamond Foundry plans to collaborate with major semiconductor manufacturers to integrate the wafer into production lines. The company also aims to refine the bonding process to ensure reliable attachment between diamond and silicon substrates.
