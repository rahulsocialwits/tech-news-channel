Amazon Leo Secures FCC Approval to Acquire Globalstar in $11.5 B Deal

2026-10-28 · 3 min read
Amazon’s satellite arm, Amazon Leo, has received FCC clearance to buy Globalstar, expanding its mobile‑satellite services.

Amazon’s satellite division, Amazon Leo, has cleared a major regulatory hurdle with the Federal Communications Commission (FCC) as it moves forward with its $11.5 billion acquisition of mobile‑satellite operator Globalstar. The approval marks a significant step for Amazon Leo, which aims to broaden its satellite‑based connectivity offerings across the globe.

What the Deal Means for Amazon Leo

The acquisition will give Amazon Leo access to Globalstar’s existing satellite network, which currently provides low‑Earth orbit (LEO) communications for a range of applications, from IoT devices to maritime and aviation services. By integrating Globalstar’s infrastructure, Amazon Leo can accelerate the rollout of its own satellite‑enabled services, potentially offering faster, more reliable connectivity to underserved regions.

Regulatory Context

The FCC’s approval follows a thorough review of the transaction’s impact on competition, spectrum usage, and national security. The commission confirmed that the deal would not create significant antitrust concerns and that it would comply with U.S. regulations governing satellite communications.

Industry Reactions

Industry analysts see the move as part of Amazon’s broader strategy to compete with established satellite operators and to diversify its cloud and edge computing services. The acquisition also positions Amazon Leo to leverage its existing AWS infrastructure, potentially creating new hybrid cloud solutions that combine satellite and terrestrial connectivity.

Next Steps

With FCC clearance in place, Amazon Leo will now focus on finalizing the financial terms and integrating Globalstar’s operations. The company has indicated that it plans to maintain Globalstar’s brand identity while gradually aligning its services with Amazon’s broader satellite ecosystem.
