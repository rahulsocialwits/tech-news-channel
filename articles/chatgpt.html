What Is ChatGPT? How OpenAI's AI Assistant Works

September 26, 2026 · 6 min read
ChatGPT is a conversational AI service developed by OpenAI. It can respond to prompts, explain concepts, draft text, summarize information, help with coding and perform many other language-based tasks.

How does ChatGPT work?

At a high level, ChatGPT uses machine-learning models trained to process and generate language. When a user sends a prompt, the system analyzes the context and generates a response based on patterns learned during training and the instructions available to it.

The experience is conversational, so users can ask follow-up questions and refine a request. Depending on the product and tools available, an AI assistant may also work with files, images, web information or other connected capabilities.

What can ChatGPT be used for?

  • Explaining technical and general topics in simpler language.
  • Drafting and rewriting emails, articles, reports and other text.
  • Brainstorming ideas, outlines and content plans.
  • Helping developers understand, write and debug code.
  • Summarizing information supplied by the user.

Why can ChatGPT make mistakes?

Generative AI does not guarantee that every response is correct. A fluent answer can still contain an inaccurate statement, missing context or an incorrect interpretation. Important information should therefore be checked against appropriate primary or authoritative sources.

Why is ChatGPT important?

ChatGPT helped make conversational generative AI accessible to a broad audience. Its impact is best understood through practical use: people can interact with software using natural language rather than learning a separate command or interface for every task.

The bottom line

ChatGPT is a general-purpose conversational AI assistant rather than a traditional search engine or a simple chatbot script. Its usefulness depends on the quality of the prompt, the capabilities available in the product and the user's judgment when verifying important outputs.
