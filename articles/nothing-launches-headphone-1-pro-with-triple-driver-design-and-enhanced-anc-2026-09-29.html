Nothing Launches Headphone (1) Pro with Triple‑Driver Design and Enhanced ANC

2026-09-29 · 3 min read
Nothing’s new Headphone (1) Pro adds a third driver per ear, upgraded ANC and deeper audio tuning, priced at $399.

Nothing has expanded its flagship headphone line with the Headphone (1) Pro, a new over‑ear model that builds on the original’s minimalist aesthetic while adding significant audio upgrades. The Pro version introduces a third driver in each ear cup, a move that brings the total to six drivers per ear, and is tuned by Metropolis Studios to deliver studio‑grade sound for both studio and commuting use.

Hardware and Design Enhancements

The new headphones feature a larger, more robust housing that accommodates the additional driver and improved active noise cancellation (ANC) circuitry. The ANC system is now more powerful, offering deeper attenuation across a wider frequency range. Users can also access a dedicated tuning mode that allows fine‑grained control over the final audio output, giving them the ability to tailor the sound profile to personal preference.

Price and Availability

Nothing has positioned the Headphone (1) Pro at a $399 price point, a $100 increase over the original Headphone (1). The company has stated that the price hike reflects the added hardware and the enhanced tuning capabilities. The Pro model is available for purchase worldwide through Nothing’s official website and select retailers.

Market Context

With the headphone market seeing a surge in premium offerings, Nothing’s move to introduce a higher‑end model aligns with industry trends toward more feature‑rich audio products. The triple‑driver setup and advanced ANC are expected to appeal to audiophiles and professionals who demand studio‑grade sound in a portable form factor.
