Vitonnix Secures First Institutional Investment to Accelerate AI‑Driven Healthcare

2026-10-16 · 3 min read
Healthcare startup Vitonnix raises institutional funding led by Inflection Point Ventures, with participation from doctors and Bollywood stars.

In a move that signals growing confidence in AI‑powered medical solutions, Vitonnix, a Bengaluru‑based healthcare startup, announced a successful first institutional investment round led by Inflection Point Ventures. The funding round also attracted a mix of practising doctors and high‑profile Bollywood personalities, including actor Riteish Deshmukh, actor Boman Irani, singer Jaspinder Narula and film producer Madhu.

Funding details and strategic focus

The investment, which marks Vitonnix’s first institutional round, is aimed at scaling the company’s AI‑driven diagnostic platform. While the exact amount was not disclosed, the participation of Inflection Point Ventures—a firm known for backing early‑stage health tech—underscores the potential of Vitonnix’s technology to transform patient care.

Industry context

AI in healthcare has been gaining traction, with startups increasingly partnering with medical professionals to validate and refine their solutions. Vitonnix’s collaboration with practising doctors signals a commitment to clinical rigor, while the involvement of celebrities is expected to boost public awareness and trust.

Next steps for Vitonnix

With the new capital, Vitonnix plans to expand its product suite, enhance data security measures, and accelerate regulatory approvals. The company also intends to broaden its geographic reach beyond India, targeting markets with high unmet diagnostic needs.
