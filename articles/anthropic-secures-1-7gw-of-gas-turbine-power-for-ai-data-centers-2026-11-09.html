Anthropic Secures 1.7GW of Gas‑Turbine Power for AI Data Centers

2026-11-09 · 3 min read
Anthropic has committed $3.4 billion to acquire 1.7GW of gas‑fired generators, bypassing long equipment lead times and ensuring reliable power for its AI workloads.

In a move that underscores the growing need for dedicated power infrastructure in the AI sector, Anthropic has announced a $3.4 billion deal to secure 1.7 GW of gas‑fired generators from OnePWR Solutions. The agreement allows the language‑model company to take over existing Caterpillar orders that were facing extended lead times, a common bottleneck for firms looking to expand their data‑center capacity.

Why Generators Matter for AI

Running large language models requires massive amounts of electricity. While cloud providers traditionally rely on grid power, the surge in AI demand has pushed companies to consider on‑site generation to guarantee uptime and manage costs. Anthropic’s deal is a clear signal that the industry is moving toward hybrid power strategies.

Details of the Deal

The contract covers 1.7 GW of gas‑turbine capacity, a substantial addition to Anthropic’s planned data‑center footprint. By partnering with OnePWR Solutions, the company can bypass the typical six‑year waitlist that has plagued the sector, ensuring that its AI workloads have the necessary power supply without delay.

Industry Implications

Anthropic’s move highlights a broader trend of AI firms investing heavily in their own power infrastructure. As cloud giants face pressure from both regulatory bodies and community groups concerned about the environmental impact of large server farms, companies are exploring more flexible and potentially greener solutions.
