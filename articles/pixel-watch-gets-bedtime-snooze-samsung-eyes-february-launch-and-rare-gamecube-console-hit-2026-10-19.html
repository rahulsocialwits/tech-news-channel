Pixel Watch Gets Bedtime Snooze, Samsung Eyes February Launch, and Rare GameCube Console Hits Auction Prices

2026-10-19 · 3 min read
Google’s Pixel Watch may soon let users snooze auto‑bedtime, Samsung is weighing a February launch for the Galaxy S27, and a rare GameCube console sold for thousands at auction.

Google is reportedly adding a new bedtime feature to the Pixel Watch that would let users snooze the auto‑bedtime function, giving them more control over when the watch nudges them to sleep. The change would allow users to temporarily disable the automatic bedtime reminder, a feature that has been a point of frustration for some wear‑able users who prefer to set their own sleep schedule.

Samsung’s Galaxy S27 Pro and Ultra: February Launch Likely

Samsung has not yet finalized the details for its next flagship line, the Galaxy S27 Pro and Ultra. Industry analysts say the company is aiming for a February launch at the latest, but it has yet to confirm a date. The delay is reportedly due to the need to finalize component specifications and supply chain logistics for the new models.

Rare GameCube Console Sells for Thousands at Auction

Collectors are paying top dollar for one of the most elusive variations of the Nintendo GameCube. A limited‑edition console, known for its unique color and design, recently sold for thousands of dollars at an auction. The high price reflects the console’s rarity and the growing demand among retro gaming enthusiasts.

What These Updates Mean for Consumers

For Pixel Watch users, the snooze feature could improve the device’s usability for those who prefer a more flexible sleep schedule. Samsung’s potential February launch of the Galaxy S27 line means that consumers can expect new hardware with upgraded cameras, processors, and display technology, though the exact specifications remain under wraps. Meanwhile, the GameCube auction highlights the continued interest in vintage gaming hardware and the potential for collectors to find valuable pieces.

Looking Ahead

As Google expands its wearable ecosystem, Samsung pushes forward with its flagship lineup, and collectors chase rare gaming hardware, the gadget market continues to evolve. Users can anticipate new features, updated hardware, and high‑value collectibles in the coming months.
