Apple Unveils iPhone Duo: The First Foldable Smartphone That Packs a Punch

2026-11-02 · 4 min read
Apple’s new iPhone Duo folds into a 7.9‑inch tablet‑size display, offering a larger screen for multitasking while keeping a premium price tag.

Apple has taken a bold step into the foldable‑smartphone arena with the iPhone Duo, a device that folds in half to reveal a 7.9‑inch display. The new phone is a direct response to the growing demand for larger screens that can still fit in a pocket. While the concept is not new, Apple’s execution brings a level of polish and ecosystem integration that sets it apart from other foldable offerings on the market.

Design and Build

The iPhone Duo’s exterior is a seamless blend of glass and aluminum, with a hinge that sits flush on the back. When unfolded, the device measures 7.9 inches diagonally, offering a screen area that rivals many tablets. The hinge mechanism is engineered to withstand thousands of folds, and Apple has reportedly used a proprietary metal alloy to reduce wear over time. The phone’s weight is 210 grams, slightly heavier than a standard iPhone but still manageable for everyday use.

Display and Performance

The 7.9‑inch screen is a Super Retina XDR panel with a 120‑Hz refresh rate, delivering smooth scrolling and vibrant colors. When folded, the display shrinks to a 6.5‑inch size that retains the same resolution, ensuring that apps and media look sharp in either mode. Under the hood, the iPhone Duo is powered by Apple’s latest A17 Bionic chip, which offers a 20% boost in CPU performance and a 30% increase in GPU efficiency compared to its predecessor. The device ships with 12GB of RAM and starts at 256GB of internal storage, with options up to 1TB.

Camera System

Apple has not compromised on photography. The iPhone Duo features a triple‑camera setup on the rear: a 12‑MP wide lens, a 12‑MP ultra‑wide lens, and a 12‑MP telephoto lens with 3× optical zoom. The front camera is a 12‑MP sensor that supports 4K video recording. Despite the larger body, the camera module remains compact, allowing the phone to maintain a slim profile when folded.

Software and Ecosystem

Running iOS 18, the iPhone Duo introduces new multitasking features that take advantage of the larger screen. Users can open multiple apps side by side, drag and drop content between them, and even use the device as a mini desktop when connected to an external monitor. The new “Foldable Mode” in the Settings app lets users customize how the phone behaves when unfolded, including options for app layout and keyboard placement.

Price and Availability

The iPhone Duo starts at $1,999, positioning it firmly in the premium segment. Apple has announced that the phone will be available in the United States, Canada, and select European markets in early December. While the price is higher than most foldable competitors, the device’s build quality, performance, and integration with the Apple ecosystem justify the premium for many consumers.

Market Impact

Apple’s entry into the foldable market could shift the competitive landscape. Existing players like Samsung and Huawei have been refining their hinge mechanisms and software for years, but Apple’s reputation for durability and seamless integration may attract users who have been hesitant to adopt foldable devices. The iPhone Duo also signals Apple’s commitment to pushing the boundaries of smartphone form factors, potentially paving the way for future innovations such as foldable displays with higher refresh rates or even rollable screens.

Conclusion

With the iPhone Duo, Apple delivers a foldable smartphone that balances size, performance, and premium design. While the price may deter some, the device offers a compelling proposition for power users and early adopters who value a larger screen without sacrificing portability. As the market for foldable devices matures, the iPhone Duo’s success will likely influence how other manufacturers approach the next generation of smartphones.
