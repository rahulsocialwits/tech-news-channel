Samsung’s Galaxy SmartTag 3 Drops to $20, Outshining Google’s Pixel Tag

2026-11-13 · 3 min read
Samsung’s Galaxy SmartTag 3 is now available for just $20, offering a budget-friendly alternative to Google’s new Pixel Tag for Galaxy phone users.

Samsung has slashed the price of its Galaxy SmartTag 3 to a mere $20, making it a compelling choice for users who want a lightweight, affordable way to keep track of their belongings. The new price point comes just a week after the tag’s launch, and it positions the device as a direct competitor to Google’s recently announced Pixel Tag, which is only available to Pixel phone owners.

What the SmartTag 3 Offers

The Galaxy SmartTag 3 is a small, Bluetooth‑enabled tracker that plugs into the Galaxy SmartThings ecosystem. It can be attached to keys, bags, or other items, and users can locate them via the SmartThings app. The tag also supports a “Find My” feature that plays a sound on the device, and it can trigger a “SmartThings Automations” routine when it’s moved or when its battery is low.

Why the $20 Price Matters

At $20, the SmartTag 3 is significantly cheaper than many other Bluetooth trackers on the market. The price drop is especially attractive for budget-conscious consumers who already own a Samsung Galaxy phone. Because the tag works only with Galaxy devices, the discount is a strategic move to lock in the existing Galaxy user base and encourage them to upgrade their accessories.

Comparing to Google’s Pixel Tag

Google’s Pixel Tag, which debuted alongside the Pixel 8 series, is designed to work exclusively with Pixel phones. While it offers similar tracking capabilities, it is not available to non‑Pixel users. Samsung’s decision to price the SmartTag 3 so low may push users who are dissatisfied with the Pixel Tag’s price or limited compatibility to switch to a Galaxy phone.

Market Impact

Analysts suggest that Samsung’s aggressive pricing could shift the competitive landscape for Bluetooth trackers. By offering a low-cost, high‑integration solution, Samsung may capture a larger share of the “smart accessory” market, which has seen steady growth as consumers look for ways to protect their personal items.

How to Get the SmartTag 3

Retailers are already stocking the $20 SmartTag 3, and it can be purchased online through Samsung’s official store or through major e‑commerce platforms. Users should ensure they have the latest version of the SmartThings app to take full advantage of the tag’s features.
