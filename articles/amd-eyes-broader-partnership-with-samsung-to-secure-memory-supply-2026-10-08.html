AMD Eyes Broader Partnership With Samsung to Secure Memory Supply

2026-10-08 · 3 min read
AMD is pursuing a wider partnership with Samsung to tie advanced memory supply to its foundry orders, aiming to strengthen its semiconductor supply chain.

AMD is looking to deepen its relationship with Samsung as it seeks to secure a steady supply of advanced memory for its processors. The chipmaker has announced plans to pursue a broader partnership that would link Samsung’s memory production directly to orders at its own foundry unit.

Why the partnership matters

With global demand for high‑performance computing and gaming chips on the rise, memory supply has become a critical bottleneck. By tying memory supply to foundry orders, AMD hopes to ensure that its customers receive the latest memory technologies without interruption.

What Samsung brings to the table

Samsung is one of the world’s largest memory manufacturers, producing cutting‑edge DRAM and NAND flash. The company has expressed interest in expanding its collaboration with AMD, potentially turning its memory supply relationship into a more integrated foundry partnership.

Potential benefits for AMD customers

Customers could see faster time‑to‑market for new AMD processors, as the tighter integration would streamline the supply chain. It could also reduce the risk of shortages that have plagued the industry in recent years.
