Pakistan Undersea Cable Fault Triggers Nationwide Internet Outage

2026-11-19 · 3 min read
A fault in a key undersea cable near the Strait of Hormuz has caused widespread internet disruptions across Pakistan, prompting authorities to reroute traffic and investigate the cause.

Pakistan’s telecommunications authority confirmed that a fault in an undersea network cable is behind the recent internet connectivity issues affecting the country. The outage, detected weeks after Iranian officials warned about regional infrastructure vulnerabilities, has forced traffic to be rerouted while experts work to pinpoint the cause and restore service.

Impact on Connectivity

Users across Pakistan have reported intermittent access to online services, with some regions experiencing complete outages. The fault has disrupted both domestic and international data flows, highlighting the critical role of submarine cables in global connectivity.

Response and Investigation

Transworld Associates, a leading telecommunications consultancy, is leading the investigation into the fault’s origin. The authority has urged the public to remain patient as repairs are underway and to use alternative routing options where possible.

Broader Implications

Experts warn that such incidents underscore the vulnerability of undersea cables to natural and man-made disruptions. Strengthening monitoring and rapid response protocols is essential to mitigate future outages and ensure resilient internet infrastructure.
