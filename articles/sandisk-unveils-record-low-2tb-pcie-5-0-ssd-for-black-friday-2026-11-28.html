SanDisk Unveils Record‑Low 2TB PCIe 5.0 SSD for Black Friday

2026-11-28 · 3 min read
SanDisk’s Optimus GX Pro 8100 2TB PCIe 5.0 SSD drops to $349, offering one of the cheapest Gen5 drives for gamers and creators.

SanDisk has slashed the price of its flagship Optimus GX Pro 8100 2TB PCIe 5.0 SSD to an all‑time low of $349, making it one of the most affordable 2TB Gen5 drives on the market. The move comes as the holiday shopping season ramps up, giving gamers, content creators and PC builders a budget‑friendly option for high‑speed storage.

Performance that matches the price

The Optimus GX Pro 8100 delivers read speeds up to 7,400 MB/s and write speeds up to 6,500 MB/s, thanks to its PCIe 5.0 interface and NVMe 2.0 protocol. These figures place it among the fastest consumer SSDs available, while the 2TB capacity ensures ample space for large game libraries, 4K footage and other data‑intensive workloads.

Why the price cut matters

With the price now at $0.18 per gigabyte, the drive offers a compelling value proposition. The discount of 21% from its original MSRP brings the unit into the same price bracket as some 2TB Gen4 SSDs, yet it retains the superior speed of a Gen5 drive. For users who need both speed and capacity without breaking the bank, the Optimus GX Pro 8100 is a strong contender.

Availability and shipping

SanDisk has announced that the discounted SSD will be available for purchase through major retailers and online marketplaces starting today. Shipping times vary by region, but most customers can expect delivery within 3–5 business days.
