Samsung Names New Mobile Division Head as Galaxy Z Fold 8 Debuts

2026-11-20 · 3 min read
Samsung’s mobile division welcomes Choi Won-Joon as chief, following the launch of the Galaxy Z Fold 8.

Samsung has announced a leadership change in its mobile division, appointing Choi Won-Joon as the new chief after a seven‑year tenure without a change at the top. The move comes as the company rolls out the Galaxy Z Fold 8, a foldable smartphone that has already begun to generate buzz among consumers and analysts.

New Leadership for a New Era

Choi Won-Joon, who has been with Samsung for many years, is stepping into the role of head of the mobile division. His appointment marks the first time in years that Samsung has changed the leadership of its mobile unit, signaling a shift in strategy as the company seeks to strengthen its position in the competitive smartphone market.

Galaxy Z Fold 8 Sets the Stage

The Galaxy Z Fold 8, launched under Choi’s leadership, showcases Samsung’s continued investment in foldable technology. Early reviews highlight the device’s improved durability and performance, positioning it as a strong contender against rivals in the premium segment.

Implications for Samsung’s Mobile Strategy

With a new chief at the helm, Samsung is expected to accelerate its focus on innovation and market expansion. The appointment aligns with the company’s broader goal of maintaining leadership in the evolving smartphone landscape, where foldable devices are becoming increasingly mainstream.
