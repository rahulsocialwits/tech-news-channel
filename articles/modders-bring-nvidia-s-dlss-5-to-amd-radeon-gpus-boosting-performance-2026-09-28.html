Modders Bring Nvidia’s DLSS 5 to AMD Radeon GPUs, Boosting Performance

2026-09-28 · 3 min read
Unofficial project delivers DLSS 5 neural rendering to AMD GPUs, raising frame rates in Cyberpunk 2077 by 74% in early tests.

In a surprising development for PC gamers, an unofficial project has enabled Nvidia’s DLSS 5 neural rendering on AMD Radeon GPUs. The modders’ build, released today, shows early testing in Cyberpunk 2077 climbing from roughly 30 frames per second to 50 frames per second after a rapid optimization cycle.

How the Mod Works

The project leverages a custom launcher that automates the installation of the DLSS 5 runtime on AMD hardware. By translating the neural network inference engine to run on Radeon’s RDNA architecture, the mod bypasses the need for Nvidia’s proprietary hardware.

Performance Gains

Initial benchmarks indicate a 74% performance boost in the game’s most demanding scenes. While the results are promising, the mod is still in a testing phase and may not be stable across all titles.

Implications for the GPU Market

If the technology matures, it could level the playing field between Nvidia and AMD, offering AMD users access to a feature that has traditionally been exclusive to Nvidia’s RTX line. The move also highlights the growing influence of community-driven projects in shaping the future of PC gaming hardware.
