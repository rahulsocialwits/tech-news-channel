Google Cloud Outage Disrupts UK Banking and Web Services

2026-10-20 · 3 min read
A widespread Google Cloud outage on October 20th caused service interruptions for UK banks and popular websites, highlighting the sector’s reliance on cloud infrastructure.

On October 20th, a significant outage of Google Cloud Services rippled across the United Kingdom, bringing a wave of disruptions to banking platforms and a host of high‑traffic websites. The incident, which began in the early hours of the day, affected a range of services that rely on Google Cloud’s infrastructure, including online banking portals for major UK banks and the Revolut app, as well as several popular news and e‑commerce sites.

Impact on Financial Services

Customers of several UK banks reported that their online banking portals were inaccessible, with error messages indicating server connectivity issues. The outage also impacted Revolut, a fintech company that uses Google Cloud for its backend operations, leading to temporary service unavailability for its users. Bank officials confirmed that the disruption was tied to a failure in Google Cloud’s data centers, which caused a cascade of service interruptions across the financial sector.

Broader Web Disruptions

Beyond banking, the outage affected a range of websites that host critical services and content. Users attempting to access news outlets, e‑commerce platforms, and other web services experienced slow load times or complete inaccessibility. The incident underscored the growing dependency of digital businesses on cloud providers for uptime and reliability.

Response and Recovery

Google Cloud’s engineering teams worked to isolate the root cause and restore services throughout the day. While the exact technical details remain under investigation, preliminary reports suggest that a misconfiguration in a key network component triggered the widespread outage. The company has pledged to conduct a thorough post‑mortem and implement safeguards to prevent similar incidents in the future.

Industry Implications

The outage has reignited discussions about the resilience of cloud‑centric architectures, especially for mission‑critical sectors such as finance. Experts argue that diversified cloud strategies and robust failover mechanisms are essential to mitigate the risk of single‑point failures. As businesses increasingly migrate to cloud platforms, the incident serves as a reminder of the importance of comprehensive disaster‑recovery planning.
