UGreen DXP4800 GT NAS Enclosure Slashes Cloud Costs with 20% Black Friday Discount

2026-11-22 · 3 min read
UGreen’s 4‑bay DXP4800 GT NAS drops 20% off on Amazon, offering dual 10GbE ports and up to 144TB of storage to help users cut cloud subscription fees.

For anyone looking to reduce reliance on cloud storage, UGreen’s DXP4800 GT 4‑bay NAS enclosure is making a splash this Black Friday. The device, which supports up to 144TB of storage and features dual 10GbE ports, is now available on Amazon at a 20% discount, bringing the price down to $132 off the original list price.

Why the DXP4800 GT is a Game‑Changer

The enclosure’s dual 10GbE connectivity allows for high‑speed data transfer, making it ideal for media professionals and power users who need rapid access to large files. With four bays, users can configure the drive layout for RAID 0, 1, 5, 6, or 10, providing flexibility for performance or redundancy.

Black Friday Deal Details

UGreen’s promotion marks the second time the DXP4800 GT has returned to its all‑time low price point. The 20% discount is available through the end of the Black Friday weekend, giving buyers a chance to secure a high‑capacity NAS at a fraction of the cost of comparable models.

Moving Beyond the Cloud

By investing in a local storage solution, users can avoid ongoing cloud subscription fees while maintaining full control over their data. The DXP4800 GT’s robust hardware and expandable storage make it a compelling alternative for those who want to keep their files on-premises.
