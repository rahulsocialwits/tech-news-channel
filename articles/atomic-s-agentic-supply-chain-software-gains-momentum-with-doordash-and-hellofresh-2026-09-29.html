Atomic’s Agentic Supply‑Chain Software Gains Momentum with DoorDash and HelloFresh

2026-09-29 · 3 min read
Atomic’s new agentic supply‑chain platform is now powering logistics for DoorDash and HelloFresh, signaling a shift toward autonomous supply‑chain management.

Atomic, a startup founded by former Tesla engineers, has announced that its agentic supply‑chain software is now being adopted by major players such as DoorDash and HelloFresh. The company raised $12.5 million in a recent funding round, according to a TechCrunch report dated September 29, 2026.

What Atomic Brings to the Table

Atomic’s platform is designed to automate the end‑to‑end flow of goods, from procurement to delivery. By leveraging machine‑learning models that can predict demand, optimize routing, and negotiate contracts with suppliers, the system reduces the need for human intervention in routine supply‑chain tasks.

Early Adoption by Food‑Delivery Giants

DoorDash, the on‑demand delivery service, and HelloFresh, the meal‑kit provider, are among the first companies to integrate Atomic’s software into their operations. The partnership allows these firms to streamline inventory management, cut lead times, and improve the accuracy of order fulfillment.

Funding and Future Plans

The $12.5 million round was led by a group of investors focused on logistics and AI. Atomic plans to use the capital to expand its product suite, enhance its predictive analytics capabilities, and scale its platform to serve additional industries beyond food delivery.

Industry Implications

Atomic’s success illustrates a broader trend toward agentic systems that can autonomously manage complex supply‑chain processes. As more companies seek to reduce operational costs and increase resilience, platforms like Atomic’s are likely to become a staple in modern logistics.
