UGREEN Launches HomeAgent: A Local‑First Smart Home Hub

2026-11-14 · 3 min read
UGREEN’s new HomeAgent collection brings cameras, speakers and more to the home with local processing, reducing reliance on cloud services.

UGREEN has unveiled its HomeAgent line, a suite of smart‑home devices that emphasize local processing over cloud dependence. The collection includes cameras, speakers, and other accessories that perform most of their functions inside the home network, according to the company’s announcement.

Local‑First Design

Unlike many smart‑home products that rely on cloud servers and subscription services, HomeAgent devices keep the bulk of their data handling on the user’s own network. This approach is intended to improve privacy, reduce latency, and lower ongoing costs for consumers.

Product Features

Key features highlighted by UGREEN include high‑definition cameras, open‑ear speakers, and directional microphones. The devices are designed to work together seamlessly, allowing users to interact naturally with their environment without needing external cloud services.

Market Context

The move toward local‑first solutions comes as privacy concerns grow and consumers seek more control over their smart‑home ecosystems. UGREEN’s strategy aligns with a broader industry trend toward edge computing in home automation.
