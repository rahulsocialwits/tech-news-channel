JSAUX FlipGo Dual‑Screen Portable Monitor Delivers Touch‑Enabled Displays and Built‑In Hub

2026-10-27 · 3 min read
The FlipGo offers two 16‑inch touch screens, a USB hub, and a sleek design for mobile productivity.

The JSAUX FlipGo has been unveiled as a dual‑screen portable monitor that promises to bring desktop‑style multitasking to the road. Each side of the device sports a 16‑inch display that supports touch input, allowing users to interact directly with applications on both screens at once.

Key Features

  • Dual 16‑inch touch displays with high resolution and wide viewing angles
  • Integrated USB hub with multiple ports for connecting peripherals
  • Lightweight and foldable design for easy transport
  • Compatible with laptops, tablets, and smartphones via USB‑C or HDMI

Performance and Build

Reviewers have noted that the FlipGo’s touch responsiveness is smooth, making it suitable for drawing, note‑taking, and gaming. The built‑in hub reduces cable clutter, and the device’s chassis feels sturdy despite its portability. Battery life is not a concern, as the monitor relies on external power from the host device.

Use Cases

Professionals who need a second screen on the go—such as designers, developers, and sales teams—can benefit from the FlipGo’s ability to run multiple applications side by side. The touch capability also opens up possibilities for interactive presentations and collaborative work sessions.
