Team Group CEO Warns Memory Crisis Could Reach Point Where Even Money Can’t Buy Goods

2026-11-05 · 3 min read
Team Group CEO warns that DRAM shortages may force prices to stay high, with no downward trend expected next year.

In a recent earnings call, Team Group CEO Chen Ching‑wen highlighted the escalating memory crisis that could push DRAM prices to unprecedented levels. The company, a major player in the semiconductor supply chain, cautioned that the current shortage might reach a point where even substantial financial investment would not secure inventory.

Key Points from the Call

Chen Ching‑wen emphasized that the market is experiencing a “no downward trend in price whatsoever,” suggesting that the supply-demand imbalance is likely to persist into the next year. He noted that the industry’s ability to absorb new production capacity is limited by both manufacturing constraints and the high cost of raw materials.

Implications for the Tech Industry

With DRAM being a critical component for everything from smartphones to data centers, the sustained price pressure could ripple across multiple sectors. Companies may need to accelerate their transition to alternative memory technologies or adopt more efficient memory architectures to mitigate the impact.

Industry Response

Other semiconductor firms have echoed concerns about the supply chain bottleneck, pointing to the need for coordinated investment in new fabrication facilities. Analysts predict that the memory market could see a gradual shift toward higher-capacity, lower-cost solutions as the industry adapts.
