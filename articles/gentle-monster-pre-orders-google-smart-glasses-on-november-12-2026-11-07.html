Gentle Monster Pre‑Orders Google Smart Glasses on November 12

2026-11-07 · 3 min read
Gentle Monster’s Intelligent Eyewear will be available for pre‑order on November 12, marking a new partnership with Google for smart glasses.

Gentle Monster, the Korean eyewear brand known for its avant‑garde designs, has confirmed that its new line of Intelligent Eyewear will be available for pre‑order on November 12. The announcement comes as part of a partnership with Google, which has been quietly developing a line of smart glasses for several years.

What the partnership means

The collaboration signals Google’s continued interest in wearable technology, following the earlier launch of its original Nest Doorbell and other smart‑home devices. While the exact specifications of the glasses have not yet been released, Gentle Monster’s statement indicates that the product will combine Google’s software capabilities with the brand’s distinctive aesthetic.

Pre‑order details

Customers who wish to secure a pair of the Intelligent Eyewear can place a pre‑order starting on November 12. The pre‑order window will likely run for a limited time, as is common with high‑profile tech releases. Once the pre‑orders close, production is expected to begin shortly thereafter, with shipping slated for early 2027.

Industry context

Smart glasses have long been a niche market, with early attempts from companies like Google Glass and Microsoft HoloLens. The new partnership with Gentle Monster may help bring the technology to a broader consumer audience by offering a stylish, fashion‑forward option that still delivers the functionality users expect from a Google‑powered device.
