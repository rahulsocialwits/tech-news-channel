Google Messages Launches Enhanced Protection Mode to Fight Spam and Scams

2026-10-24 · 3 min read
Google rolls out a new spam‑blocking feature in its Messages app, adding a protective layer for Android users.

Google has expanded the security of its Messages app with the rollout of an Enhanced Protection mode designed to curb spam and scam attempts. The update, announced earlier this month, is now available to Android users worldwide.

What the new mode does

Enhanced Protection adds a verification step for incoming messages that appear suspicious. When a message triggers the filter, the app displays a warning and blocks the sender’s number until the user confirms the contact. This helps prevent phishing links, malicious attachments, and other deceptive tactics that have plagued mobile messaging.

How it works

The feature relies on Google’s existing spam‑detection algorithms, which analyze message content, sender reputation, and known malicious patterns. If a message is flagged, the app shows a banner that reads “This message may be spam” and offers the option to block or report the sender. Users can also opt out of the protection if they prefer a more open inbox.

Impact for users

By adding a second layer of scrutiny, Google aims to reduce the number of scam attempts that reach users’ phones. The company has stated that the feature will be continuously refined as new spam tactics emerge.

Availability

Enhanced Protection is being rolled out gradually. Android users who have the latest version of the Messages app will see the option in the app’s settings under “Security.” The feature is expected to be available to all users by the end of the month.
