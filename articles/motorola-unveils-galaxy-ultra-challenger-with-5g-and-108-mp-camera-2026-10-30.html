Motorola Unveils Galaxy Ultra Challenger with 5G and 108‑MP Camera

2026-10-30 · 3 min read
Motorola announces launch date for its flagship Galaxy Ultra, featuring a 108‑megapixel sensor, 5G connectivity and a 6.7‑inch OLED display.

Motorola has finally set a release date for its highly anticipated Galaxy Ultra, the company’s flagship smartphone that promises to compete with the industry’s top models. The device will ship on October 27 in the United States, according to the company’s official announcement.

Key Features

  • 108‑MP primary camera – The Galaxy Ultra’s main sensor is the largest in a consumer phone, offering unprecedented detail for photography enthusiasts.
  • 5G‑enabled – The handset supports both sub‑6GHz and mmWave bands, ensuring fast data speeds across the country.
  • 6.7‑inch OLED display – With a 120Hz refresh rate and HDR10+ support, the screen delivers vibrant colors and smooth scrolling.

Design and Build

The Galaxy Ultra features a glass front and back with a metal frame, giving it a premium look and feel. The device also includes an in‑display fingerprint sensor and a 5000‑mAh battery that supports 30W fast charging.

Software and Ecosystem

Running on Android 14 with Motorola’s custom skin, the phone will receive regular security updates for at least three years. Motorola also announced a partnership with Google to bring the latest AI‑driven features to the device, such as real‑time translation and enhanced photo editing.

Market Position

With its high‑resolution camera and competitive pricing, Motorola aims to capture a share of the premium smartphone market that has been dominated by Samsung and Apple. The company’s marketing team highlighted the Galaxy Ultra’s “unmatched photography experience” as a key selling point.
