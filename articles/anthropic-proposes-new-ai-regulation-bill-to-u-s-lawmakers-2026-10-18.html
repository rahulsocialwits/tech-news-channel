Anthropic Proposes New AI Regulation Bill to U.S. Lawmakers

2026-10-18 · 3 min read
Anthropic has drafted a bill that would let Washington block an AI model if it poses a severe risk, requiring judicial approval before release.

In a move that could reshape the regulatory landscape for artificial intelligence, Anthropic has presented a draft bill to U.S. lawmakers that would grant the federal government the power to block the release of an AI model if it is deemed to pose an imminent risk of causing significant harm. The proposal, which was announced on October 17, 2026, would allow Washington to intervene when a model could potentially kill more than 50 people or cause damages exceeding $1 billion. However, the bill would not grant blanket authority; it would require a judge’s approval before any action could be taken.

Key Provisions of the Draft Bill

The proposed legislation centers on a risk‑based approach. Under the bill, the government would be able to request a halt to the deployment of a model that meets either of two thresholds: a projected death toll of more than 50 individuals or projected financial damages surpassing $1 billion. The measure is designed to target only the most dangerous AI systems, rather than imposing blanket restrictions on all AI development.

Judicial Oversight to Balance Power

Anthropic’s draft includes a safeguard that would require judicial approval before the government could enforce a block. This provision aims to prevent potential overreach by ensuring that any decision to halt an AI model is reviewed by an independent court. The requirement for a judge’s approval is intended to provide a check on executive power while still allowing for swift action in cases of extreme risk.

Industry Reaction and Context

While the proposal has not yet been formally introduced in Congress, it follows a broader trend of increasing scrutiny over large language models and other advanced AI systems. The bill comes at a time when several tech companies are grappling with the ethical and safety implications of their products, and it reflects growing calls for clearer regulatory frameworks.

Potential Impact on AI Development

If enacted, the legislation could set a precedent for how governments interact with AI developers. By tying the ability to block a model to specific risk thresholds and judicial oversight, the bill seeks to balance innovation with public safety. Developers would need to assess the potential impact of their models more carefully, and they might face additional compliance requirements if their systems approach the defined risk limits.

Next Steps

Anthropic has indicated that it will continue to engage with policymakers and stakeholders to refine the proposal. The company’s draft is currently open for public comment, and it is expected that lawmakers will review the details before deciding whether to advance the bill through the legislative process.
