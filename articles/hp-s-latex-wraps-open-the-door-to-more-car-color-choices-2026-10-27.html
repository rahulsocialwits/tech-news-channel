HP’s Latex Wraps Open the Door to More Car Color Choices

2026-10-27 · 3 min read
HP partners with Ferrari to create customizable car wraps that expand color options and dealer services.

HP has announced a new line of latex wraps that could transform the automotive customization market. The company’s collaboration with Ferrari is aimed at offering a broader palette of paint finishes for cars, while also providing new dealer services and easier personalization options for vehicle owners.

What the wraps bring to the table

Unlike traditional paint jobs, the latex wraps are applied as a thin film that can be removed or replaced without damaging the underlying paint. This gives customers the flexibility to change their car’s appearance more frequently and at a lower cost. HP’s technology also supports a wide range of colors and finishes, including metallic and matte options that were previously difficult to achieve with conventional coatings.

Implications for the automotive industry

By partnering with a high‑profile brand like Ferrari, HP is positioning its wraps as a premium product that could appeal to luxury car owners. The collaboration also hints at potential new dealer services, such as on‑site wrap installation and maintenance, which could streamline the customization process for both dealers and consumers.

Future outlook

HP’s entry into the automotive wrap market could spur further innovation in vehicle personalization. As more automakers and aftermarket companies look for cost‑effective ways to offer unique styling options, the latex wrap technology may become a standard tool in the industry’s customization toolkit.
