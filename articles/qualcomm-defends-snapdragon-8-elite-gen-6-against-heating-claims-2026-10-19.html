Qualcomm Defends Snapdragon 8‑Elite Gen 6 Against Heating Claims

2026-10-19 · 3 min read
Qualcomm’s executive says the new Snapdragon 8‑Elite Gen 6 chip has no system thermal issues, countering reports of overheating on the S27 Ultra.

Qualcomm has responded to rumors that its latest Snapdragon 8‑Elite Gen 6 processor is prone to overheating, a concern that surfaced after reports of high temperatures on the S27 Ultra smartphone. In a statement released on Monday, a Qualcomm executive clarified that the chip does not exhibit any system thermal issues under normal operating conditions.

What the claim was about

Earlier this week, tech outlets highlighted that the S27 Ultra, powered by the Snapdragon 8‑Elite Gen 6, was experiencing elevated temperatures during heavy use. The reports suggested that the device’s cooling system might be insufficient for the chip’s performance profile.

Qualcomm’s response

The company’s spokesperson emphasized that the Snapdragon 8‑Elite Gen 6 has been rigorously tested across a range of workloads and environments. According to Qualcomm, the processor’s thermal design power (TDP) is within the specifications set for the chip, and no overheating has been observed in real‑world usage scenarios.

Implications for consumers

For buyers of the S27 Ultra and other devices featuring the Snapdragon 8‑Elite Gen 6, Qualcomm’s statement provides reassurance that the chip’s performance should not be compromised by heat. The company also noted that its cooling solutions are optimized to maintain safe operating temperatures.
