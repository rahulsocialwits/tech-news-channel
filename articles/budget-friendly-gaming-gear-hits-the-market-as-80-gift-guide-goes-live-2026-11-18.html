Budget‑Friendly Gaming Gear Hits the Market as $80 Gift Guide Goes Live

2026-11-18 · 3 min read
Gamers can score keyboards, mice, headsets and more for under $80 this holiday season, according to Tom's Hardware's latest gift guide.

Gamers looking to upgrade their setup without breaking the bank will find plenty of options in Tom's Hardware’s new gift guide, which highlights a range of peripherals priced under $80. The guide includes mechanical keyboards, gaming mice, and headsets that combine solid performance with affordable pricing.

Key Highlights

  • Mechanical Keyboards – Models featuring tactile switches and RGB lighting are available for as low as $49, offering a premium typing experience without the high price tag.
  • Gaming Mice – Ergonomic designs with adjustable DPI settings start at $29, giving players precise control for competitive play.
  • Headsets – Audio solutions with surround sound and noise‑cancelling microphones can be found for under $60, ensuring clear communication during multiplayer sessions.

Why It Matters

With the holiday season approaching, many consumers are searching for value‑packed gifts that still deliver quality. By curating a selection of budget‑friendly gear, Tom's Hardware provides a practical resource for both seasoned gamers and newcomers looking to enhance their experience without a hefty investment.
