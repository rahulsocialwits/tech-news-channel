Apple Unveils New 4K TV and HomePod Mini, Shipping Oct. 30

2026-10-11 · 3 min read
Apple announces the launch of its new Apple TV 4K and HomePod Mini, set to ship on October 30, expanding its streaming and smart‑home lineup.

Apple has officially announced that its latest Apple TV 4K and HomePod Mini will begin shipping on October 30. The move comes as the company continues to broaden its ecosystem of streaming and smart‑home devices.

Apple TV 4K: A New Streaming Hub

The updated Apple TV 4K promises a richer viewing experience with support for HDR10+ and Dolby Vision, as well as a redesigned remote that offers improved accessibility features. Users will also benefit from a streamlined interface that integrates Apple’s growing library of streaming services.

HomePod Mini: Compact Smart Speaker

The HomePod Mini, a smaller and more affordable version of Apple’s flagship speaker, brings spatial audio and Siri voice control to a pocket‑sized package. It is designed to work seamlessly with HomeKit, allowing users to control smart‑home devices and set up automations through the Home app.

Expanding the Ecosystem

By adding these two products to its lineup, Apple is reinforcing its strategy to create a cohesive ecosystem that spans entertainment, home automation, and personal assistants. The new devices are expected to compete directly with Amazon’s Fire TV and Echo lineups, as well as Google’s Nest and Chromecast products.
