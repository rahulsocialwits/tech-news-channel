Google’s AI‑Driven Personalisation Could Shift Targeted Advertising

2026-10-26 · 3 min read
Google is testing AI‑powered personalisation that may enable more precise ad targeting based on user data, raising questions about privacy and ad effectiveness.

Google is experimenting with artificial intelligence to personalise the advertising experience for users. The company’s new approach uses AI to analyse a broader range of user data, potentially allowing advertisers to deliver more relevant ads. The initiative is part of Google’s broader push to integrate AI across its product suite, a move that has already sparked debate among privacy advocates and advertisers alike.

How the AI Personalisation Works

According to a recent article from the Indian Express, Google’s AI system processes user data from multiple sources—including search history, app usage, and location information—to build a more detailed profile of each user. This profile is then used to match ads that are more likely to resonate with the individual’s interests and needs.

Implications for Advertisers

For marketers, the promise of higher relevance could translate into better conversion rates and a higher return on ad spend. By targeting users with ads that align closely with their current context and preferences, advertisers may see increased engagement and sales. However, the effectiveness of this approach will depend on the accuracy of the AI models and the quality of the underlying data.

Privacy Concerns and Regulatory Scrutiny

While the technology offers clear benefits for advertisers, it also raises significant privacy concerns. The use of more granular data could lead to a perception of intrusive surveillance, especially if users are unaware of how their information is being combined and used. Regulators in the European Union and the United States are already scrutinising how tech giants handle personal data, and Google’s new AI‑driven system could become a focal point in future policy discussions.

Industry Response

Industry analysts note that Google is not alone in exploring AI for ad targeting. Other major platforms, such as Meta and Amazon, have also invested heavily in machine learning to improve ad relevance. However, Google’s extensive data ecosystem gives it a unique advantage in building comprehensive user profiles.

Future Outlook

As AI continues to evolve, the line between personalised content and targeted advertising will blur further. Google’s current experiments are a step toward a future where ads are almost seamlessly integrated into the user experience. Whether this will lead to a net positive for consumers and advertisers remains to be seen, but the potential for more efficient advertising is undeniable.
