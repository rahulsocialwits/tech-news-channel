Vivo Unveils X500 Series and First Smartwatch in India

2026-11-26 · 3 min read
Vivo launches its X500 Pro Max, X500, and X500 smartphones along with the Watch GT2 smartwatch, featuring a 2.07‑inch display and up to 25 days of battery life.

In a move that signals its intent to broaden its product portfolio, Chinese smartphone maker Vivo rolled out a trio of new phones and its first smartwatch in India on Thursday. The company introduced the X500 Pro Max, the X500, and the X500, all of which share a similar design language but differ in specifications and price points. Alongside the phones, Vivo also launched the Watch GT2, its inaugural smartwatch aimed at the Indian market.

Phone lineup details

The X500 Pro Max is positioned as the flagship of the series, boasting a higher‑resolution display, more advanced camera modules, and a larger battery compared to its siblings. The X500 and X500 models offer a more budget‑friendly option, with slightly lower resolution screens and fewer camera sensors, but still maintain the brand’s signature design cues. All three phones feature a 6.5‑inch OLED panel, a 120‑Hz refresh rate, and a 48‑MP primary camera setup.

Watch GT2 specifications

The Watch GT2 is a 2.07‑inch smartwatch that comes with a full‑color display and a claimed battery life of up to 25 days on a single charge. The device supports heart‑rate monitoring, sleep tracking, and a range of fitness‑related metrics. It also offers LTE connectivity, allowing users to make calls and receive notifications without needing to be tethered to a smartphone. The watch is available in several colors, including black, white, and rose gold.

Market strategy and timing

Vivo’s launch comes at a time when the Indian smartphone market is highly competitive, with brands such as Samsung, Xiaomi, and OnePlus vying for market share. By introducing a smartwatch alongside its phone lineup, Vivo aims to capture consumers looking for an integrated ecosystem of devices. The company’s decision to launch the Watch GT2 in India also reflects the growing demand for wearable technology in the country, where fitness and health tracking have become mainstream.

Consumer reception and expectations

Early reviews suggest that the X500 series offers solid performance for its price range, with the Pro Max variant receiving praise for its camera performance and battery life. The Watch GT2 has been highlighted for its long battery life and robust fitness tracking features, though some reviewers note that the device’s software could benefit from additional third‑party app support.

Future outlook

Vivo’s expansion into wearables signals a broader strategy to diversify its product offerings beyond smartphones. The company has indicated plans to roll out additional smartwatch models in the coming months, potentially incorporating more advanced health sensors and tighter integration with its smartphone ecosystem. As the Indian market continues to grow, Vivo’s new lineup positions it to compete more aggressively against established players in both the phone and wearable segments.
