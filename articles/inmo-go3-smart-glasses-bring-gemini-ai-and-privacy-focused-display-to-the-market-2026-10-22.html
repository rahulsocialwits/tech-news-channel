INMO GO3 Smart Glasses Bring Gemini AI and Privacy‑Focused Display to the Market

2026-10-22 · 3 min read
The new INMO GO3 smart glasses combine a high‑resolution display with Google Gemini integration and a camera‑privacy feature, addressing two common smart‑glasses pain points.

INMO has launched its GO3 smart glasses, a device that tackles two of the biggest shortcomings in today’s wearable tech: a lack of a true futuristic display and intrusive camera usage. The GO3 features a high‑resolution screen that delivers a clear, immersive visual experience, while its built‑in camera is designed with privacy in mind, ensuring users can keep their surroundings secure.

Gemini AI Integration

One of the standout features of the GO3 is its seamless integration with Google’s Gemini AI. The glasses can pull in Gemini’s conversational capabilities, allowing users to ask questions, get real‑time translations, and manage tasks hands‑free. This integration positions the GO3 as a practical tool for productivity and everyday convenience.

Privacy‑Focused Camera Design

Smart glasses have long struggled with camera privacy concerns. INMO addresses this by incorporating a clever camera‑privacy mechanism that automatically disables the camera when it detects a user’s face or when the device is not in active use. This feature helps mitigate the risk of accidental recordings and builds trust with consumers wary of constant surveillance.

Market Impact

By combining a high‑quality display, AI‑powered assistance, and a privacy‑centric camera, the INMO GO3 sets a new benchmark for what consumers can expect from wearable tech. Its launch could spur further innovation in the smart‑glasses space, encouraging other manufacturers to prioritize both user experience and privacy.
