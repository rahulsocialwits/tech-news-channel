Apple to Shut Down Online Support Community on November 20

2026-11-11 · 3 min read
Apple will permanently close its online Support Community forum, ending a key resource for users and developers.

Apple announced that its long‑running online Support Community forum will be permanently closed on November 20. The decision marks the end of a platform that has served millions of users and developers with troubleshooting tips, feature discussions, and peer‑to‑peer support since its launch in 2005.

What the Closure Means

The forum has been a central hub for users to ask questions about macOS, iOS, iPadOS, watchOS, and tvOS, as well as for developers to share insights on App Store policies and software development best practices. With the closure, Apple is encouraging users to turn to its official support channels, including the Apple Support app, the Apple Support website, and the Apple Support Communities mobile app, which offers a more streamlined experience.

Apple’s Rationale

Apple’s support team cited a shift toward more structured, AI‑driven help resources and a desire to consolidate support efforts into fewer, more efficient channels. The company has been investing heavily in its AI‑powered chat assistant, which can answer a wide range of queries without human intervention. By redirecting traffic to these newer tools, Apple hopes to provide faster, more consistent assistance.

Impact on the Community

Community moderators and frequent contributors have expressed disappointment, noting that the forum has been a valuable place for knowledge sharing. Some users have already begun migrating their discussions to the Apple Support app, which offers a similar question‑and‑answer format but with tighter moderation and a more polished interface.

Future of Apple Support

Apple’s broader strategy appears to focus on integrating AI and machine learning into its support ecosystem. The company’s recent updates to the Apple Support app include enhanced search capabilities and personalized recommendations based on device usage patterns. These features aim to reduce the need for community‑driven troubleshooting by providing instant, context‑aware solutions.

What Users Should Do

Users who rely on the forum for troubleshooting should start familiarizing themselves with the Apple Support app and the Apple Support website. Both platforms now offer comprehensive knowledge bases, live chat options, and the ability to schedule appointments with Apple technicians. For developers, the Apple Developer Forums remain active and continue to provide a space for technical discussions and policy clarifications.
