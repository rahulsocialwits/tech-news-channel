Amazon Acquires Decart’s AI Optimization Tech in $7.5 B Deal

2026-11-23 · 3 min read
Amazon is buying Decart’s AI optimization technology and research team for $7.5 billion as it prepares to sell its Trainium chips.

Amazon announced a $7.5 billion acquisition of Decart, a startup known for its AI optimization technology and research team. The deal is part of Amazon’s strategy to strengthen its AI infrastructure ahead of the planned sale of its Trainium chips. Decart’s technology focuses on improving the efficiency and performance of machine learning models, a capability that Amazon can integrate into its cloud services and AI workloads.

Strategic Fit for Amazon’s AI Ecosystem

The purchase aligns with Amazon’s broader push to offer end‑to‑end AI solutions to developers and enterprises. By adding Decart’s optimization tools, Amazon can help customers reduce training times and lower operational costs on its AWS platform. The acquisition also positions Amazon to compete more aggressively with other cloud providers that are investing heavily in AI acceleration.

Implications for the AI Hardware Market

Amazon’s decision to sell Trainium chips while acquiring Decart’s software suggests a shift toward a software‑centric AI strategy. Trainium, Amazon’s custom AI inference chip, has been a key component of its AI services. The sale could free up capital for further software development and potentially open new revenue streams from licensing optimization technology.

Industry Reactions

Analysts view the deal as a signal that Amazon is prioritizing AI software capabilities over hardware. The $7.5 billion price tag reflects the high value placed on AI optimization tools in the current market, where performance gains can translate into significant cost savings for large‑scale AI deployments.
