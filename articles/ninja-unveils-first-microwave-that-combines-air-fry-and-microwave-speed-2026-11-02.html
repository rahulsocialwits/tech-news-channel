Ninja Unveils First Microwave That Combines Air‑Fry and Microwave Speed

2026-11-02 · 3 min read
The Ninja Crispi Microwave introduces FusionCrisp technology, delivering fast heating with crispy results, positioning it as a dual‑function kitchen appliance.

Ninja, known for its kitchen gadgets, has launched its first microwave, the Ninja Crispi Microwave, that promises to replace two appliances in one. The device uses FusionCrisp technology, a proprietary system that blends the rapid heating of a microwave with the crisping effect of an air fryer. According to the company, the microwave can cook a frozen pizza in under a minute while still producing a golden, crunchy crust.

How FusionCrisp Works

FusionCrisp combines a traditional microwave cavity with a convection fan and a high‑temperature heating element. The fan circulates hot air around the food, while the heating element provides the direct heat needed for crisping. This dual‑mode approach allows the appliance to deliver both speed and texture, a feature that has drawn attention from home cooks and tech reviewers alike.

Market Impact

Industry analysts note that the Ninja Crispi Microwave could disrupt the small kitchen appliance market by offering a single device that performs multiple functions. The company’s marketing campaign highlights the convenience of having one appliance that can microwave, air‑fry, and roast, potentially reducing countertop clutter and energy usage.

Consumer Reception

Early reviews praise the microwave’s performance and the ease of use. Users report that the device heats food quickly and that the crisping feature works well on a variety of foods, from frozen meals to fresh vegetables. Some reviewers also point out that the appliance’s sleek design fits well in modern kitchens.
