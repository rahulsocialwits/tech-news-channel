Waymo Launches Autonomous Airport Rides at Las Vegas International

2026-10-21 · 3 min read
Waymo begins offering self‑driving pickups and drop‑offs at Las Vegas airport terminals, expanding its autonomous mobility services.

Waymo, Alphabet’s autonomous‑vehicle arm, has begun offering rides at the Las Vegas International Airport, marking a significant step in the deployment of self‑driving cars for public transportation. The service will operate from dedicated autonomous vehicle zones located at Terminal 1 and Terminal 3, where passengers can board and disembark without the need for a human driver.

How the Service Works

Passengers can request a Waymo vehicle through the company’s mobile app or via the airport’s digital kiosks. Once the ride is confirmed, the autonomous car will navigate to the designated pickup point, wait for the passenger, and then transport them to their chosen destination within the airport or to nearby hotels and attractions. The vehicles are equipped with a full suite of sensors, including lidar, radar, and cameras, to safely navigate the complex airport environment.

Implications for Airport Mobility

By integrating autonomous vehicles into its ground‑transportation network, Las Vegas International Airport aims to reduce congestion, lower wait times, and provide a more seamless travel experience. The move also positions Waymo as a key player in the emerging market for autonomous airport shuttles, a niche that has attracted interest from several major carriers and airport operators worldwide.

Future Expansion

Waymo has indicated that it plans to expand its airport services to other major hubs in the United States over the next few years. The company’s focus on safety and reliability will be critical as it scales the service to accommodate higher passenger volumes and more complex routing scenarios.
