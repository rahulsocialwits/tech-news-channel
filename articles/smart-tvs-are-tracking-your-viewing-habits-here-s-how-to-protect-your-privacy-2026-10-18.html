Smart TVs Are Tracking Your Viewing Habits—Here’s How to Protect Your Privacy

2026-10-18 · 3 min read
A new study shows that many smart TVs collect detailed viewing data, raising privacy concerns. Learn what manufacturers are tracking and how users can limit data collection.

Recent research from Engadget has revealed that a significant number of smart TVs are actively tracking what viewers watch. The study examined a range of popular models and found that manufacturers routinely log viewing habits, including the titles watched, the time of day, and even the duration of each session.

What Is Being Tracked?

The data collected includes:

  • Program titles and episode numbers
  • Viewing times and session lengths
  • Device identifiers and network information

Manufacturers use this information to personalize recommendations, improve content delivery, and target advertising. However, the level of detail captured raises significant privacy questions, especially for households with children or individuals who prefer to keep their viewing preferences private.

How to Limit Data Collection

Users can take several steps to reduce the amount of data shared with manufacturers:

  1. Check the privacy settings: Most smart TVs allow users to disable data collection or limit the types of data that can be sent to the manufacturer.
  2. Use a VPN or local network isolation: Routing traffic through a VPN or isolating the TV on a separate network can help prevent external access to the device’s data.
  3. Regularly update firmware: Manufacturers often release updates that include privacy improvements or new settings to control data sharing.

Engadget’s article also notes that some manufacturers provide an option to opt out of data collection entirely, though this may limit certain features such as personalized recommendations.

Industry Response

In response to growing consumer concerns, several manufacturers have announced plans to enhance transparency. They are working on clearer privacy notices and more granular controls that allow users to decide exactly what data is shared and with whom.

While the convenience of smart TVs continues to grow, users should remain vigilant about the data they share. By understanding what is being tracked and taking proactive steps to limit data collection, viewers can enjoy their entertainment without compromising their privacy.
