Nintendo Switch 2 Hits Record Low Price on Amazon UK

2026-09-29 · 3 min read
The Nintendo Switch 2 is now available for just £354.99 on Amazon UK, the lowest price yet for the handheld console.

Gamers across the UK have a new reason to celebrate as the Nintendo Switch 2 drops to a record low price on Amazon. The handheld console, which boasts a 7.9‑inch 1080p screen, a 4K dock, and magnetic controllers, is now available for just £354.99, a discount of £65 from its launch price.

What the price cut means

The price reduction comes at a time when Nintendo is looking to broaden its market share in the competitive handheld segment. By offering the Switch 2 at a more accessible price point, the company hopes to attract both new players and those who previously found the console out of reach.

Key features of the Switch 2

  • 7.9‑inch 1080p display for crisp visuals
  • 4K output when docked for a console‑like experience
  • Magnetic controller attachment for easy detachment and storage
  • Improved battery life and faster charging compared to its predecessor

Availability and shipping

Amazon UK lists the Switch 2 for immediate delivery, with standard shipping options available. The console is also expected to be in stock at major retailers across the UK in the coming weeks.
