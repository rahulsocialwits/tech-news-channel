Meta AI Director Rohit Patel Leaves to Launch Ndoc, a New Office Suite Challenger

2026-10-27 · 3 min read
Former Meta AI lead Rohit Patel exits to build Ndoc, a startup aiming to compete with Microsoft Office and Google Workspace.

Meta’s AI division has lost a key figure as Rohit Patel, who spent eight years on AI evaluations and reinforcement learning, announced his departure. Patel is set to focus on his own venture, Ndoc, which plans to offer an alternative to the dominant Microsoft Office Suite and Google Workspace.

From Meta to the Startup World

Patel’s exit follows a period of intense AI development at Meta, where he led teams that refined reinforcement learning models for content moderation and user experience. The move signals a broader trend of experienced AI engineers pivoting to create niche software solutions that challenge established enterprise platforms.

Ndoc’s Ambitious Goal

According to TechCrunch, Ndoc’s mission is to provide a lightweight, AI‑powered office experience that can compete with the feature sets of Microsoft Office and Google Workspace. While the startup has not yet released a product, early reports suggest it will leverage advanced language models to streamline document creation, collaboration, and data analysis.

Industry Implications

Patel’s departure and the launch of Ndoc could intensify competition in the office software market, traditionally dominated by a few large players. If Ndoc succeeds in delivering a compelling AI‑enhanced suite, it may attract users seeking more privacy, customization, or cost‑effective alternatives.

Looking Ahead

With the AI landscape rapidly evolving, Ndoc’s success will depend on its ability to integrate cutting‑edge models, maintain data security, and offer a seamless user experience. The tech community will be watching closely as the startup moves from concept to market.
