Microsoft’s Automatic Super Resolution Now Powers Xbox Ally X and Select Copilot+ PCs

2026-11-11 · 3 min read
Microsoft’s upscaling technology, Automatic Super Resolution, has been released for the ROG Xbox Ally X and a handful of Copilot+ PCs, boosting game visuals on handheld devices.

Microsoft has officially rolled out its Automatic Super Resolution (ASR) feature for the ROG Xbox Ally X, a handheld gaming console that blends the power of a PC with the portability of a console. The update also brings ASR to a select group of Copilot+ PCs, giving users a new way to enhance low‑resolution games and media on the fly.

What is Automatic Super Resolution?

ASR is an upscaling technology that uses machine‑learning models to generate higher‑resolution frames from lower‑resolution input. By analyzing patterns in the source image, the algorithm predicts and adds detail, producing a sharper and more detailed output without the need for native high‑resolution assets.

Why the Xbox Ally X?

The ROG Xbox Ally X is a handheld that runs Windows 11 and is designed for gamers who want a portable PC experience. With a 7‑inch display and a custom AMD processor, the device is capable of running many PC titles, but some games still run at 720p or 1080p to keep performance smooth. ASR allows those titles to be upscaled to 1440p or even 4K, giving players a clearer picture without sacrificing speed.

How It Works on the Ally X

When a game is launched, the Ally X’s software automatically detects the resolution and applies ASR if the game is running below the device’s native resolution. The upscaling happens in real time, with minimal impact on frame rates. Players can toggle the feature on or off in the settings menu, and the system will remember the choice for future sessions.

Extension to Copilot+ PCs

Microsoft’s Copilot+ PCs are a line of pre‑configured workstations that come with the Copilot AI assistant pre‑installed. The ASR update extends to a handful of these models, allowing users to upscale video content, streaming services, and older games. The feature is integrated into the Windows Graphics Settings panel, where users can choose the desired output resolution and quality level.

Impact on Gaming and Media

For gamers, ASR means that titles that were previously limited by hardware constraints can now look sharper on the Ally X. Developers can also benefit by reducing the need to create multiple resolution assets for handheld releases. Media consumers, on the other hand, can enjoy higher‑quality playback of 720p or 1080p videos on their PCs without needing a native 4K source.

Future Outlook

Microsoft has indicated that ASR will continue to improve as the underlying models are refined. Future updates may expand support to more devices and offer additional customization options, such as selective upscaling for specific UI elements or textures. The company also plans to integrate the technology into its cloud gaming services, potentially allowing users to stream games at higher resolutions without local hardware upgrades.

Conclusion

The release of Automatic Super Resolution for the ROG Xbox Ally X and select Copilot+ PCs marks a significant step in bringing high‑quality visuals to portable and desktop gaming. By leveraging machine‑learning upscaling, Microsoft is providing a practical solution for users who want better graphics without the cost of new hardware.
