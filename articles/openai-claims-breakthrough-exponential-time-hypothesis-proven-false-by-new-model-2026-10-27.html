OpenAI Claims Breakthrough: Exponential Time Hypothesis Proven False by New Model

2026-10-27 · 3 min read
OpenAI announced that its latest models generated a proof that the Exponential Time Hypothesis is false, marking a milestone in theoretical computer science.

OpenAI has announced that its advanced language models have produced a proof that the Exponential Time Hypothesis (ETH) is false. The ETH, a cornerstone assumption in computational complexity theory, posits that certain problems cannot be solved in sub‑exponential time. The new proof, generated by the company’s models, challenges this long‑standing belief and could reshape the field of theoretical computer science.

What the Proof Means

According to OpenAI, the models were able to construct a formal argument that contradicts ETH. While the details of the proof are still under peer review, the announcement signals that artificial intelligence can now contribute to foundational research in mathematics and computer science. The company also released additional results for a range of math and physics problems, indicating a broader impact of its models on scientific discovery.

Implications for the Industry

If the proof holds up under scrutiny, it could lead to new algorithms that solve previously intractable problems more efficiently. This would have ripple effects across fields that rely on complex optimization, such as cryptography, logistics, and machine learning. The announcement also underscores the growing role of AI in accelerating research that traditionally required human expertise.

Next Steps

OpenAI has made the proof available to the research community for verification. The company is also continuing to explore other mathematical and physical challenges, aiming to push the boundaries of what AI can achieve in scientific domains.
