Used PS5 Pros Surge to $1,399 as New Units Remain Out of Stock

2026-10-06 · 3 min read
GameStop hikes refurbished PS5 Pro price to $1,399, 50% above new model, amid ongoing supply shortages.

GameStop has announced a new price for its refurbished PlayStation 5 Pro units, setting the cost at $1,399. This marks a 50% increase over the price of new PS5 Pros, which remain scarce across major retailers.

Supply Shortages Drive Price Spike

While Sony’s official channels and retailers such as Best Buy and Walmart still list new PS5 Pros at around $1,500, GameStop’s decision to raise the price of refurbished models reflects the broader market strain. The company cited continued demand and limited availability of new consoles as the primary reason for the adjustment.

What This Means for Gamers

For players looking to upgrade, the price hike may push some to wait for new stock or consider alternative platforms. The move also highlights the growing trend of retailers leveraging refurbished units to meet demand when new inventory is scarce.
