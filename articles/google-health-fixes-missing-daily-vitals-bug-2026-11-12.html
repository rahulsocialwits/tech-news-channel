Google Health Fixes Missing Daily Vitals Bug

2026-11-12 · 3 min read
Google Health users report missing daily vitals; the company confirms a fix is underway.

Google Health has acknowledged a bug that caused daily vitals to disappear from the app’s dashboard. The issue surfaced last week, leaving users unable to view their heart rate, blood pressure, and other key metrics for the day. Google’s engineering team has identified the root cause and is actively working on a patch.

What’s Happening?

According to a statement from Google, the missing vitals problem is “officially on the company’s radar.” The company has not yet released a timeline for the fix, but it has assured users that the issue will be resolved in an upcoming update.

Impact on Users

While the bug has not affected data collection, it has prevented users from accessing their daily health summaries. Many users rely on the app for tracking trends and sharing information with healthcare providers.

Next Steps

Google Health’s team is prioritizing the issue and will roll out a fix through the Google Play Store. Users are encouraged to keep the app updated to receive the patch as soon as it becomes available.
