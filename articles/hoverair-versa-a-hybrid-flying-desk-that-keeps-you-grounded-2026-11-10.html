HoverAir Versa: A Hybrid Flying Desk That Keeps You Grounded

2026-11-10 · 3 min read
The HoverAir Versa blends a hoverboard and a laptop stand, offering a portable workspace that flies but still feels like a desk.

HoverAir’s latest offering, the Versa, is a hybrid device that marries the freedom of a hoverboard with the practicality of a laptop stand. Designed for commuters and remote workers who crave mobility without sacrificing ergonomics, the Versa promises a new way to work on the go.

What the Versa Looks Like

The device is a lightweight frame that sits on a hoverboard base. A fold‑out desk extends from the center, providing a flat surface for a laptop or tablet. The hoverboard’s motor and battery are integrated into the frame, allowing the entire unit to glide silently over smooth surfaces. The design is compact enough to fit in a backpack when folded, yet sturdy enough to support a 15‑inch laptop.

Performance and Practicality

According to the review, the hoverboard component can reach speeds of up to 12 mph, which is sufficient for short trips across a campus or office building. The battery life is rated at roughly 45 minutes of continuous hover, which is comparable to other consumer hoverboards on the market. When the device is stationary, the desk can be set up in less than a minute, making it easy to transition from a walk to a work session.

Pros and Cons

  • Convenience: The Versa eliminates the need for a separate laptop stand and a separate hoverboard, saving space and simplifying travel.
  • Ergonomics: The desk height is adjustable, allowing users to maintain a neutral posture while working.
  • Limited Battery: The 45‑minute hover time may require frequent recharging for longer commutes.
  • Weight: At 12 pounds, the device is heavier than a typical laptop stand but lighter than most hoverboards.

Who Should Consider the Versa?

Frequent travelers, students, and professionals who spend a lot of time on the move could find the Versa a useful addition to their gear. The device is especially appealing to those who want to combine exercise with productivity, as the hoverboard’s motion can provide a light cardio workout while the desk keeps work within reach.

Where to Buy

HoverAir is currently selling the Versa through its website and select online retailers. Prices start at $799, with a 30‑day return policy for those who want to test the device in real‑world conditions.
