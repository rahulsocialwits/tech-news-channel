Roku Shifts Focus: Ending Smart‑Home Line to Double Down on Streaming

2026-11-11 · 3 min read
Roku will stop selling video doorbells and other smart‑home devices, redirecting resources to its core streaming business.

Roku announced today it will discontinue its smart‑home product line, including video doorbells and home monitoring systems, and will concentrate on its streaming platform. The decision follows a period of declining sales for the company’s home‑automation devices, which have struggled to compete with larger rivals in the market.

What’s Changing?

The company will phase out all smart‑home hardware, citing a strategic shift toward strengthening its streaming services and expanding its content library. Existing Roku smart‑home customers will receive support for the remaining lifespan of their devices, but no new units will be produced.

Impact on Consumers

Owners of Roku’s video doorbells and monitoring systems can continue to use them as they have, but they will not receive firmware updates or new features. The move is expected to simplify Roku’s product portfolio and allow the company to allocate engineering resources to its core streaming business.

Industry Context

Roku’s decision comes amid a crowded smart‑home market, where companies like Amazon, Google, and Apple dominate. By narrowing its focus, Roku aims to reinforce its position as a leading streaming platform and to invest in new content partnerships and platform enhancements.
