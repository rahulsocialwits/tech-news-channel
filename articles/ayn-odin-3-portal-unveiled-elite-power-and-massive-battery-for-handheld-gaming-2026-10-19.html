AYN Odin 3 Portal Unveiled: Elite Power and Massive Battery for Handheld Gaming

2026-10-19 · 3 min read
AYN reveals the Odin 3 Portal, a handheld with top-tier specs and a gigantic battery, poised to become the most powerful Android device on the market.

AYN has announced the Odin 3 Portal, a handheld gaming device that promises to set a new benchmark for performance and endurance in the Android ecosystem. The company’s press release details a configuration that rivals the most powerful laptops, positioning the Odin 3 Portal as a serious contender for gamers and power users alike.

Powerful Hardware

The device is equipped with a Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 processor, paired with 16GB of LPDDR5 RAM. This combination delivers a significant boost over previous handhelds, enabling smooth gameplay for graphically intensive titles and efficient multitasking for productivity apps. A 6.7-inch OLED display with a 120Hz refresh rate provides vibrant visuals and fluid motion, while the 1440p resolution ensures sharp detail.

Massive Battery Life

One of the standout features of the Odin 3 Portal is its 20,000mAh battery. AYN claims that the device can sustain up to 12 hours of continuous gaming at 60% battery usage, a figure that outpaces most competitors in the handheld segment. The battery is supported by a 65W fast-charging system, which can replenish the device to 80% in just 30 minutes.

Design and Build

AYN has opted for a robust chassis made from aluminum alloy, giving the Odin 3 Portal a premium feel while maintaining durability. The device includes a detachable controller with a full-size thumbstick, trigger buttons, and a 3.5mm headphone jack, catering to both handheld and docked playstyles. A 3.5-inch secondary display on the back provides quick access to system stats and notifications without interrupting gameplay.

Software and Ecosystem

The Odin 3 Portal runs on Android 14, with AYN’s custom UI layer adding a suite of gaming optimizations. Features such as a built-in game mode, customizable key mapping, and a dedicated performance monitor are designed to enhance the user experience. AYN also announced a partnership with major game developers to bring exclusive titles to the platform.

Availability and Pricing

AYN has not yet confirmed a release date, but the company indicates that the Odin 3 Portal will be available in select markets later this year. Pricing is expected to start at $1,299, positioning it in the premium handheld segment. AYN has stated that the device will be available through its own online store and select retailers.
