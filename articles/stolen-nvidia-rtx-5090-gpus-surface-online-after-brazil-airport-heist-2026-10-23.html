Stolen Nvidia RTX 5090 GPUs Surface Online After Brazil Airport Heist

2026-10-23 · 3 min read
Three airport employees stole a high‑value Nvidia RTX 5090 shipment, and one card has appeared for sale online, police say.

Police in Brazil have confirmed that a shipment of Nvidia RTX 5090 graphics cards, worth roughly $470,000, was stolen from an airport terminal. Three employees were identified as the culprits, and the stolen GPUs have begun to appear on online marketplaces.

What Happened?

The RTX 5090, Nvidia’s latest high‑end gaming card, was en route to a distributor when the theft occurred. The incident was uncovered when the shipment’s serial numbers were cross‑checked against a database of stolen electronics.

Online Sale

One of the stolen cards has already been listed for sale on a popular e‑commerce platform. The listing includes the card’s serial number, allowing buyers to verify its authenticity. The price is significantly below the retail value, a common tactic used to attract buyers unaware of the card’s illicit origin.

Law Enforcement Response

Police have issued a warning to potential buyers and are working with the platform to remove the listing. They are also investigating how the employees accessed the shipment and whether additional cards remain missing.

Impact on the Market

While the loss of a single card is unlikely to affect the overall supply of RTX 5090 units, the incident highlights the vulnerability of high‑value electronics during transit. Manufacturers and distributors are urged to enhance security protocols to prevent similar thefts.
