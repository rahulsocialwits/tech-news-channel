Amazon Unveils Echo Dot 6th Gen with Built‑In Smart Home Hub

2026-10-28 · 3 min read
Amazon’s latest Echo Dot adds a smart home hub, boosting home‑automation control but at a higher price point.

Amazon has rolled out a new Echo Dot, the company’s sixth‑generation smart speaker, that now includes a built‑in smart home hub. The addition means users can control compatible devices directly from the Echo Dot without needing a separate hub, streamlining home‑automation setups.

What’s New in the Echo Dot 6th Gen?

The updated speaker features a redesigned, slimmer chassis and a higher‑quality speaker driver, delivering clearer audio. The built‑in hub supports Zigbee, Thread, and Matter protocols, allowing it to act as a bridge for a wide range of smart‑home products.

Price and Availability

While the new Echo Dot offers more functionality, Amazon has increased its price. The device is now priced at $49.99, up from the $39.99 of the previous generation. The company says the higher cost reflects the added hub capabilities and improved hardware.

Implications for Smart‑Home Users

With the hub built into the Echo Dot, users can set up and manage devices like lights, thermostats, and locks directly through Alexa. This integration simplifies the setup process and reduces the need for separate hubs, potentially lowering the overall cost of a smart‑home ecosystem.
