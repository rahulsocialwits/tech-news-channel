Apple Music’s New Selfie Sing Feature Lets Users Record Karaoke Videos

2026-11-04 · 3 min read
Apple Music is adding a Selfie Sing mode that lets users record themselves singing karaoke with a single tap, launching with iOS 27.3.

Apple Music is expanding its creative toolkit with a new Selfie Sing feature that lets users record themselves singing karaoke videos directly from the app. The addition, slated for the upcoming iOS 27.3 update, allows users to capture a front‑camera video while the app plays the chosen track, automatically syncing the audio and providing a simple, one‑tap recording experience.

How It Works

When a user selects a song, the Selfie Sing mode opens a camera view that records the performer’s face. The app overlays the track’s waveform and lyric display, giving real‑time feedback. Once the user finishes, the video is saved to the device and can be shared on social media or within the Apple Music community.

Why It Matters

Apple’s move taps into the growing popularity of user‑generated music content and the trend of integrating social features into streaming services. By simplifying the recording process, the company aims to encourage more fans to create and share their own renditions of popular songs.

Availability

Selfie Sing will be available to all Apple Music subscribers once iOS 27.3 rolls out later this month. The feature is expected to be supported on iPhone 15 and newer models, as well as iPadOS devices that meet the hardware requirements for high‑definition video capture.
