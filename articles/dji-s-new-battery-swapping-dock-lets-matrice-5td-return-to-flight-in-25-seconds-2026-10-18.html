DJI’s New Battery‑Swapping Dock Lets Matrice 5TD Return to Flight in 25 Seconds

2026-10-18 · 3 min read
DJI’s Dock 4 can swap a Matrice 5TD drone’s battery in just 25 seconds, but the system lacks FCC clearance for U.S. sales.

DJI has unveiled its first battery‑swapping dock, promising to cut downtime for its high‑end Matrice 5TD drone from minutes to seconds. The Dock 4 can replace a depleted battery in 25 seconds, allowing operators to keep the drone airborne with minimal interruption.

How the Dock Works

The system uses a robotic arm to lift the drone, remove the old battery, and install a fresh one. The entire process is automated, requiring no manual intervention from the pilot. Once the new battery is secured, the drone can resume flight almost immediately.

Performance and Limitations

In tests conducted by Tom’s Hardware, the dock achieved a 25‑second swap time for the Matrice 5TD. However, the device currently lacks FCC clearance, meaning it cannot be sold in the United States. DJI has not yet announced a timeline for regulatory approval.

Implications for Commercial Operations

For commercial drone operators, the ability to swap batteries quickly could significantly improve mission efficiency. Tasks such as surveying, mapping, and inspection could see reduced downtime, translating to higher productivity and lower operational costs.

Next Steps for DJI

DJI will need to secure FCC clearance before the dock can reach U.S. customers. The company has indicated plans to expand the dock’s compatibility to other drone models in the future, potentially broadening its market impact.
