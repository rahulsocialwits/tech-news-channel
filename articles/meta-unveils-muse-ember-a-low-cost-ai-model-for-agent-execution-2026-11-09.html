Meta Unveils Muse Ember, a Low‑Cost AI Model for Agent Execution

2026-11-09 · 3 min read
Meta’s new Muse Ember model offers a lightweight, inexpensive option for AI agents, priced at 10 cents per million input tokens.

Meta has introduced Muse Ember, a new lightweight AI model designed to execute agent tasks more efficiently. The company announced the release on November 9, 2026, positioning Ember as a cost‑effective alternative to larger models.

Key Features and Pricing

Ember is priced at just 10 cents per million input tokens, making it significantly cheaper than competitors such as Haiku and Luna. The model’s streamlined architecture allows it to perform agent execution tasks while keeping resource usage low.

Implications for Developers

Developers looking to deploy AI agents in production environments can now consider Ember as a budget‑friendly option. Its lower cost could enable broader experimentation and faster iteration cycles, especially for startups and smaller enterprises that need to manage cloud spend.

Industry Context

The announcement comes amid a growing trend of companies offering specialized, cost‑efficient AI models. Meta’s move highlights the importance of balancing performance with affordability in the rapidly evolving AI landscape.
