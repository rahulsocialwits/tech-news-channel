Anycubic Kobra X 3D Printer Slashes Prices to $299.99 for Prime Members

2026-10-18 · 3 min read
The award‑winning Anycubic Kobra X multicolor 3D printer is now available at an all‑time low of $299.99 for Amazon Prime members, offering four‑color printing and a 220 mm build volume.

Anycubic has announced a dramatic price cut for its Kobra X multicolor 3D printer, slashing the cost to just $299.99 for Amazon Prime members. The Kobra X, which has earned Editor’s Choice status for its ability to print in four colors simultaneously, now offers an entry‑level option that rivals higher‑end models in both capability and affordability.

Key Features at a New Price Point

The printer retains its 220 mm × 220 mm × 250 mm build volume, a 0.4 mm nozzle, and a dual‑extruder system that supports four‑color printing. Users can also benefit from the Kobra X’s built‑in camera, which allows for real‑time monitoring of prints via a smartphone app.

What the Price Cut Means for Hobbyists

At $299.99, the Kobra X becomes one of the most budget‑friendly options for hobbyists and educators looking to explore multicolor 3D printing without a hefty investment. The discount is available exclusively to Amazon Prime members, making it a limited‑time offer that could spur a surge in early adopters.

Industry Context

The move comes amid a broader trend of manufacturers lowering prices to capture market share in a competitive consumer 3D printing landscape. By offering a feature‑rich printer at a price point traditionally reserved for basic models, Anycubic positions itself as a strong contender for both new users and seasoned makers.
