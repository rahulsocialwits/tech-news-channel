Foldable iPhone Duo Faces Cracked Screens and Hinge Failures, Repairability Ratings Raise Concerns

2026-10-26 · 3 min read
Apple’s first foldable iPhone Duo is experiencing widespread cracked-screen and hinge issues, prompting iFixit to release a low repairability score and users to report durability problems.

Apple’s ambitious foray into foldable smartphones has hit a rough patch as users report a surge of cracked screens and hinge malfunctions on the iPhone Duo. The company’s first foldable device, launched earlier this year, was marketed as a durable, high‑performance companion to the iPhone 15 series. However, a wave of complaints has emerged, prompting a closer look at the phone’s design and repairability.

Repairability Ratings Highlight Design Flaws

iFixit, the well‑known repair‑ability rating organization, recently released its assessment of the iPhone Duo. The company assigned the device a low score, citing several factors that make repairs difficult and costly. Among the key issues are the phone’s complex hinge mechanism and the use of proprietary components that are hard to source. iFixit’s report suggests that the Duo’s design prioritizes a sleek, foldable form factor over ease of repair, a trade‑off that has not resonated well with early adopters.

User Reports of Cracked Screens and Hinge Problems

Independent reports from users across the globe have documented a significant number of cracked screens and hinge failures. Many owners describe the screens cracking after only a few months of use, often after the phone has been folded and unfolded repeatedly. In addition, several users have reported that the hinge mechanism becomes loose or sticks, leading to a wobbling or uneven fold. These issues have prompted a number of owners to seek professional repairs or to return the device to Apple for service.

Possible Causes and Apple’s Response

While Apple has not yet released an official statement detailing the root cause of the problems, industry analysts point to the phone’s hinge design and the materials used in the display assembly as likely contributors. The hinge’s complex geometry, combined with the need to maintain a thin profile, may create stress points that are prone to failure under repeated use. Additionally, the display’s glass layers may be more susceptible to cracking when subjected to the flexing required for a foldable device.

Apple has acknowledged the reports and has begun to offer repair services for affected units. The company’s repair program includes a free repair for certain hinge and screen issues, though it has not yet announced a full recall. Users are advised to keep their devices updated with the latest iOS releases, as Apple has indicated that software updates may help mitigate some of the mechanical stresses on the hinge.

Implications for Foldable Smartphone Market

The iPhone Duo’s challenges underscore the broader difficulties faced by manufacturers attempting to combine foldable technology with everyday durability. While the market for foldable devices has grown, consumer expectations for reliability remain high. The Duo’s issues may prompt Apple to revisit its design approach or to offer more robust warranty coverage for future models.

For now, iPhone Duo owners must weigh the benefits of a foldable form factor against the potential for hardware failures. As the company works to address the problems, the foldable smartphone segment will likely see further innovation and refinement in the coming months.
