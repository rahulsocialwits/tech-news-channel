Blackwall Raises $100 Million to Strengthen Bot and AI Traffic Filtering

2026-10-20 · 3 min read
Blackwall secures a $100 million round, valuing the company at $2.2 billion as it expands its security and filtering tools for websites facing rising bot and AI traffic.

Blackwall, a San Francisco‑based security firm, announced on Tuesday that it has closed a $100 million funding round that values the company at $2.2 billion. The new capital will help the company scale its suite of tools that detect and filter automated traffic, including bots and AI‑driven agents, from websites.

Why the timing matters

In recent months, the volume of automated traffic on the web has surged, driven by both traditional bots and the rapid deployment of large language models. Websites that rely on accurate analytics, ad revenue, or user experience are increasingly vulnerable to traffic that skews metrics, inflates ad impressions, or attempts to scrape content.

Blackwall’s approach

The company’s platform uses machine‑learning models to distinguish between human visitors and automated traffic in real time. It then applies configurable rules to block, challenge, or allow traffic based on the site’s needs. Blackwall’s customers include e‑commerce retailers, media outlets, and SaaS providers that require granular control over who can access their content.

Funding and future plans

Lead investors in the round include Sequoia Capital and Andreessen Horowitz, with participation from existing backers such as Accel and Lightspeed. The firm plans to use the proceeds to expand its engineering team, enhance its AI models, and broaden its market reach in the United States and Europe.

Industry context

Security firms that specialize in bot mitigation have seen a surge in demand as the web becomes more crowded with automated traffic. Blackwall’s valuation quadruples from its last round, underscoring the growing importance of reliable traffic filtering solutions for online businesses.
