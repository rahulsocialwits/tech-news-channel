Apple Unveils M6‑Powered iMac and Entry‑Level MacBook Pro

2026-10-28 · 3 min read
Apple refreshes its desktop and laptop lineup with the new M6 chip, adding Wi‑Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6 to the iMac and entry‑level MacBook Pro.

Apple has rolled out a new generation of its flagship desktop and laptop, both powered by the company’s latest M6 silicon. The refresh arrives with a host of upgrades that aim to keep Apple’s hardware lineup competitive in a market that is increasingly focused on performance and connectivity.

M6 Chip and Performance Boost

The M6 brings a significant jump in processing power over its predecessor, the M5. Apple highlights a 30% increase in CPU performance and a 40% boost in GPU throughput, according to the company’s technical brief. The chip also introduces a new neural engine that is designed to accelerate machine‑learning tasks, a feature that is expected to enhance the experience of creative applications such as Final Cut Pro and Logic Pro.

New Connectivity Standards

Alongside the silicon upgrade, Apple has integrated Wi‑Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6 into both the iMac and the entry‑level MacBook Pro. These standards promise faster wireless speeds, lower latency, and improved reliability, especially in environments with many connected devices. The move is part of Apple’s broader strategy to future‑proof its devices for the next generation of home and office networking.

Design and Display Enhancements

The refreshed iMac retains its 24‑inch Retina display but now supports a 4K resolution with a 60‑Hz refresh rate, a step up from the previous 60‑Hz 4K panel. The MacBook Pro, meanwhile, features a new 13‑inch Liquid Retina XDR display that offers higher brightness and better color accuracy, making it a compelling option for professionals who need a portable yet powerful machine.

Software Integration

Apple has also added new AI features to its Creator Studio suite, which is bundled with the new hardware. These features include automated video editing suggestions and real‑time audio enhancement, leveraging the M6’s neural engine to deliver smoother workflows for creators.

Market Impact

Analysts predict that the M6 refresh will help Apple maintain its market share in the high‑end desktop and laptop segments, where competition from Windows‑based PCs and other manufacturers is intensifying. The new connectivity options are expected to appeal to users who rely on high‑speed wireless networks for streaming, gaming, and remote work.
