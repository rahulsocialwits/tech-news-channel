MIT Bengaluru Launches Quantum‑Hub to Propel India’s Quantum Tech Ecosystem

2026-10-17 · 3 min read
The Manipal Institute of Technology opens a new quantum‑engineering hub at its MAHE campus, aiming to boost research, innovation and workforce development in India.

On October 16, 2026, the Manipal Institute of Technology (MIT) in Bengaluru inaugurated a new Quantum‑Hub at its MAHE campus. The facility is designed to strengthen India’s quantum engineering, research, innovation and workforce development efforts.

Key Features of the Quantum‑Hub

  • State‑of‑the‑art laboratories for quantum computing, communication and sensing.
  • Collaborative spaces for students, faculty and industry partners.
  • Programs aimed at training the next generation of quantum scientists and engineers.

Strategic Impact

The hub is part of a broader national push to position India as a leader in quantum technology. By providing advanced research infrastructure and fostering industry collaboration, MIT Bengaluru hopes to accelerate the development of quantum‑based solutions across sectors such as healthcare, finance and cybersecurity.

Future Outlook

With the Quantum‑Hub, MIT Bengaluru is set to become a central node in India’s growing quantum ecosystem, offering resources that can help translate theoretical breakthroughs into practical applications.
