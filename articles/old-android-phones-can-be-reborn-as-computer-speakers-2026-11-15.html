Old Android Phones Can Be Reborn as Computer Speakers

2026-11-15 · 3 min read
Discover how a simple app turns an unused Android phone into a wireless speaker for your PC, extending the life of old devices.

Tech enthusiasts have found a new way to breathe life into their forgotten Android phones. By installing a free app that turns the device into a Bluetooth speaker, users can stream audio from their computers without buying new hardware.

How It Works

The app creates a virtual audio output on the phone, which the computer can detect as a Bluetooth speaker. Once paired, any sound played on the PC—music, podcasts, or even system alerts—flows through the phone’s speaker system.

Benefits for Users

  • Cost‑effective: No need to purchase a dedicated speaker.
  • Eco‑friendly: Repurposes old devices, reducing e‑waste.
  • Convenient: Works with any computer that supports Bluetooth audio.

Limitations

Performance depends on the phone’s speaker quality and battery life. The app requires a stable Bluetooth connection, and some older models may struggle with latency.

Getting Started

Download the app from the Google Play Store, follow the pairing instructions, and enjoy your PC’s audio through a familiar device.
