Apple Unveils HomeView: The Wall‑Mounted Smart‑Home Dashboard That Could Replace Your Phone

2026-11-24 · 3 min read
Apple’s new HomeView turns a wall into a smart‑home control center, pairing HomeKit with HomeAssistant for a seamless dashboard experience.

Apple has quietly rolled out a new wall‑mounted device that could change the way we interact with our smart homes. The HomeView, announced by Apple in a recent developer preview, is a sleek, flat panel that sits on any wall and brings the full power of HomeKit and HomeAssistant to a single, touch‑friendly interface.

What is HomeView?

HomeView is a thin, 10‑inch display that plugs into a standard power outlet and connects to your home Wi‑Fi network. Once set up, it acts as a dedicated smart‑home hub, displaying real‑time status for lights, thermostats, cameras, and other HomeKit‑compatible devices. The device also runs HomeAssistant, an open‑source platform that expands the range of supported devices beyond Apple’s native ecosystem.

Design and Usability

Apple has kept the design minimalistic, with a matte finish and a slim bezel that blends into any décor. The screen is responsive to touch and supports multi‑finger gestures, allowing users to swipe between rooms or tap to toggle devices. The device also includes a small speaker and a built‑in microphone, enabling voice control via Siri or HomeAssistant’s own voice assistants.

Integration with HomeKit and HomeAssistant

One of the key selling points of HomeView is its dual‑platform support. HomeKit users can control their existing accessories directly from the dashboard, while HomeAssistant integration opens the door to a wider array of third‑party devices, including those that use Zigbee, Z‑Wave, or custom protocols. This hybrid approach means that homeowners no longer need to juggle multiple apps or devices to manage their smart home.

Potential Impact on the Smart‑Home Market

By offering a wall‑mounted solution, Apple is targeting a segment of the market that has traditionally relied on smartphones or tablets for control. The HomeView could become a staple in modern kitchens, living rooms, or bedrooms, providing a more intuitive and accessible way to monitor and adjust home settings. The integration with HomeAssistant also positions Apple as a more inclusive player in the smart‑home space, potentially attracting users who have been hesitant to adopt HomeKit due to device compatibility issues.

Availability and Pricing

Apple has not yet announced a retail price or release date for HomeView. However, the company has indicated that the device will be available to developers and early adopters through the Apple Developer Program. Rumors suggest that a consumer version could launch in early 2027, with a price point that would likely compete with high‑end smart‑display devices from other manufacturers.

Conclusion

Apple’s HomeView represents a bold step into the smart‑home dashboard market, combining the company’s polished hardware design with the flexibility of HomeAssistant. If the device lives up to its promise, it could become a new standard for home automation control, offering a seamless, wall‑mounted experience that rivals smartphones and tablets.
