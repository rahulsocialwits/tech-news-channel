Omen AI Acquires Aquatic Labs to Tackle Data‑Center Water Challenges

2026-11-02 · 3 min read
Omen AI’s $16 million purchase of Aquatic Labs brings liquid‑management expertise to the data‑center sustainability arena.

In a move that signals a growing focus on environmental sustainability in the tech sector, Omen AI has announced a $16 million acquisition of Aquatic Labs, a startup that specializes in water‑management solutions for data centers. The deal, reported by TechCrunch on November 2, 2026, brings together two companies that have been working independently to address the same critical problem: how to reduce the water footprint of large‑scale computing facilities.

Why Water Matters for Data Centers

Data centers consume vast amounts of water for cooling and other operational needs. As the industry expands to meet the demands of cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and edge services, the pressure on local water resources has intensified. Public sentiment, highlighted by a new website that aggregates over 10,000 comments from more than 410 local governments, shows that more than 90 percent of the feedback is negative toward data‑center projects, with water usage cited as the top concern. This sentiment underscores the urgency for companies to adopt more efficient cooling technologies.

Aquatic Labs’ Liquid‑Management Technology

Founded in 2024, Aquatic Labs has developed a suite of tools that monitor, optimize, and recycle water within data‑center environments. Their platform uses real‑time sensors and predictive analytics to adjust cooling flows, reducing overall consumption while maintaining thermal performance. The startup’s technology has already been piloted in several mid‑size facilities, demonstrating measurable reductions in water use without compromising reliability.

Omen AI’s Strategic Expansion

Omen AI, known for its AI‑driven infrastructure optimization solutions, has been positioning itself as a leader in sustainable data‑center operations. By acquiring Aquatic Labs, Omen AI gains immediate access to liquid‑management expertise and a proven product line that complements its existing AI‑based resource‑allocation tools. The integration is expected to accelerate the deployment of end‑to‑end solutions that address both energy and water efficiency.

Implications for the Industry

The acquisition reflects a broader trend in which tech companies are investing in sustainability technologies to meet regulatory pressures and public expectations. With the data‑center sector facing scrutiny over its environmental impact, the combined capabilities of Omen AI and Aquatic Labs could set a new standard for responsible infrastructure management.

Looking Ahead

While the deal is still in its early stages, industry analysts anticipate that the partnership will spur further innovation in water‑efficient cooling systems. Omen AI’s leadership team has indicated plans to integrate Aquatic Labs’ sensors with its AI platform, enabling predictive maintenance and automated adjustments that could reduce water usage by up to 30 percent in large facilities. If successful, the model could be replicated across the sector, driving a shift toward greener data‑center operations.
