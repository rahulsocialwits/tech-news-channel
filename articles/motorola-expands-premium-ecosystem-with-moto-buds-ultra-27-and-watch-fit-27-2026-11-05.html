Motorola Expands Premium Ecosystem with Moto Buds Ultra 27 and Watch Fit 27

2026-11-05 · 3 min read
Motorola launches new premium earbuds and smartwatch, integrating Qira AI assistant for North American consumers.

Motorola is set to broaden its premium accessory lineup with the upcoming North American release of the Moto Buds Ultra 27 and the Watch Fit 27. The two devices are positioned as complementary pieces of a larger ecosystem that includes the company’s AI‑powered Qira assistant.

What’s New in the Moto Buds Ultra 27?

The earbuds build on the success of the earlier Moto Buds Ultra, adding a new generation of active noise cancellation and a slimmer design. They feature a 30‑hour battery life with the charging case and support for high‑resolution audio codecs. The earbuds also come with a dedicated app that allows users to customize equalizer settings and manage firmware updates.

Watch Fit 27: A Smartwatch for the Everyday User

The Watch Fit 27 is marketed as a “premium” smartwatch that offers health and fitness tracking, sleep monitoring, and on‑screen notifications. It runs on Motorola’s custom Wear OS skin, which includes support for third‑party apps and a new gesture‑based interface. The watch also supports wireless charging and a 1.5‑inch AMOLED display.

Integration with Qira AI Assistant

Both the earbuds and the smartwatch are designed to work seamlessly with Qira, Motorola’s AI assistant. Qira can be activated via voice commands on the earbuds or by tapping the watch’s screen. The assistant can handle routine tasks such as setting reminders, controlling smart home devices, and providing real‑time traffic updates.

Release Timeline and Availability

Motorola has announced that the Moto Buds Ultra 27 and Watch Fit 27 will be available in the United States in the coming months. The company plans to roll out the devices through its own retail channels, as well as through major electronics retailers and online marketplaces. Pricing details have not yet been disclosed, but the company expects the earbuds to be priced in the mid‑$200 range and the smartwatch in the mid‑$250 range.

Why It Matters

Motorola’s push into premium audio and wearables reflects a broader industry trend of companies building integrated ecosystems around AI assistants. By pairing earbuds and a smartwatch with Qira, Motorola aims to create a seamless user experience that encourages brand loyalty and increases the average revenue per user. The move also positions the company to compete with established players such as Apple, Samsung, and Google in the crowded wearables market.
